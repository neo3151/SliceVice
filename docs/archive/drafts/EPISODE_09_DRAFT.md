# TAPE 09: THE SHALLOWS RECLAMATION

**SETTING:** SECTOR 305-R -> STILTSVILLE (THE SHALLOWS)
**ATMOSPHERE:** Radioactive Fog. Neon-purple bioluminescence. Dying Industrial Drone.

---

### PANEL 01
**VISUAL:** A wide shot of the Marine Stadium vanishing into a thick, swirling green-grey fog. The concrete tiers look like the ribs of a giant, sunken beast.
**CAPTION:** SECTOR 305-R. THE RICKENBACKER IS BREACHED, BUT THE MAINLAND IS STILL A MILE OF BLACKWATER AND RUST AWAY.

### PANEL 02
**VISUAL:** Axel and Reya are on a small, matte-black Scavenger skiff. Axel is holding a long carbon-fiber pole, pushing off a submerged concrete pillar. The LeMans is gone—abandoned in the stadium's shadow.
**REYA:** "Transmission was shredded anyway, Axel. In the Shallows, wheels are just anchors."

### PANEL 03
**VISUAL:** Close-up on the water’s surface. Thick, oily barnacles cling to a rusted pylon. They are pulsing with a faint, sickly purple light.
**AXEL:** (CAPTION) The barnacles glow when they detect corporate hardware. Right now, the bay is screaming.

### PANEL 04
**VISUAL:** Reya is crouched at the bow, holding a flickering V-Sentry 900 Signal Jammer. It’s sparking.
**REYA:** "The jammers are on a 3-second reboot cycle. If we time the push, we can stay under the Vanguard’s radar domes."

### PANEL 05
**VISUAL:** Above them, a Vanguard Hover-Drone (V-Drone 4) slices through the fog with a harsh white searchlight. It just barely misses the skiff.
**AXEL:** (CAPTION) Stay ghost. Don’t breathe. The fog smells like ozone and failed audits.

### PANEL 06
**VISUAL:** They pass a series of wooden houses on stilts—Stiltsville. The structures look fragile, held together by barnacle-crusted cables and neon-blue wiring.
**REYA:** "Check the pylon at House 404. There's a Scavenger cache hidden in the loose panel."

### PANEL 07
**VISUAL:** A new character emerges from the fog, standing on a floating platform. They wear a heavy, pressurized diving suit with a cracked visor. They are holding a salvaged "Mother-Board" plate.
**DIVER:** "You're late for the delivery, Cutter. The tide is rising, and Vance's sonar is already pinging the floorbeds."

### PANEL 08
**VISUAL:** Axel climbs onto the platform, reaching for the Mossberg in his back-strap. 
**AXEL:** "Vance is blind in the dark. We need a way across the Shallows before the sun hits the Rickenbacker."
