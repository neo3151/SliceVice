# TAPE 10: THE AUDIT EXCHANGE

**SETTING:** SECTOR 305-R -> THE FLOATING PLATFORM
**ATMOSPHERE:** Low-tide. Exposed concrete pylons. Distant sonar pings.

---

### PANEL 01
**VISUAL:** Axel and Reya are on the platform. Axel is handing the salvaged 'Mother-Board' circuit plate to the Scavenger Diver (Kallan). The Diver’s visor reflects the neon-yellow moon.
**DIVER:** "Vance’s signature is all over the encryption on this board. You didn’t just salvage this... you stole a piece of his brain."

### PANEL 02
**VISUAL:** Close-up on Axel. He’s looking out at the foggy horizon. A distant, rhythmic red pulse is visible in the clouds.
**AXEL:** "He’s trying to map the floorbeds. If he finds the Stiltsville Data-Cradle, we’re all foreclosed."

### PANEL 03
**VISUAL:** The Diver turns the circuit board over, revealing a hidden port. He plugs in a bulky handheld deck. A holographic map of the stadium’s sub-levels flickers into existence.
**DIVER:** "There’s a drainage pipe—Sector 404. It leads directly under the Megaplex. It’s dry for exactly three minutes during the jammer reboot."

### PANEL 04
**VISUAL:** Reya is checking the skiff’s motor. She looks worried. The motor is held together by rusted zip-ties and luck.
**REYA:** "We won't make it in this bucket. The Vanguard Interceptors move at 40 knots on the surface. We're doing 12, tops."

### PANEL 05
**VISUAL:** The Diver kicks over a heavy metal crate. Inside sits a pristine, cobalt-blue turbine motor—OUTCALL BRAND.
**DIVER:** "Take the Outcall Turbine. It's a prototype from the 305-R blockade. It bypasses the sonar, but it drinks high-octane isopropyl like a dying man."

### PANEL 06
**VISUAL:** Axel is bolting the new turbine to the skiff. Sparks fly as he uses a heavy-duty mag-wrench.
**AXEL:** (CAPTION) The exchange was made. One piece of Vance’s legacy for three minutes of invisibility.

### PANEL 07
**VISUAL:** A sudden, earth-shaking rumble. In the distance, a massive Vanguard Hover-Carrier (The V-Sovereign) emerges from the radioactive cloud, its searchlights cutting the bay in half.
**REYA:** "They’re not waiting for the audit. They’re level-zeroing the sector."

### PANEL 08
**VISUAL:** Axel rips the starter cord. The Outcall Turbine erupts with a high-pitched, electric scream. The skiff's bow lifts out of the water.
**AXEL:** "Vance wants his brain back? Tell him to come get it."
