# TAPE 11: THE BLACKWATER PURSUIT

**SETTING:** THE FLORIDA SHALLOWS → SECTOR 404 APPROACH
**ATMOSPHERE:** Full throttle. Electric engine scream. Vanguard spotlights sweeping the fog.

---

### PANEL 01
**VISUAL:** Wide, low-angle shot. The skiff explodes across the blackwater surface, bow lifted completely out of the water. The OUTCALL turbine leaves a glowing blue wake-trail in the oily dark. Behind them, three V-Interceptor sleds close fast, their red running lights cutting through the fog like laser surgery.
**AXEL:** (CAPTION) *The Shallows at 40 knots. The water turns into concrete if you fall off.*

### PANEL 02
**VISUAL:** Inside the skiff. Reya is flat against the hull, feeding isopropyl fuel from a salvaged army jerrycan directly into the turbine's intake. Her jaw is set, knuckles white.
**REYA:** "We've got maybe three minutes of fuel at this burn rate. After that, we're a floating target."
**AXEL:** "Three minutes is all Kallan gave us through the pipe window anyway."

### PANEL 03
**VISUAL:** A V-Interceptor sled flanks them on the right. A Vanguard enforcer in heavy black riot gear stands upright on the deck, bracing a mag-pulse cannon on their shoulder. The cannon's coils are charging cyan.
**VANGUARD COMM:** "SKIFF 404-ECHO. REDUCE SPEED. THIS IS A MANDATORY ASSET RECOVERY OPERATION."

### PANEL 04
**VISUAL:** Axel yanks the tiller hard left. The skiff skids sideways, spraying a massive rooster tail of black water directly into the path of the interceptors. The mag-pulse fires wide, detonating a rusted pylon in a shower of orange sparks and concrete shrapnel.
**AXEL:** "Asset recovery. They're not coming to arrest us. They're coming to repossess the board."

### PANEL 05
**VISUAL:** Above them, the V-Sovereign descends lower through the fog. Its underside glows with red thermal targeting arrays. The massive ship begins flooding the entire sector with a bone-rattling sonar ping.
**REYA:** "The sonar is going to cook the barnacles on the pylons. This whole sector is about to light purple."

### PANEL 06
**VISUAL:** The barnacles on every submerged pylon detonate with blinding neon-purple bioluminescent flashes. The entire Shallows suddenly looks like a strobing disco floor. It kills the Vanguard's thermal targeting completely — and blinds their own visibility too.
**AXEL:** (CAPTION) *Vance's sonar just nuked his own surveillance network. Every barnacle in the bay is screaming at once.*

### PANEL 07
**VISUAL:** Axel spots a gap between two massive, half-submerged concrete storm drain arches. A narrow, pitch-black channel. The entry to Sector 404.
**REYA:** "That's the pipe. Kallan said it's 40 meters of darkness and then we're under the Megaplex."
**AXEL:** "Hold the fuel line. Don't let go."

### PANEL 08
**VISUAL:** The skiff punches into the drain tunnel at full speed. The turbine's blue glow is the only light. The walls are inches from each side. Behind them, the V-Sovereign's searchlights sweep the empty, strobing bay — too late.
**AXEL:** (CAPTION) *Three minutes. The clock starts now.*
