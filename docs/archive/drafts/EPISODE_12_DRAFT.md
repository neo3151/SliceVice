# TAPE 12: THE DRAIN RUN

**SETTING:** SECTOR 404 DRAINAGE PIPE → NEON SLICE MEGAPLEX SUB-BASEMENT
**ATMOSPHERE:** Claustrophobic. Dripping concrete. The hum of corporate infrastructure above.

---

### PANEL 01
**VISUAL:** The skiff is wedged sideways inside the storm drain. Axel and Reya are on foot now, wading knee-deep in black water through a narrow concrete tunnel lit only by a thin strip of bioluminescent moss on the ceiling. Axel has the Motherboard tucked under his arm in a sealed waterproof bag.
**AXEL:** (CAPTION) *Two minutes, thirty seconds. The jammer reboot is on a hard clock. When it comes back online, every sensor in the Megaplex sub-floor lights up.*

### PANEL 02
**VISUAL:** Close-up of Reya's hand on the tunnel wall. She traces a painted glyph — a crude neon-orange arrow pointing deeper in. Scavenger markings.
**REYA:** "Kallan's crew has been running contraband through here for three years. These marks lead to the maintenance shaft."
**AXEL:** "How do you know Kallan?"
**REYA:** "Everyone who works off-grid knows Kallan."

### PANEL 03
**VISUAL:** They emerge from the water into a dry maintenance shaft. Steel ladders run up the walls. Above them, through a heavy iron grate, the chrome and glass floor of the Neon Slice Megaplex lobby is visible — shoes of corporate workers crossing, oblivious.
**AXEL:** (CAPTION) *One minute, forty seconds. The whole machine runs twenty floors above our heads, and nobody down here even knows we exist.*

### PANEL 04
**VISUAL:** Reya spots a wall panel — a secondary terminal port. She pulls out a handheld scrubber tool and jacks the Motherboard directly into it. Data begins flooding across a small green-phosphor screen.
**REYA:** "I can copy Vance's neural map from here. The board is the key. We don't need to go upstairs."
**AXEL:** "We go upstairs. I need him to know it was me."

### PANEL 05
**VISUAL:** Axel is already climbing the ladder rungs toward the grate. The grate has a corporate seal stamped into it: NEON SLICE INC. — AUTHORIZED PERSONNEL ONLY.
**REYA:** (from below) "That's not in the plan, Axel."
**AXEL:** "The plan died with the LeMans."

### PANEL 06
**VISUAL:** Axel cracks the grate. A thin strip of blinding white corporate light floods down onto his face. He looks up into the clean, sterile world above.
**AXEL:** (CAPTION) *One minute. Clean floors. Climate control. The smell of money. I forgot places like this existed.*

### PANEL 07
**VISUAL:** Reya yanks his ankle from below. She holds up the green-phosphor scrubber screen. It shows a complete neural schematics dump — Vance's entire strategic memory, copied clean.
**REYA:** "Axel. I have everything. His routes. His accounts. His surveillance network. His asset registry. All of it."

### PANEL 08
**VISUAL:** Beat panel. Axel frozen on the ladder. The grate open above him. The white corporate light. Below, the data. He looks between them — up at Vance's world, down at Reya's screen.
**AXEL:** (CAPTION) *Thirty seconds. Two ways to end this. One loud. One quiet.*
**AXEL:** "...Which one actually hurts him more?"
