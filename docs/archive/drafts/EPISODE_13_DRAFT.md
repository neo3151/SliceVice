# TAPE 13: THE AUDIT

**SETTING:** NEON SLICE INC. MEGAPLEX — FLOOR 17: DIRECTOR VANCE'S OFFICE
**ATMOSPHERE:** Sterile corporate silence. Fluorescent white. The violence of total control.

---

### PANEL 01
**VISUAL:** Axel goes up anyway. He emerges through the grate into a maintenance corridor — white walls, buzzed fluorescent tubes, a single security camera in the corner with a blinking red light. He walks straight toward it. He doesn't cover his face.
**AXEL:** (CAPTION) *I want him to see me coming.*

### PANEL 02
**VISUAL:** Director Vance's corner office. Floor-to-ceiling glass. He is standing at the window, looking out over the Miami grid at night — a sea of neon and concrete sprawl. He is impeccably dressed. He doesn't turn around when Axel enters.
**VANCE:** "I watched you burn the LeMans."
**AXEL:** "I didn't burn it."
**VANCE:** "Something did."

### PANEL 03
**VISUAL:** Wide shot of the office. Axel stands in the center of the room, holding the Motherboard. Vance still faces the window, hands clasped behind his back. Between them, a massive glass desk with nothing on it — completely sterile.
**AXEL:** "You sent a Hover-Carrier after two people in a skiff. That's not a recovery operation. That's a burial."
**VANCE:** "Everything I do is a recovery operation, Mr. Overdrive."

### PANEL 04
**VISUAL:** Vance finally turns around. He is calm, measured, surgical. He looks at the Motherboard in Axel's hand with a detached clinical interest — not fear. Not anger. Just accounting.
**VANCE:** "That board contains seventeen months of strategic projection. Where exactly were you planning to deliver it?"
**AXEL:** "Everywhere."

### PANEL 05
**VISUAL:** Axel pulls out a compact broadcast transmitter — a jury-rigged piece of hardware from Kallan's cache — and places it on the desk. It's already blinking. Already transmitting. Reya's copy of the neural map is going out live across every open-channel frequency in 305-R.
**AXEL:** "Reya's been broadcasting everything on your board for the last four minutes. Cartel channels. Scavenger bands. Every faction grid in Miami."
**VANCE:** (still calm) "…You didn't come to hurt me."
**AXEL:** "No."

### PANEL 06
**VISUAL:** Close-up on Vance's face. For the first time — a crack. A micro-expression. The faintest flicker of something behind his eyes that isn't composure. His entire surveillance network, his entire asset map, his routes, his enforcers — all of it, public domain.
**AXEL:** "I came to make you irrelevant."

### PANEL 07
**VISUAL:** Through the floor-to-ceiling glass behind Vance, the Miami grid begins responding. Tiny flickers of neon light in distant sectors start going dark — his HOA fortresses losing signal. His drone network dropping offline one by one. His empire evaporating block by block in real time.
**VANCE:** "You understand this doesn't end with me. There are holding companies. There are contracts. There are—"
**AXEL:** "I know. That's not my problem."

### PANEL 08
**VISUAL:** Axel turns and walks toward the door. He leaves the Motherboard on Vance's desk. He doesn't look back. Vance stands alone in his glass office, the city grid slowly going dark behind him, the transmitter blinking on his pristine desk.
**AXEL:** (CAPTION) *The delivery was made. 29 minutes, 47 seconds.*
**AXEL:** (CAPTION) *Under the wire.*
