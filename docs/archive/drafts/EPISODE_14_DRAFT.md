# TAPE 14: THE UNINSTALL
## [VOLUME 02 FINALE]

**SETTING:** NEON SLICE MEGAPLEX LOBBY → THE MIAMI GRID → THE I-95 SLIPSTREAM
**ATMOSPHERE:** The world after. Neon fading. The road opening up. Exhaust and static.

---

### PANEL 01
**VISUAL:** Axel walks through the Neon Slice lobby alone. The corporate workers stare. Security guards reach for radios — then stop. The broadcast is already out. Their authority doesn't mean the same thing it did ten minutes ago. He pushes through the revolving glass door into the Miami night.
**AXEL:** (CAPTION) *Nobody stops a courier. It's the one rule that holds.*

### PANEL 02
**VISUAL:** Outside. Reya is waiting at the curb. Behind her, parked badly on the sidewalk, is a battered late-70s muscle car — not the LeMans. Something different. Older. Angrier. A 1979 Pontiac Firebird with a broken hood and a cracked windshield. A hand-written note is tucked under the wiper: "FROM KALLAN. YOU OWE ME."
**REYA:** "It's not the LeMans."
**AXEL:** (beat) "No. It's not."

### PANEL 03
**VISUAL:** Wide night shot of downtown Miami. In the distance, across the skyline, the lights of the Vanguard network are flickering out sector by sector. The V-Sovereign is descending — not to hunt, but because its targeting grid has gone dark. It's a ship with no orders. A machine with no master.
**REYA:** "What happens to Vance now?"
**AXEL:** "He becomes a civilian."

### PANEL 04
**VISUAL:** Axel gets into the Firebird. He grips the steering wheel. It doesn't fit his hands the way the LeMans did. He looks at the dashboard — analog gauges, no magic, no blood-pact. Just a car. He exhales slowly.
**AXEL:** (CAPTION) *The LeMans was a contract. Every mile was a negotiation between the machine and the road.*
**AXEL:** (CAPTION) *This one is blank. No obligations. No pact. No Neon Slice logo.*

### PANEL 05
**VISUAL:** Reya in the passenger seat. She is holding the green-phosphor scrubber — Vance's complete neural map. She looks at it, then at Axel.
**REYA:** "I kept a copy. Before I transmitted it."
**AXEL:** "I know."
**REYA:** "There are fourteen other Directors on this map. Fourteen other grids."
**AXEL:** (long pause) "…I know."

### PANEL 06
**VISUAL:** The Firebird pulls onto the I-95 on-ramp. The massive highway opens up ahead of them — twelve lanes of asphalt, the skyline receding in the rear-view mirror. The yellow center line stretches into the dark horizon.
**AXEL:** (CAPTION) *The Slipstream doesn't care who you are. It just asks if you're moving.*

### PANEL 07
**VISUAL:** The Firebird accelerates. No pursuit. No V-Interceptors. No drones. Just the open I-95. The engine's roar is rough and untuned. Headlights cut the dark. Ahead, the highway is empty all the way to the horizon.
**REYA:** "Where are we going?"
**AXEL:** "North."
**REYA:** "That's not an answer."
**AXEL:** "It is for tonight."

### PANEL 08
**VISUAL:** Final splash panel. Rear-view shot from inside the car. The Miami skyline in the mirror, half its lights gone dark. The I-95 ahead of them, two headlights pushing into the black. The yellow center line scrolling past. Above the horizon: a thin, electric-yellow pre-dawn stripe.
**AXEL:** (CAPTION) *Volume Two. Closed.*
**AXEL:** (CAPTION) *Fourteen Directors.*
**AXEL:** (CAPTION) *Fourteen more deliveries.*
