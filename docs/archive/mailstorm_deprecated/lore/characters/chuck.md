# Chuck

**Class/Craft:** Station Manager (Inquisitor)
**Table Status:** Table-less (Soul surrendered to EAS)
**Alignment:** Lawful Evil (A prisoner to his own metrics)

## DBZ Power Scaling (Bureau-Kinesis)
*In the Mailstorm universe, power isn't measured in "Ki"—it's measured in PPH (Pieces Per Hour) output and Grievance Mana.*

* **Aura Signature:** A flickering, unstable red neon grid. Instead of flowing organically like a carrier's aura, Chuck's aura snaps in harsh right angles. It smells heavily of ozone and cheap cologne. When the District Manager calls him, the aura spikes into a terrifying, towering silhouette with multiple glowing eyes (The Overseer state).
* **Base Power Level:** The DOIS Projection (Always claims the route takes exactly 8 hours, regardless of reality).
* **Max Output Level:** "The Final Dispatch" state (Unleashed solely to kick everyone out of the building at 18:00).

## Passive Abilities
* **[The Clipboard Gaze]:** A passive debuff aura. If Chuck stands behind a craft employee and silently watches them work, that employee's movement speed decreases by 10% and their anxiety stat raises by 50%.
* **[Division Mana Siphon]:** Chuck actually heals HP whenever the Clerks and the Carriers argue with each other. The friction of the floor nourishes his administrative core.

## Signature Attacks (Arts & Wards)
* **[The Catch-22 Geass]:** Functionally identical to the "Orb of Confusion." Chuck issues a direct order that fundamentally contradicts established safety protocols. If the carrier disobeys, they are written up for "Failure to Follow Instructions." If they obey and get hurt, they are written up for "Unsafe Acts." It paralyzes logical thought.
* **[The Phantom Pivot]:** An aggressive, unblockable sneak attack. Chuck waits until a carrier has their vehicle completely loaded and the engine running before sliding exactly 45 minutes of extra mail through their window, slamming the door, and walking away before allowing a response.

## Inventory Loadout
* **[The DOIS Printout]:** (Blessed) A sacred spreadsheet printed fresh every morning that Chuck uses as an absolute religious text to dictate reality, even if the physical mail proves otherwise.
* **[The Teleconference Headset]:** (Cursed) Constantly implanted in his ear. It physically roots his brain to the District level, forcing him to absorb the endless psychic screams of the higher-ups demanding better numbers.

## Deep Lore / Secrets
At his core, Chuck used to be human. He was a decent carrier 15 years ago, but he accepted exactly one 204b detail to get off a heavy walking route during a bad winter. He hasn't seen the sun since. The human Chuck is dead, trapped inside the hollowed-out Administrator shell. At exactly 3:00 AM, he occasionally wakes up in a cold sweat, remembering what it felt like to have friends.
