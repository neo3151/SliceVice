# Heather

**Class/Craft:** Window Clerk (Mage/Cleric)
**Table Status:** Table 2 (But utterly immune to its curse)
**Alignment:** True Neutral (Refuses to engage in emotional labor)

## DBZ Power Scaling (Bureau-Kinesis)
*In the Mailstorm universe, power isn't measured in "Ki"—it's measured in PPH (Pieces Per Hour) output and Grievance Mana.*

* **Aura Signature:** An aggressively hollow void. Her aura isn't a bright light—it is a literal black hole of apathy so dense that it sucks the light, sound, and energy out of the lobby. When angry customers attempt to scream at her, their sound waves are visibly absorbed into the void, leaving them breathless and exhausted while Heather just blinks slowly.
* **Base Power Level:** Bare Minimum Effort. She deliberately moves at exactly 50% speed to pace herself.
* **Max Output Level:** "The Dead Stare." She stops blinking entirely until the customer yields.

## Passive Abilities
* **[Gen-Z Shield]:** A passive immunity buff. She is completely immune to Boomer logic, emotional manipulation, and traditional forms of stress. Management threats casually bounce off her because she genuinely does not care if they fire her; she assumes society will collapse before she retires anyway.
* **[Airflow Regeneration]:** Standing behind the retail counter under the AC vents slowly regenerates her HP. She intentionally chose the Clerk class solely to avoid walking outside in the heat.

## Signature Attacks (Arts & Wards)
* **[The Manager Summon]:** The ultimate emotional diversion spell. When a customer demands a manager, Heather effortlessly steps aside without a word, allowing "The Algorithm" (in the form of the local Postmaster) to emerge and repeat the exact, identical policy she just stated. It inflicts massive ego damage on the customer.
* **[The Form 3811 Rejection]:** An annoying, high-tier intercept. She simply looks at the customer's incorrectly filled-out certified mail receipt, taps it exactly once with a highlighter, and pushes it back across the counter without speaking. It forces the customer to break their own momentum and rewrite their form in a corner of shame.

## Inventory Loadout
* **[The Barcode Scanner]:** (Blessed) Her primary wand. It emits a harsh beep that formally accepts or rejects reality depending on what the machine decides.
* **[The RSS Terminal]:** (Cursed) The Retail Systems Software. Slower than dial-up internet and prone to completely unprompted crashes during massive lines, forcing her to cast "The Apology Sigh" over and over again.

## Deep Lore / Secrets
Heather's deepest, darkest fear is being trapped at the front Window during the duration of "Holiday Season." Even her ironclad apathy cracks under the weight of three hundred consecutive Karens demanding to know why shipping an oversized grandfather clock to Alaska costs more than twelve dollars. She uses noise-canceling earbuds to survive her commute to prevent "Spillover Aggro."
