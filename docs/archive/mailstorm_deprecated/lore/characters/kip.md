# Kip Baxter

**Class/Craft:** CCA - City Carrier Assistant (Rogue/Sacrifice)
**Table Status:** Table 2 (Cursed / Erratic Aura)
**Alignment:** Lawful Good (Rapidly deteriorating towards Chaotic Neutral)

## DBZ Power Scaling (Bureau-Kinesis)
*In the Mailstorm universe, power isn't measured in "Ki"—it's measured in PPH (Pieces Per Hour) output and Grievance Mana.*

* **Aura Signature:** A flickering, unstable yellow spark of pure anxiety. Kip possesses almost zero Grievance Mana, making his aura incredibly weak compared to the veterans. However, because his aura is completely untethered by the rules of the Union Contract (due to his probation status), it occasionally violently flares out uncontrollably when he is cornered by Management.
* **Base Power Level:** 15% Efficiency (Constantly getting lost or mis-delivering).
* **Max Output Level:** "The Panic Sprint" (He literally runs his route, bleeding HP the entire time).

## Passive Abilities
* **[The Invisible Man]:** Because he isn't a career regular, the Algorithm views him as wildly disposable. While this means he takes maximum damage from Management attacks, he can occasionally slip out the back dock unnoticed because the system doesn't physically recognize his soul as permanent yet.
* **[Incorruptible Will]:** Kip’s hidden, terrifying stat. No matter how many times he is yelled at, given terrible routes, or sent back out in the dark to deliver someone else's mail, his HP never permanently zeroes out. His constant survival acts as a deep anomaly in the Algorithm’s data, infuriating Chuck.

## Signature Attacks (Arts & Wards)
* **[Hyper-Anxious Flow]:** When Kip is entirely overwhelmed by mail volume and the sun sets, he enters his Awakening state. Time doesn't slow down—instead, Kip moves at double speed while his internal monologue screams the entire time. To the outside world, he appears as a blurry tornado of khaki, frantically throwing mail into boxes completely fueled by pure adrenaline and the desperate fear of being fired.
* **[The Mis-Delivery Misdirection]:** A clumsy evasion technique. By accidentally delivering mail to the wrong address, he temporarily confuses the Algorithm's GPS tracking, gaining a few brief seconds of respite while the system attempts to calculate where he actually is.

## Inventory Loadout
* **[The Glitching MDD Scanner]:** (Cursed) Completely unreliable. It screams randomly, requires multiple reboots, and constantly tries to trick him into failing "Stationary Events." However, when thrown in a panic, it acts as a remarkably heavy projectile.
* **[The Broken Headlamp]:** (Blessed... barely) Given to him in the dead of winter by a supervisor who laughed. It emits a sad, dim yellow cone of light for exactly three seconds before failing. He relies on it completely, having to physically smack it like a magical talisman just to see the house numbers in the abyssal dark.

## Deep Lore / Secrets
Kip truly believes the corporate propaganda that "hard work pays off." He is fighting a war he doesn't fully understand yet. His greatest pending character arc is surviving his 90-day probationary period. The moment day 91 arrives, his soul will undergo "The Hardening"—his erratic yellow aura will stabilize, his frantic running will stop, and he will look the Algorithm directly in the eye and say the magic words for the very first time: *"No."*
