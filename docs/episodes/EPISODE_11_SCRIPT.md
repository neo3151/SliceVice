# EPISODE 11 SCRIPT: "THE CONCRETE SIEGE"
**Date:** Feb 19, 1980
**Historical Anchor:** Mariel Boatlift pre-tension. Cuban nationals flooding the Miami harbor channels. Coast Guard is stretched to the limit—Vanguard fills the vacuum.

---

**Panel 01**
AXEL: (Caption) The Palmetto Overpass. We crawled out of the Megaplex tunnels like mold from a cracked wall. Stolen turbine, no plates, a tape that's worth more than the city it mapped.

**Panel 02**
AXEL: (Caption) The radio's been dead for six hours. That's not silence—that's the Grid being turned off one node at a time. Vance is Level-Zeroing from the inside.
REYA: (Dialogue) "Axel. The Civic Amphitheater. Look at the skyline."

**Panel 03**
AXEL: (Caption) I look. Three separate convoys are converging on the structure from three separate vectors. White Vanguard SUVs from the north. Low, black Cartel wedge-cars from the south. And something else—heavy, armored, military-surplus—from the east.
AXEL: (Dialogue) "Who's the third faction?"

**Panel 04**
REYA: (Dialogue) "Ghost-roster Vanguard. Vance's private off-books unit. They don't appear on comms. They don't file audits. They just collect."
AXEL: (Caption) A three-way siege on a concrete amphitheater in a flooded city. Every one of them wants the Tape. Only one of them has my mother's debt on file.

**Panel 05**
AXEL: (Caption) We ditch the turbine in the drainage canal and push on foot. The Cartel is already breaching the south gate—a pair of Countach wedges nosing through a chain-link curtain wall like knives through wet cardboard.
AXEL: (Dialogue) "Reya. The PA tower. It's our only high ground."

**Panel 06**
REYA: (Dialogue) "The tower's inside the Vanguard perimeter, Axel. We'd have to go through them."
AXEL: (Caption) Through them. Right. Like the whole night hadn't been a controlled demolition of every good option I had left.

**Panel 07**
AXEL: (Caption) I pull the Mossberg and rack a shell. One round left in the drum after the stadium. I'm building from scrap again.
AXEL: (Dialogue) "Then we go through the Vanguard. The Cartel and the Ghost-Roster will do our flanking for us. We just have to outlast the crossfire."

**Panel 08**
AXEL: (Caption) An explosion. The south wall of the Amphitheater erupts in a plume of white phosphorous—a Cartel signature incendiary. The Vanguard line breaks at the south. They pivot, pulling troops off the north perimeter.
REYA: (Dialogue) "NOW, Axel!"

**Panel 09**
AXEL: (Caption) We breach the gap and sprint through the smoke. The Amphitheater's main floor is a battlefield—concrete dust, spinning amber lights, screaming hydraulics. We stay low, moving through the chaos like two more pieces of debris.

**Panel 10**
AXEL: (Caption) Halfway up the PA tower stairs, a Ghost-Roster operative steps out of the dark. No uniform. No insignia. Just a zip-tie on his wrist where the Vanguard patch used to be.
GHOST: (Dialogue) "The Tape. Hand it over. Director Vance wants it returned quietly."
AXEL: (Caption) Quietly. Right.

**Panel 11**
AXEL: (Caption) I swing the Mossberg stock—a calculated use of the last thing I've got. The operative goes down on the iron staircase with a sound like a dropped engine block. Reya is already at the top.

**Panel 12**
REYA: (Dialogue) "I'm into the tower's broadcast array. I can use the Tape's magnetic frequency to jam every comm unit on the field simultaneously. All three factions go blind at once."
AXEL: (Caption) A window. Ten, maybe fifteen seconds of controlled chaos where nobody can talk to anybody. In a three-faction siege, that's an extinction-level event for command structure.

**Panel 13**
AXEL: (Caption) The jam hits. Every comm on the field erupts in white noise. Vanguard SUVs peel in the wrong direction. Two Cartel Countaches ram each other at the south gate. The Ghost-Roster freezes, waiting for orders that aren't coming.
AXEL: (Dialogue) "Reya. Tell me the Tape is still intact."

**Panel 14**
REYA: (Dialogue) "The Tape is intact. But Axel—I found a second signal buried in the frequency. Someone else has been reading the Grid. Someone who isn't Vance."
AXEL: (Caption) The city holds its breath. So do I.
