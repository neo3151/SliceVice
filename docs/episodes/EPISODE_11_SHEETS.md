# EPISODE 11 VISUAL SHEETS: "THE CONCRETE SIEGE"

**Style Anchors (Volume 2):**
> "...heavy industrial ink style, deep amber lighting, rain-drenched rusted metal, brutalist 1980s miami architecture, cinematic noir shadows, thick mechanical detail, analog grit, 4k high fidelity graphic novel art."

**New Location Notes — The Civic Amphitheater:**
The Palmetto Overpass feeds down into a brutalist concrete open-air amphitheater—exposed aggregate walls, a massive curved concrete shell roof, standing black water pooling on the performance floor. Every surface is stained with saltwater rust rings. The PA tower is a skeletal steel lattice bolted to the shell's spine.
