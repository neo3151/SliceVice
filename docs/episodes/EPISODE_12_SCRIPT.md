# EPISODE 12 SCRIPT: "THE BREAKING POINT"
**Date:** Feb 20, 1980
**Historical Anchor:** Freedom Tower Miami—formerly the Columbus Hotel—is re-designated as a Cuban refugee processing center. The building literally becomes a machine for processing displaced people. Mirrors Axel's mother being processed/stored in the Vanguard's servers.

---

**Panel 01**
AXEL: (Caption) The second signal. Reya ran the printout through the tower's half-functional terminal and found a clean origin address. Not a corporate node. Not a Cartel relay. A single, residential broadcast terminal in the Drowned District. Someone in the water has been reading the Grid-Tape for months.

**Panel 02**
REYA: (Dialogue) "The address parses to a structure in Sector 404-Delta. That's three meters underwater at high tide, Axel. Nobody lives there."
AXEL: (Caption) Nobody they have on record.

**Panel 03**
AXEL: (Caption) We move before the factions regroup. Twelve blocks east through a city that's been quietly turning itself off. Neon signs dark. Shops shuttered. The streets belong to the water and the rats and the two of us.

**Panel 04**
AXEL: (Caption) The Vanguard Dispatch frequency clicks back on. One word at a time, broken by interference.
DISPATCH: (Dialogue) "...Cutter... Override Protocol... your mother's archive... is scheduled for... full Erasure... at 0600..."
AXEL: (Caption) Erasure. Not repossession. Not foreclosure. Erasure.

**Panel 05**
AXEL: (Caption) My legs stop. I'm standing in six inches of black canal water, rain hammering my shoulders, and the world just changed pitch.
REYA: (Dialogue) "Axel. I know what you heard. Keep moving. You can't help her if you're standing in the open."

**Panel 06**
AXEL: (Caption) Sector 404-Delta. The building is a two-story 1972 apartment block—concrete, rotting, half-submerged. But the second floor windows are lit. Pale blue CRT glow behind boarded glass.
AXEL: (Dialogue) "Someone's home."

**Panel 07**
AXEL: (Caption) The contact is old. A man named Kallan—the same Kallan from ep10, the one who'd helped us cross the Megaplex. He'd been here the whole time. His apartment is floor-to-ceiling magnetic tape archives. Thousands of reels, meticulously catalogued.
KALLAN: (Dialogue) "I've been reading the Tape's broadcast for two years. Every time Vance ran a 'Sector Format,' I intercepted the bleed frequency and mapped what was being erased. I have a record of everything they've deleted."

**Panel 08**
AXEL: (Dialogue) "Can you extract my mother? Her archive record. Can you pull it from the Vanguard servers before 0600?"
KALLAN: (Dialogue) "I can reach the server. But if I do, it triggers a locator pulse. Vance will have this building surrounded in eleven minutes."
AXEL: (Caption) Eleven minutes. That's math I can work with.

**Panel 09**
KALLAN: (Dialogue) "There's something else, Axel. The Grid-Tape—if you play it into the central terminal at the Freedom Tower, it doesn't just reformat the city. It executes a full 'Reboot.' Every stored archive in every Vanguard server gets flushed and rewritten to the Tape's original 1970 baseline. Every debt record. Every repossession order. Every foreclosure file. Gone."
AXEL: (Caption) Gone. Thousands of people in the Drowned District—their debt chains, their repossession orders, every mechanism of Vanguard ownership over their lives. All of it erased. Including my mother's file.

**Panel 10**
REYA: (Dialogue) "But the 1970 baseline also wipes the land reclamation infrastructure. Every pylon, every flood gate, every pump station goes offline simultaneously. Axel... there are people living in Sector 404. They'd be underwater within the hour."
AXEL: (Caption) There it is. The debt of the choice. Free thousands by erasing the records—but drown the ones who built their lives in the margins.

**Panel 11**
AXEL: (Caption) I sit with that for a long time. Longer than I've sat with anything since I picked up the dead courier's cargo on New Year's Day.

**Panel 12**
AXEL: (Dialogue) "Kallan. Pull my mother's archive. Buy me eleven minutes."
AXEL: (Caption) Not the whole Grid. Not yet. Just her. One file. The only debt that was ever actually mine.

**Panel 13**
AXEL: (Caption) The locator pulse goes out the moment Kallan hits the server. I'm already moving—back to the street, back to the canal, back to the last working payphone in the Drowned District.

**Panel 14**
AXEL: (Caption) I dial the only number Vance ever gave me for emergencies. He picks up before the second ring.
AXEL: (Dialogue) "Vance. I have the Tape. And I have a proposition. You're going to want to hear this in person."
AXEL: (Caption) Eleven minutes. And I intend to use every single one of them.
