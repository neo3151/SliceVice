# EPISODE 12 VISUAL SHEETS: "THE BREAKING POINT"

**Style Anchors (Volume 2):**
> "...heavy industrial ink style, deep amber lighting, rain-drenched rusted metal, brutalist 1980s miami architecture, cinematic noir shadows, thick mechanical detail, analog grit, 4k high fidelity graphic novel art."

**New Location Notes — Sector 404-Delta / Kallan's Archive:**
A partially-submerged 1972 concrete apartment block. The ground floor is black water and floating debris. The second floor is densely inhabited—walls lined with custom-built wooden shelving holding thousands of magnetic tape reels in cardboard boxes. Pale blue CRT monitors on every surface. The lighting is entirely self-generated: extension cords running out a window to a gasoline generator on the roof.
