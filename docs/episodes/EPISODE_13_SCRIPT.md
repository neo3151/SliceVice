# EPISODE 13 SCRIPT: "THE UNINSTALLER"
**Date:** Feb 20, 1980 — 0430 Hours
**Historical Anchor:** The Freedom Tower, Miami. Built 1925, used as a processing center for over 400,000 Cuban refugees. It is the physical monument to the act of being erased by one system and rewritten by another.

---

**Panel 01**
AXEL: (Caption) The Freedom Tower. Its Moorish revival brickwork glows damp amber under the pre-dawn sky. Twenty-six stories of archive servers humming behind the ornate facade. The most beautiful foreclosure filing cabinet in the Western Hemisphere.

**Panel 02**
AXEL: (Caption) Vance meets me at the service entrance alone. That tells me everything. He doesn't want witnesses for this part.
VANCE: (Dialogue) "You look like a car wreck, Cutter."
AXEL: (Dialogue) "I've been driving one."

**Panel 03**
VANCE: (Dialogue) "Give me the Tape. Walk away. I'll release your mother's archive. Clean slate. You go back to running pizza slips and I never file the Repossession Order."
AXEL: (Caption) Clean slate. He says it like it's a gift. Like the last fifty-one days didn't happen. Like the Drowned District doesn't exist.

**Panel 04**
AXEL: (Dialogue) "Here's my counter-offer."
AXEL: (Caption) I hit him. Clean. Hard. The way a man hits a machine that's been running too long without a service check. He goes down on the service ramp and stays down.
AXEL: (Caption) I take his access card. I take his radio. I take the stairs.

**Panel 05**
AXEL: (Caption) Level 14. The Grid Terminal. A room-sized mainframe of magnetic drum readers and reel-to-reel drives, floor to ceiling. The hum is physical—a 60-cycle vibration I feel in my back teeth.

**Panel 06**
REYA: (Dialogue, over radio) "Axel. Kallan confirmed your mother's file is extracted and decrypted. It's on the reel in your jacket pocket. She is not in the server anymore."
AXEL: (Caption) She's out. The file that was used as a leash around my neck for eleven years is sitting in my jacket pocket on a spool the size of my fist. 

**Panel 07**
AXEL: (Caption) Now comes the hard part. The Grid-Tape in the master drive slot. Not a full Reboot. Not a wipe. Something Reya calculated in the hour between the Amphitheater and now.
REYA: (Dialogue, over radio) "A selective reformat. I wrote the instruction headers directly onto the Tape leader. It will execute a targeted flush—debt records, repossession orders, foreclosure files only. The infrastructure logic stays intact. The pumps keep running. The sector doesn't flood."

**Panel 08**
AXEL: (Caption) I thread the Tape into the master drive. The terminal accepts it with the sound of a lock recognizing the right key for the first time.

**Panel 09**
AXEL: (Caption) The drives spin up. The amber indicator lights across the room switch from red to a cycling pattern I've never seen before. The sound changes—from a hum to something almost like a held breath.

**Panel 10**
AXEL: (Caption) On every floor of the Freedom Tower, Vanguard screens display the same message in block green phosphor text: **ARCHIVE REFORMAT IN PROGRESS. ESTIMATED COMPLETION: 14 MINUTES.**
AXEL: (Caption) Fourteen minutes. Every debt chain in the Drowned District, quietly unwinding.

**Panel 11**
AXEL: (Caption) A crash behind me. Vance. I underestimated his recovery time. He's at the terminal room door with two Ghost-Roster operatives. His face is controlled fury, the first real emotion I've ever seen him show.
VANCE: (Dialogue) "HALT THE PROCESS, CUTTER. RIGHT NOW."
AXEL: (Caption) I look at the progress indicator. Eight percent complete.

**Panel 12**
AXEL: (Caption) I put my back against the terminal and my feet against the door. All two hundred and ten pounds of me, every mile of the last fifty-one days, every foreclosure notice, every dead-end back road, holding a door shut for six more minutes.
AXEL: (Dialogue) "Come back in fourteen minutes, Vance. I'll have a receipt ready."

**Panel 13**
AXEL: (Caption) The door holds. The drives spin. The indicator climbs: 40%. 67%. 89%.
AXEL: (Caption) I think about the LeMans, rusting at the foot of the Marine Stadium. I think about my mother's voice on those Vanguard PA drones—synthesized, weaponized, used against me. And I think about how the real version of it is now on a tape in my jacket, small enough to hold in one hand.

**Panel 14**
AXEL: (Caption) 100%. The drives wind down. The amber lights go steady green—all of them, simultaneously, floor to ceiling. A quiet, analog click as the terminal reads: **REFORMAT COMPLETE.**
AXEL: (Caption) I open the door. Vance is still there. I hand him his access card back. He looks at the terminal. He looks at me. He's running the calculation—the cost of making noise versus the cost of walking away.
AXEL: (Dialogue) "The Drowned District's debt is cleared. The infrastructure is intact. Nobody drowned tonight. File whatever audit you want. The Grid already answered it."
AXEL: (Caption) He walks away. That's it. No final speech. No dramatic repossession order. Just a man making the only economic decision left to him. I let him go. I already got what I came for.
