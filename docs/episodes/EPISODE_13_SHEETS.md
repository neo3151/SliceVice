# EPISODE 13 VISUAL SHEETS: "THE UNINSTALLER"

**Style Anchors (Volume 2):**
> "...heavy industrial ink style, deep amber lighting, rain-drenched rusted metal, brutalist 1980s miami architecture, cinematic noir shadows, thick mechanical detail, analog grit, 4k high fidelity graphic novel art."

**New Location Notes — The Freedom Tower:**
Miami Freedom Tower (1925 Moorish Revival). 26 stories. Ornate brickwork with Spanish Colonial detailing—arched windows, a decorative copper cupola. The lower floors have been gut-renovated into an industrial data center behind the historic facade. Server racks behind Renaissance arches. Amber indicator lights reflected in polished marble floor.
