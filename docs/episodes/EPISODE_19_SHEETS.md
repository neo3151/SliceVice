# EPISODE 19: THE INVISIBLE GRID

## SCENE 01: Tin Roofs and Copper Wire

**[PANEL 1 - WIDE SHOT]**
**Visual:** Torrential Miami afternoon downpour. The sky is a bruised, oppressive gray. Axel’s highly modified ’78 LeMans is parked half-under a rusted corrugated tin overhang in Sector H. Rain is exploding off the hood, washing away thick coats of dried sea-salt. 

**[PANEL 2 - MEDIUM SHOT]**
**Visual:** Under the overhang. Axel is wiping grease off his hands with a dirty shop rag. Jean-Pierre is sitting on an overturned milk crate, using a rusted utility knife to strip black insulation off a thick coil of copper wire. 
**JEAN-PIERRE (CAPTION):** "You’re lucky the salt didn’t eat through the coaxial completely. The Vanguard's thermal ping would have lit you up on the Causeway."
**AXEL (CAPTION):** "I kept it in the mud. They don’t scan the shallows."

**[PANEL 3 - CLOSE UP]**
**Visual:** Rainwater pouring off the jagged edge of the tin roof, creating a literal curtain of water just inches from Jean-Pierre’s face. He doesn’t look up from stripping the wire.
**JEAN-PIERRE (CAPTION):** "Maybe. But my receivers were screaming all night anyway. Channel 16. Coast Guard frequencies."
**AXEL (CAPTION):** "Vanguard running a sweep?"

**[PANEL 4 - MEDIUM SHOT]**
**Visual:** Jean-Pierre stops cutting. He drops the wire and looks up at Axel, his expression deadly serious despite the casual setting.
**JEAN-PIERRE (CAPTION):** "No. Unfiltered marine engines. Hundreds of them. Coming straight north up the Straits."

**[PANEL 5 - CLOSE UP]**
**Visual:** Axel looking out through the curtain of rain. He remembers the dawn at the Lighthouse.
**AXEL (CAPTION):** "...I know. I heard them."

## SCENE 02: The Ventanita Defense

**[PANEL 6 - WIDE ACTION SHOT]**
**Visual:** The matte-black LeMans fishtailing violently out of the mud of Sector H, tearing onto a rain-slicked avenue. A massive plume of dirty water is kicked up from the rear tires.
**CAPTION (AXEL):** "The Vanguard wasn't just doing radio sweeps. They were drawing a net."

**[PANEL 7 - STREET LEVEL WIDE]**
**Visual:** Calle Ocho in the storm. The neon lights of Versailles Restaurant reflect beautifully in massive, turbulent puddles on the asphalt. A heavy Miami police presence is vaguely visible in the blurred background—cruisers with their lightbars flashing, signaling the rising structural tension in the city.

**[PANEL 8 - TIGHT INTERIOR]**
**Visual:** Inside the cramped Ventanita. Steam from the espresso machines fills the tight frame. Axel physically slams his grease-stained hands down onto the stainless steel counter, rainwater dripping heavily from his face. 
**AXEL:** "Carmen. You need to lock this down. The Straits are flooded with—"

**[PANEL 9 - MEDIUM SHOT]**
**Visual:** Doña Carmen stands firmly behind the counter. She is completely calm, her eyes sharp. With a fluid, practiced motion, she is racking a shell into a heavy pump-action shotgun resting right next to the industrial coffee grinder. 
**DOÑA CARMEN:** "I know. The local band hasn't stopped."

**[PANEL 10 - CLOSE UP]**
**Visual:** Doña Carmen pushing a heavy, military-surplus canvas duffel bag across the stainless steel counter toward Axel. A stack of analog radios and a thick envelope of cash spills slightly from the zipper. 
**DOÑA CARMEN:** "Vance is locking down the coast early. The municipal police are paralyzed. I need you to run these bypass-transmitters to the docks right now, or thousands of people are going to walk straight into The Vanguard's collection nets."

## SCENE 03: The First Wave (The Docks)

**[PANEL 11 - WIDE SHOT]**
**Visual:** The pitch-black docks at the edge of Sector H. No streetlights. The water is churning violently. Axel's LeMans is parked near the edge of a splintering wooden pier, its headlights turned off. Only the dim amber glow of the dashboard illuminates Axel inside.

**[PANEL 12 - MEDIUM SHOT]**
**Visual:** A dilapidated, 40-foot cigarette boat slowly cuts through the fog and docks at the pier. It is absurdly overcrowded with terrified, exhausted refugees huddled under plastic tarps. The boat looks like it barely survived the Straits.

**[PANEL 13 - ACTION SHOT]**
**Visual:** Axel standing on the wooden pier in the heavy rain, physically handing analog bypass-transmitters (from Doña Carmen's duffel bag) down to the people on the boat. A refugee looks up at him with profound relief.
**AXEL (CAPTION):** "The Mesh-Network isn't built on copper wire. It's built on blood. And right now, the city is bleeding fast."

**[PANEL 14 - WIDE SHOT]**
**Visual:** In the deep background, half a mile down the coast, a row of blinding white headlights abruptly clicks on. Heavily armored Vanguard Repo-Trucks establish a perimeter. In the center of the lights, a silhouette steps out of the lead truck onto the beach. It's Director Vance, personally overseeing the nets. 
**AXEL (CAPTION):** "Vance is on the beach. He's here to collect."

**[PANEL 15 - CLOSE UP]**
**Visual:** Axel sliding back into the driver's seat of the LeMans. He is shifting into first gear, his eyes sharp and focused in the rearview mirror as the cigarette boat vanishes safely into the swamp fog behind him. He didn't get his money tonight, but he beat the house.
**AXEL (CAPTION):** "Let the Vanguard scan the shore. Tonight, they only catch the ghosts."
