# EPISODE 1 SCRIPT: "THE I-95 SLIPSTREAM"

**Panel 01**
AXEL: (Caption) Miami after midnight. The humidity is so thick you can taste the salt and the smog. The only thing moving faster than the rain is the debt.

**Panel 02**
AXEL: (Caption) The Slice Vice Hub. A grease-stained garage on Biscayne. One of the last places that still delivers to the 'Grey Zones' without a police escort.

**Panel 03**
RILEY: (Dialogue) "Cutter, you're up. Secure delivery to the Glades. A 'Black-Box' special. No questions, no stops."

**Panel 04**
AXEL: (Caption) I check the LeMans. The V8 is grumbling. The tires are slick, but the block is solid. I’ve got twenty-nine minutes to hit the shack.

**Panel 05**
AXEL: (Caption) I hit the I-95 slipstream. The asphalt is a mirror of mercury and pink neon.

**Panel 06**
AXEL: (Caption) Suddenly, a matte-black sports car swerves into my lane. A local cartel runner. They don't like couriers crossing their turf without a tax.

**Panel 07**
AXEL: (Caption) He tries a PIT maneuver. I feel the steel of his bumper scrape the LeMans' fender. Sparks rain like orange snow.

**Panel 08**
AXEL: (Caption) I know this road. I know where the sinkhole is hidden under the puddled asphalt. I tap the brake, let him slide, and watch his suspension shatter as he hits the dip.

**Panel 09**
AXEL: (Caption) The road turns to gravel as I hit the edge of the Everglades. The smell of rotting mangroves fills the cabin.

**Panel 10**
AXEL: (Caption) A massive Florida Gator, six feet of prehistoric muscle, drags its tail across the road. Eyes glowing yellow in my headlights.

**Panel 11**
AXEL: (Caption) I rip the wheel, drifting the rear of the LeMans into the mud to clear the tail. The car screams, but we keep moving.

**Panel 12**
AXEL: (Caption) The drop-off. A stilted shack deep in the blackwater. No lights. Just the sound of crickets and a cocked shotgun.

**Panel 13**
CUSTOMER: (Dialogue) "You're late, boy. Delivery's cold."
AXEL: (Dialogue) "Check the seal. It’s exactly what you ordered."

**Panel 14**
AXEL: (Caption) He tosses a heavy envelope of black-market cash onto the dashboard. I don't count it. I just peel out, leaving the mangroves behind. One more night paid for.
