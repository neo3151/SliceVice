# EPISODE 20 PANEL BREAKDOWN: "THE WATER LEVEL" (9 PANELS)
**Date:** March 28, 1980 — Late Afternoon
**Tone:** Still water. A body coming back online. The city is quiet in a way that has weight.
**Color Key:** Warm amber post-rain light. Steam off black asphalt. The Brickell marina is wrong-empty. Pocket-left is where the tape was. That pocket has a recipe card now.

---

**Page 1 (The Drive)**
- **Panel 01:** (Interior/Dashboard) The LeMans dashboard from the passenger side. The rebuilt transmission — no digital readouts, only Stewart-Warner gauges at rest. Nixie timer showing [11:07] in calm amber. Axel's hands on the wheel, knuckles visible. Steering wheel perfectly centered — no pull, no fight. The smoothness is suspicious.
- **Panel 02:** (Aerial/Wide) Brickell Avenue after the 3 PM rain. Asphalt steaming, wet neon smearing in the puddles, the bay glittering to the east. The LeMans moves deliberately, not fast — the long way down, taking its time. The city looks momentarily clean.
- **Panel 03:** (Ground Level/Static) The marina near the Hyatt. Half the slips are bare. Dock lines hang loose from the cleats with nothing attached — limp, swaying slightly in the cross-breeze. No cigarette boats, no sport fishers. The absence feels like something left in a hurry. The southern slips emptied fastest.

**Page 2 (The Delivery)**
- **Panel 04:** (Wide/Building Exterior) A Brickell high-rise service entrance. The LeMans sits against the curb. The doorman — blue blazer, salt-and-pepper mustache — looks at the car with the practiced contempt of a man confusing proximity to money with having it.
- **Panel 05:** (Interior/Hallway) Fourteenth floor. Apartment 1408. The door open — a woman in her seventies in a housedress, holding a dishcloth. Behind her the apartment smells visible: sofrito steam, something baking. She is looking at Axel the way people look at faces from half-remembered dreams. She is not looking at the pizza box.
- **Panel 06:** (Interior/Service Elevator) Axel alone in the elevator going down. In his hand: a small folded index card. Blue ink, careful cursive. He is reading it with the focused attention of someone trying to understand something simple that keeps being surprising. The card reads: *arroz con leche, serves 4.*

**Page 3 (The Return)**
- **Panel 07:** (Close-up/Jacket) Axel's left jacket pocket. His hand sliding the folded index card in. The pocket is otherwise empty — the tape reel is gone from here, has been since the lighthouse. The card settles into the space it left.
- **Panel 08:** (Ground Level/Marina Redux) Back past the marina. Same shot as Panel 03 but slightly different angle. The dock lines still waving in the cross-breeze. Loose ends. The LeMans reflected in the puddles on the dock road.
- **Panel 09:** (Wide/Tin Can Roof) The tar roof of the laundromat at dusk. Blinker on the hot surface, one eye tracking Axel up the stairs. On a milk crate inside the open container doors: the index card, unfolded. Beside it: a tin with spare keys and razor blades. The mango sits cut on the tar. Blinker has not moved yet. He has opinions about timing.

**Visual Generation Notes:**
- The marina emptiness should feel eerie, not peaceful — dock lines in focus, slips conspicuously bare
- The index card should be legible: *arroz con leche, serves 4*
- Panel 07 is the emotional pivot — the left pocket, the emptiness where the tape was, the card going in
- Blinker always one eye visible; the good one tracks, the socket side is away
