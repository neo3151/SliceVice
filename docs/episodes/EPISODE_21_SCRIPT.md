# EPISODE 21 SCRIPT: "DOCKERY'S"
**Date:** April 3, 1980 — Morning into Afternoon
**Historical Anchor:** Cuba has spent the past six days processing the Peruvian Embassy situation. Today the McDuffie trial — the manslaughter case against five Dade County officers for beating Arthur McDuffie to death in December — has been officially moved to Tampa. Change of venue granted. The argument: Miami cannot produce an impartial jury. The community of Liberty City hears this the way it was meant: the system has removed itself to a room where it cannot be watched. Elio Valdés hears this on the radio in his garage at 10:14 AM and does not speak for eleven minutes.

---

**Panel 01**
AXEL: (Caption) The front end has been pulling left since the Rickenbacker jump. Not dramatically — not enough to fight. Just a suggestion. A persistent, quiet argument from the steering geometry that something isn't aligned the way it used to be.
AXEL: (Caption) Elio is the only person I trust with the LeMans' bones. He doesn't ask what I did to it. He already knows I did something. He just wants to know how bad.

**Panel 02**
AXEL: (Caption) Elio Valdés runs his garage out of a converted auto-body shop on SW 8th Street — a low, wide building with a corrugated metal roll-up door that's perpetually half-open, like it can't commit to being either a business or a cave. The hand-painted sign above the door says VÁLDES AUTO in faded red. Beneath it, in smaller letters that someone added later: *se habla mecánica.*
AXEL: (Caption) Inside: organized chaos. A 1977 Cutlass Supreme on a rolling jack, missing its hood. A boat engine strapped to a workbench with no boat attached — a 350 Mercruiser, completely out of context, surrounded by torque wrenches like a patient mid-surgery. The smell of gear oil and cigarette smoke and the specific metallic tang of a grinder recently used.

**Panel 03**
AXEL: (Caption) Elio looks at the LeMans the way doctors look at X-rays. Not at the surface — through it.
ELIO: (Dialogue) "Pull it in. Full in. Stop."
AXEL: (Caption) He walks the perimeter slowly, not touching anything yet. His nephew Ricky, seventeen, is watching from a folding chair in the corner, doing homework on a clipboard. He looks up when I pull in and goes back to his homework. People bringing destroyed cars to Elio is background noise to Ricky.

**Panel 04**
AXEL: (Caption) Elio gets flat on the creeper and slides under before I've finished killing the engine.
ELIO: (Dialogue, from under the car) "When did this start?"
AXEL: (Dialogue) "March."
ELIO: (Dialogue) "March when."
AXEL: (Dialogue) "Mid."
AXEL: (Caption) A long pause from under the car. The sound of a flashlight being repositioned.
ELIO: (Dialogue) "You went off a bridge."
AXEL: (Dialogue) "A drawbridge. It was at thirty degrees."
ELIO: (Dialogue) "Un puñetero puente."
AXEL: (Caption) He already knew.

**Panel 05**
AXEL: (Caption) We work. That's the thing about Elio's garage — it's a place where silence has purpose. He doesn't explain what he's doing until he needs a second pair of hands. I hand him things. He uses them. We don't talk about the bridge.
AXEL: (Caption) By ten o'clock we have the LeMans on the alignment rack, the front end stripped down, both lower control arm bushings pulled and laid on the workbench like small, defeated organs.
ELIO: (Dialogue) "The right one is finished. The left one—" He holds it up to the shop light. "—is also finished, but it doesn't know it yet."
AXEL: (Dialogue) "Can you press new ones?"
ELIO: (Dialogue) "What do you think I'm going to do, pray over it?"

**Panel 06**
AXEL: (Caption) At 10:14, Ricky's radio — a small, battered Sony on the corner of the workbench — interrupts a commercial to report that the McDuffie manslaughter trial has been officially relocated to Tampa by order of the Circuit Court. Change of venue. The judge cited concerns about the availability of an impartial jury pool in Dade County.
AXEL: (Caption) Elio stops. He has a press handle in his right hand and a bushing in his left and he stops with the same absolute stillness that a man stops when he has heard something he was waiting to not hear.
AXEL: (Caption) Ricky looks up from his homework. Neither of them speak. The radio moves on to a traffic report. The Palmetto Expressway is backed up past NW 57th. Business as usual.

**Panel 07**
AXEL: (Caption) Elio sets the bushing on the workbench. He wipes his hands on a shop rag — slowly, thoroughly, one finger at a time. He does not look at Ricky. He does not look at me.
ELIO: (Dialogue) "They moved it so nobody's watching."
AXEL: (Caption) He isn't asking for a response. He isn't offering a conversation. He is stating a fact with the quiet fury of a man who has been storing quiet fury for a very long time and has gotten very efficient at it.

**Panel 08**
AXEL: (Dialogue) "Are you okay?"
ELIO: (Caption — beat. Elio picks the bushing back up.)
ELIO: (Dialogue) "I'm always okay. That's the problem."
AXEL: (Caption) We work until two in the afternoon without stopping.

**Panel 09**
AXEL: (Caption) When I pull the LeMans off the rack, the steering wheel is straight for the first time since the bridge. Not pulling. Not suggesting. Just — straight. The wheel sits level at noon and the car tracks true and the road comes at me clean.
AXEL: (Caption) I drive it around the block to feel it. The G-body sits flat through the turn at Calle Ocho. The rebuilt transmission grabs third exactly when I ask for it. The front end holds the line.
AXEL: (Caption) I pull back into the garage to settle up. Elio is already back under the Cutlass.

**Panel 10**
AXEL: (Caption) He doesn't come out from under the car, but he shoves something across the floor toward me with one boot — slid along the concrete like he's pretending it isn't there. A small, heavy thing wrapped in shop rag.
AXEL: (Caption) I pick it up. Unwrap it. The vault — the lead-lined steel box welded to my transmission tunnel. Elio pulled it while I wasn't paying attention.
AXEL: (Caption) The hairline fracture from the bridge jump is gone. He welded it. The seam is clean, tight — better than the original work. He didn't mention it. He didn't charge me for it. He just fixed it and slid it across the floor.
AXEL: (Dialogue) "Elio—"
ELIO: (From under the car) "Go drive."
AXEL: (Caption) I go drive.
