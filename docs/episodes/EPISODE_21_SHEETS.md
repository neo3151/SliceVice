# EPISODE 21 PANEL BREAKDOWN: "DOCKERY'S" (10 PANELS)
**Date:** April 3, 1980 — Morning into Afternoon
**Tone:** Men working. Silence with purpose. The McDuffie trial news lands like a stone in still water.
**Color Key:** Shop floor grey, motor oil brown, the specific fluorescent amber of work lights on bare metal. Ricky's Sony radio as the only warm color. The shop rag Elio uses at Panel 07 is important — linger on it.

---

**Page 1 (The Garage)**
- **Panel 01:** (Ground Level/Exterior) SW 8th Street, morning. Elio's garage — low, wide building, corrugated roll-up door perpetually half-open. The hand-painted sign: VÁLDES AUTO in faded red, *se habla mecánica* in smaller letters below. The LeMans sits on the street outside, not yet pulled in.
- **Panel 02:** (Interior/Wide) Inside the garage: organized chaos. A 1977 Cutlass Supreme missing its hood on a rolling jack. A 350 Mercruiser boat engine strapped to a workbench surrounded by torque wrenches — no boat anywhere nearby. Ricky, seventeen, in a folding chair with homework on a clipboard. Elio walking the LeMans perimeter slowly, not touching, reading it from the outside.
- **Panel 03:** (Close-up/Under Car) The creeper from below. Elio's face and flashlight visible from underneath the LeMans. Axel's boots next to the front tire. The undercarriage in harsh flashlight — the evidence of a car that went off a bridge at 30 degrees visible in bent geometry.

**Page 2 (The Work)**
- **Panel 04:** (Workbench/Close) Two lower control arm bushings laid on the metal workbench under the shop light. Small, cylindrical, rubber — one clearly destroyed, one "also finished but doesn't know it yet." Elio holding the second one up to the light with two fingers.
- **Panel 05:** (Wide/Alignment Rack) The LeMans on the alignment rack. The front end stripped down, both wheels off. The car looks vulnerable from this angle — stripped of its armor, just a machine that needs setting right. Elio and Axel working in parallel, no conversation.

**Page 3 (The News)**
- **Panel 06:** (Close-up/Radio) Ricky's small battered Sony radio on the corner of the workbench. Not a dramatic shot — just the radio, ordinary, mid-broadcast. The indicator needle. The moment just before the news lands.
- **Panel 07:** (Mid-shot/Elio) Elio stopped. Press handle in right hand, bushing in left. The specific stillness of a man who has heard something he was waiting to not hear. Ricky in the background, pencil paused above homework. Neither of them looking at each other. The radio is in the background, already moving on to traffic.
- **Panel 08:** (Close-up/Hands) Elio's hands on the shop rag. Wiping one finger at a time, slowly, thoroughly. The rag already grey with grease. He is not looking at anything. This is the most important panel in the episode — the careful ritual of a man containing something large.

**Page 4 (The Fix)**
- **Panel 09:** (Exterior/Street) The LeMans back on SW 8th, steering wheel dead center. This is an interior shot through the windshield — Axel's hands on the wheel, wheel at noon, the street coming at the car perfectly straight. No pull. The alignment holds.
- **Panel 10:** (Interior/Floor) The garage floor, concrete. A shop-rag bundle slid across the floor. Axel's hand picking it up. Unwrapped: the lead-lined vault, hairline fracture gone, weld seam clean. Elio's boots visible under the Cutlass in the background — already back under, not watching.
