# EPISODE 22 PANEL BREAKDOWN: "STATIC MAPS" (11 PANELS)
**Date:** April 9, 1980 — 11 PM to 3 AM
**Tone:** Two people talking through a mail slot at midnight. The city as frequency. Something is crossing the water right now.
**Color Key:** Deep alley dark, the van lit from inside with amber/green console glow, streetlamp sodium yellow on wet concrete. The composition notebook warped and soft from humidity. Maria's hand through the slot — small, heavily scarred. No faces visible until Panel 08.

---

**Page 1 (The Can / The Alley)**
- **Panel 01:** (Interior/Ceiling) The inside of the Tin Can at midnight. Neon light from the street — pink and blue — dragging itself across the corrugated metal ceiling through the ventilation gap. Box fans running. The mattress in frame. The absence of sleep visible in the way a room looks at midnight when someone is still awake in it.
- **Panel 02:** (Wide/Alley) The alley behind the laundromat. Maria's 1974 step-van filling the frame — roof bristling with copper wire, bent hangers, CB antennas in a chaotic canopy. The van lit from inside, warm amber glow at the seams of the rear door. Axel approaching from the left, denim jacket, hands in pockets.
- **Panel 03:** (Close-up/Mail Slot) The rear door of the van from outside. The heavy mail slot at center. Warm amber and green light leaking from inside. Axel's shoulder visible at the edge, leaning against the door. The slot is dark — nothing yet, just the hiss of frequencies behind it.

**Page 2 (The Log)**
- **Panel 04:** (Interior/Console) Seen through the mail slot from outside — a partial view of Maria's workspace inside the van. Six frequency bands on different receivers, needles all in motion. A 1977 FCC manual open on the console. The composition notebook beside it. Maria's hands visible — circuit tattoos, aviator glasses, tuning a dial.
- **Panel 05:** (Workbench-style/Notebook) The composition notebook under the alley streetlight. Warped, fat, cover soft. Pages open to a spread of handwritten marine frequency logs — dates, times, coordinates, call signs, Maria's shorthand. Three sequences circled in red ink, each with *no match. non-standard. repeat pattern* beside them.
- **Panel 06:** (Two-shot/Mail Slot) Axel crouched at the mail slot, notebook in hand. Maria's voice from inside, no face visible. This is their natural geometry — him on the outside, her in the van, the slot between them. The alley is completely dark beyond the streetlamp cone.

**Page 3 (The Question)**
- **Panel 07:** (Wide/Alley) The alley at 1 AM. The van, the streetlamp, the wet concrete. A city-sound composition: the highway hum four blocks over, a siren cresting and fading to the east, the van's frame creaking. Axel's back against the bumper, looking at the sky through a gap in the buildings. The panel should breathe.
- **Panel 08:** (Close-up/Maria) The only panel where Maria's face is partially visible — through the slightly open mail slot, in profile, looking at nothing. Blind eyes behind thick aviator glasses. Not looking at Axel when she asks the question. The question is less intrusive when it isn't aimed at your face.
- **Panel 09:** (Close-up/Slot) Maria's hand coming through the mail slot, gripping Axel's sleeve. The hand is small, heavily scarred, certain. The grip is not gentle — it's informational. The transmission is coming in right now.

**Page 4 (The Transmission)**
- **Panel 10:** (Conceptual/Ocean) Not a literal panel — an interpretive visual. Dark ocean. No lights. A boat silhouette moving north, running dark. The Florida Straits at 3 AM. No moon. The panel should feel like something real happening very far away that has immediate consequence.
- **Panel 11:** (Ground Level/Stairs) Blinker on the bottom stair of the external laundromat staircase. Blocking the path. One eye visible, tracking Axel. The mango in Axel's hand. The alley behind him empty, the van still humming. This is the return to normal after the transmission — the iguana demanding payment for the passage.

**Visual Generation Notes:**
- The mail slot is the recurring visual anchor — every exchange happens through it, never face-to-face
- Panel 10 is the most important visual in the episode — it should suggest the vast dark ocean crossing without being literal. A boat shape. Darkness. North.
- Maria's hand in Panel 09 should have visible circuit tattoos
- The composition notebook pages should be dense with handwriting — the reader doesn't need to read it, just feel its weight
