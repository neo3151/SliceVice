# EPISODE 23 PANEL BREAKDOWN: "CONDO CANYON (REDUX)" (12 PANELS)
**Date:** April 14, 1980 — 2 PM to Sunset
**Tone:** A pool deck in Hollywood. Two men, an afternoon, a photograph. The stakes arrive quietly through a legal pad.
**Color Key:** Afternoon gold light on turquoise pool water. The concrete towers of Condo Canyon bleached white by years of sun. Sol's guayabera pale blue. The legal pad yellow. The photograph warm amber. I-95 at sunset is the outro — impossible pink sky.

---

**Page 1 (The Drive / The Building)**
- **Panel 01:** (Wide/Highway) I-95 north, midday. The flat, meditative stretch past Hialeah and North Miami Beach — strip malls, exit signs, the highway almost empty in the off-peak hour. The LeMans sitting steady at 70, steering wheel dead center, engine pulling clean. The driver's posture relaxed for once.
- **Panel 02:** (Wide/Establishing) Hollywood, Florida. The Condo Canyon. A boulevard lined with identical fifteen-story concrete towers, sun-bleached facades, concrete balconies. Late afternoon light makes them look almost beautiful. A city of people who came here to stop running.
- **Panel 03:** (Interior/Lobby) The Fountainview lobby — terrazzo floors, 1971 time capsule. Gerald at the security desk, crossword in hand, waving the LeMans through without looking up. This is their entire relationship, visible in one panel.

**Page 2 (The Pool Deck)**
- **Panel 04:** (Wide/Pool Deck) The pool deck. Very blue water. Four people: two women reading paperbacks in lawn chairs, a man with a hat over his face asleep, Sol in a third chair with the afternoon paper — now folded in half and set on the small table with deliberate accessibility. A pitcher of lemonade. A plate covered with a dish towel.
- **Panel 05:** (Mid-shot/Eating) Axel eating at the poolside table. Sol watching the pool. The afternoon is quiet. The women with paperbacks in the background. This is the meal Sol made before Axel arrived — the dish towel is off, the food is visible, the plate is full.
- **Panel 06:** (Close-up/Legal Pad) Sol's legal pad on the table. Yellow ruled paper covered in columns of addresses and notations in cramped blue pen. Dade County property records. The handwriting is methodical, the margins annotated. Seven addresses. Five of them Drowned District coordinates.

**Page 3 (The Intel)**
- **Panel 07:** (Two-shot/Table) Axel and Sol at the table, the legal pad between them. Sol pointing to one of the addresses. Axel leaning in. The pool in background, the women still reading, utterly unaware. The stakes of the conversation invisible to everyone but these two.
- **Panel 08:** (Wide/Pool) The pool deck, wider. One of the women reading paperbacks has laughed at something — her head thrown back, bright and human. A completely ordinary sound in a conversation about Vanguard buying up the Drowned District. The contrast is the point.
- **Panel 09:** (Close-up/Photograph) The photograph — early 1960s amber color, Fontainebleau poolside bar. Two people: the woman laughing at something off-frame, the man (Sol, younger, all his hair) not looking at the camera. He is looking at her. The photo is small, worn at the edges, carried in a pocket a long time.

**Page 4 (The Dinner / The Drive)**
- **Panel 10:** (Table/Second Plate) Sol's hand refilling Axel's plate. Axel's plate already empty. Sol's expression: the focused satisfaction of a man feeding another person for the first time in years. No commentary. Just the action of the serving spoon.
- **Panel 11:** (Interior/Car) The LeMans passenger seat. Sol's legal pad flat on it. Seven addresses visible. Axel's hand on the wheel, the city coming back up over the horizon ahead — downtown Miami towers catching the last of the sunset gold, the Grid glowing.
- **Panel 12:** (Wide/Highway) I-95 south at sunset. The sky doing what it does — bands of pink, orange, bruised purple above the turnpike. The LeMans in the frame, moving south, radio on (visually indicated by Axel's body language — slightly different, slightly more open). The impossible sky.

**Visual Generation Notes:**
- The photograph in Panel 09 is crucial — Sol looking at Miriam, not at the camera. The warmth of early 60s color photography.
- Panel 10: the refilling of the plate is the emotional payoff. Keep it simple — the hand, the spoon, the plate.
- Panel 12 should be the most beautiful panel in Vol 3 — the sky earns it. This is the first time Axel turns the radio on in 58 days.
- The legal pad is always yellow, always on the table, always between them when the serious conversation happens
