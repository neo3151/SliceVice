# EPISODE 24 PANEL BREAKDOWN: "VERSAILLES (NIGHT SHIFT)" (10 PANELS)
**Date:** April 19, 1980 — 11 PM to 2 AM
**Tone:** A coffee window that has become something else. Thirty people watching the street. Two days before the boats land.
**Color Key:** Versailles neon — warm yellow and white against wet Calle Ocho black asphalt. The van cycles: blue, ordinary, no markings. Carmen's apron white. The cassette tape label handwritten: *MIXES — ABRIL 1980.* The left pocket has two things in it now.

---

**Page 1 (The Street)**
- **Panel 01:** (Driver's POV) The LeMans turning onto SW 8th Street. Through the windshield: the ventanita at Versailles in the distance, lit up, but wrong — too many people for midnight on a Saturday, the crowd geometry off. Thirty people where there should be five. Their backs to the wall.
- **Panel 02:** (Wide/Street Level) The ventanita exterior. The crowd visible — men in their twenties and thirties, some in clothes wrong for Miami (shoes too heavy, jackets too thick for April). They are not in a line. They are in clusters. They are watching the street. The neon of Versailles bleeds into the wet asphalt.

**Page 2 (The Window)**
- **Panel 03:** (Close-up/Counter) The ventanita counter. Carmen's hands moving — the focused economy of motion of thirty years. The colada already on the counter before Axel reaches it. The folded piece of paper beside it. Carmen's eyes track him through peripheral vision without turning her head.
- **Panel 04:** (Wide/Wall) Axel leaning against the exterior wall of Versailles, colada in hand. The van cycle: a blue van, no markings, pulls up across the street. Three people enter the restaurant main entrance. They emerge twelve minutes later with grocery bags. They get back in the van. Another van pulls up eight minutes later. Same thing. Axel watching.
- **Panel 05:** (Close-up/List) The folded paper open in Axel's hands. Carmen's handwriting: seven addresses in small precise script. Homestead. Kendall. Hialeah. Opa-locka. Coded notations beside each one. The paper is heavy-stock, ink fresh.

**Page 3 (The Break)**
- **Panel 06:** (Mid-shot/Hood) Carmen on her break, leaning against the LeMans hood. Cigarette in hand, looking at Calle Ocho. This is the only indication she considers Axel close enough to lean on his car. The street behind her: busier than it should be for midnight Saturday.
- **Panel 07:** (Two-shot/Hood) Axel and Carmen at the LeMans hood. She is not looking at him while she talks about the boats. He is not looking at her while he listens. Both of them watching Calle Ocho. The list in his hand, not yet pocketed.
- **Panel 08:** (Wide/Ventanita Redux) Another cycle at the window. The crowd thinned, replaced — older people now, two women speaking rapid Creole, leaning toward Carmen through the window. The kitchen steam, the espresso machine, the change passing, the folded papers passing. Carmen is the hub of something. This is not a coffee window tonight.

**Page 4 (The Tape)**
- **Panel 09:** (Close-up/Apron) Carmen's hand producing the cassette tape from her apron pocket. No case. The label visible in the neon light: *MIXES — ABRIL 1980* in marker. Holding it out. Simple transfer, no ceremony.
- **Panel 10:** (Wide/Street) Axel standing by the LeMans on Calle Ocho. Both objects visible: the list in one hand, the tape in the other. Versailles neon behind him — the restaurant, the old men at their dominoes through the window. He is looking down the length of the street. The city glowing amber and neon. He puts both things in the left jacket pocket — the recipe card is already there. Then he gets in the car.
