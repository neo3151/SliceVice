# EPISODE 2 SCRIPT: "12 KNOTS"

**Panel 01**
AXEL: (Caption) The dispatch ticket was practically melted into the dashboard. Smudged dot-matrix text. Only two words left: "12 KNOTS."

**Panel 02**
AXEL: (Caption) Spring Break '88. The I-95 corridor is a river of red brake lights and cheap beer. Bumper-to-bumper neon-pink convertibles filled with tourists who think they’re in paradise.

**Panel 03**
AXEL: (Caption) Fourteen minutes left on the delivery clock. If the crate is cold, my debt-timer resets. And in this economy, you don't survive a reset.
SPRING BREAKER: (Dialogue - Muffled) "HEY! MOVE IT, ASSHOLE!"

**Panel 04**
AXEL: (Caption) The target isn't on the map because it doesn't have an address. Gulf Smugglers. They cut through the Intercoastal at exactly 12 knots. 

**Panel 05**
AXEL: (Caption) I check the side-barrier. Down below, hidden in the pitch-black water, I see the matte-grey hull of a smuggler's yacht. Silent. Moving like a predator.

**Panel 06**
AXEL: (Caption) If I stay on the asphalt, the traffic traps me. I have to breach the guardrail.

**Panel 07**
AXEL: (Caption) I cut the wheel. The LeMans slams into a pink convertible, shoved it aside like a toy. Steel screams against fiberglass.

**Panel 08**
AXEL: (Caption) I bust through the construction barricade. Tearing through the muddy embankment. The V8 is roaring, fighting the swamp mud.

**Panel 09**
AXEL: (Caption) I see the 'Sunken Causeway'—a half-finished concrete overpass that ends in a seventy-foot drop into the ocean. It’s my only window.

**Panel 10**
AXEL: (Caption) I slam the pedal into the floorboards. Timing is everything in professional delivery. One second off and you're just another piece of scrap in the harbor.

**Panel 11**
AXEL: (Caption) The LeMans hits the edge of the concrete at eighty-five. Air.

**Panel 12**
AXEL: (Caption) For three seconds, we’re weightless. The LeMans is hanging over the black water, the smuggler yacht rushing underneath me.

**Panel 13**
AXEL: (Caption) I roll the window down. As we freefall, I heave the heavy metallic crate out into the rain. A perfect downward arc toward the wooden deck.

**Panel 14**
AXEL: (Caption) I hear the thud of success exactly as the LeMans nose-dives into the Atlantic. The water hits like a brick wall. This is a completed slip. Everything else is just details.
