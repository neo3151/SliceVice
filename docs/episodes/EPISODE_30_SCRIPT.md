# EPISODE 30: THE LEDGER RUN (MAY 18, 1980)

## SCENE 01: ELIO'S GARAGE
**[PANEL 1 - WIDE SHOT]**
**Visual:** Inside Elio's garage. The corrugated metal door is pulled all the way down and deadbolted. The only illumination is a single caged work-light hanging over the '78 LeMans. Outside, the muffled, heavy sound of distant sirens and the low rumble of a city tearing itself apart.
**AXEL (CAPTION):** May 18th. 2:00 AM. The Liberty City fires are spreading south. The sky over Overtown hasn't been dark for hours. 

**[PANEL 2 - MID SHOT]**
**Visual:** Jean-Pierre is sitting on an overturned milk crate, unspooling a massive reel of magnetic tape from the wooden box he brought. Elio is standing next to him, wiping his hands on a shop rag, staring at the tape with profound skepticism.
**JEAN-PIERRE (DIALOGUE):** "It's the Mesh-Archive. Eighteen months of Vanguard's undocumented property seizures. If they format the Grid tonight during the blackout, this is the only geographic memory left."
**ELIO (DIALOGUE):** "I fix transmissions. I don't underwrite real estate."

**[PANEL 3 - CLOSE UP]**
**Visual:** Axel leaning against the LeMans' hood. He looks exhausted but wired. The Nixie timer on the dashboard reflects off his eyes—it's glowing steady green, anchored.
**AXEL (CAPTION):** Vanguard doesn't let a crisis go to waste. A riot is just a distraction. A blackout is an opportunity to wipe the physical ledgers. And Sol Abramowitz is the only one who knows exactly which parcels they're erasing.

## SCENE 02: THE NORTHBOUND SLIPSTREAM
**[PANEL 4 - WIDE ACTION SHOT]**
**Visual:** The LeMans blasting north on an eerily empty I-95. A massive column of thick, oily black smoke rises from the city in the rearview mirror. No streetlights. The LeMans' high-beams cut a tunnel into the dark.
**AXEL (CAPTION):** I leave Jean-Pierre with Elio. The LeMans eats the north bound miles. Hollywood, Florida. The Condo Canyon. Sol is twenty miles clear of the fires, but he's standing right on top of the data Vanguard wants to burn.

**[PANEL 5 - DRIVER'S PERSPECTIVE]**
**Visual:** Looking over the steering wheel. The speedometer reads 85 MPH. The dashboard is quiet. The transmission hums smoothly.
**AXEL (CAPTION):** Elio's rebuild holds. The car doesn't stutter, doesn't complain. The city is breaking apart, but the machine I've built holds the line. Trust made mechanical.

**[PANEL 6 - EXTERIOR ESTABLISHING SHOT]**
**Visual:** The Fountainview Condominium in Hollywood. The brutalist concrete tower looks imposing against the night sky. But there are three matte-black Vanguard Repo-SUVs parked directly on the front lawn, crushing the manicured landscaping.
**AXEL (CAPTION):** The Fountainview. Vance didn't wait for the sun to come up. His ghost-roster sweepers are already here.

## SCENE 03: THE EIGHTEENTH FLOOR
**[PANEL 7 - TIGHT SHOT]**
**Visual:** Axel steps out of the elevator on the 18th floor. The hallway lights are steady. The door to apartment 1804 (Sol's) is wide open. Two men in crisp corporate suits stand just inside the doorway, holding heavy clipboards and looking frustrated.
**AXEL (CAPTION):** There are no breached doors. No tactical teams. Vanguard's true sweepers don't wear helmets. They wear suits and carry audit forms.

**[PANEL 8 - MID SHOT]**
**Visual:** Inside Sol's apartment. The men in suits are standing awkwardly near the entrance. Sol Abramowitz is sitting calmly in his armchair. A vintage .38 revolver rests casually on his lap. He hasn't fired it, but its quiet weight is demanding the room's absolute respect.
**SOL (DIALOGUE):** "I told you, gentlemen. The property records were shipped to Tallahassee on Tuesday. Corporate never believes the mail service is efficient."

**[PANEL 9 - WIDE SHOT]**
**Visual:** Axel enters the apartment, stepping up behind the two distressed Vanguard auditors. He doesn't draw a weapon or throw a punch. He just stands there, blocking the exit, looking like a very large, immovable problem.
**AXEL (CAPTION):** The corporate auditors are trained to handle default algorithms and panic. They aren't trained to handle an armed retiree and a courier who doesn't care about their clipboards.

**[PANEL 10 - CLOSE UP]**
**Visual:** One of the suits nervously twitches his hand toward his inner jacket pocket—maybe for a radio, maybe for a sidearm. But Sol casually cocks the hammer of the .38 revolver. The loud, mechanical *click* freezes both suits instantly.
**SOL (DIALOGUE):** "My Miriam hated loud noises. Leave your hand empty, young man."

**[PANEL 11 - WIDE SHOT]**
**Visual:** Sol calmly packs his single leather suitcase while Axel holds the door, watching the Vanguard suits slowly back out into the hallway in defeat. Through the 18th-floor panoramic window, the massive glow of a burning Miami is visible on the southern horizon.
**AXEL (DIALOGUE):** "We need to go, Sol. The blackout is creeping north. Vanguard is wiping the physical district ledgers tonight."
**SOL (DIALOGUE):** "Let them wipe the paper. I memorized the parcel numbers."

**[PANEL 12 - FINAL ESTABLISHING SHOT]**
**Visual:** The LeMans tearing away from the Fountainview, Sol sitting comfortably in the passenger seat with his leather suitcase on his lap. The Vanguard SUVs remain parked, useless. The car heads back toward the glow of Miami.
**AXEL (CAPTION):** I move the anchors. Carmen to Hialeah. Jean-Pierre to SW 8th. Sol is with me. The city belongs to fire tonight, but the Grid belongs to whoever remembers the layout in the morning.
