# EPISODE 31 PANEL BREAKDOWN: "THE MORNING AFTER" (8 PANELS)
**Date:** May 18, 1980 — 6 AM to 9 AM
**Tone:** Four people in a garage built for one. The city outside still smoking. The work continuing anyway.
**Color Key:** Dawn light — pink, then pale gold — coming through the half-open roll-up door. The smoke column to the north: grey-white now, thinning but still there. Sol's leather suitcase: brown, worn, good quality once. The leather notebook: small, dark, strap closure. The left jacket pocket receiving it.

---

**Page 1 (The Garage at Dawn)**
- **Panel 01:** (Wide/Interior) Elio's garage at 6 AM. Dawn light through the roll-up door across the concrete floor. Sol asleep in the office chair, suitcase on his lap, hand resting on top. Jean-Pierre at the workbench, reel-to-reel running, legal pad covered in notations. Elio's boots visible under the Cutlass — he never stopped.
- **Panel 02:** (Close-up/Reel-to-Reel) The reel-to-reel on the workbench running beside the boat engine. Jean-Pierre's legal pad next to it — dense with coordinates and parcel notations, two different pen colors. The Mesh archive verifying itself.

**Page 2 (The Street)**
- **Panel 03:** (Exterior/Hood) Axel sitting on the LeMans hood on SW 8th Street, early morning coffee. The street quiet in the post-emergency way — not peaceful, emptied. A woman walking fast with grocery bags. A man on his porch looking north. The smoke column visible on the horizon, thinning.
- **Panel 04:** (Two-shot/Bumper) Sol on the LeMans bumper, leather suitcase on his knees, open. Inside: the small leather notebook, strap closure, not the legal pad. Axel beside him. The street behind them. Sol's finger tracing a parcel number without looking at it — he has it memorized.

**Page 3 (The Notebook)**
- **Panel 05:** (Close-up/Notebook) Sol's leather notebook open on his knees. Dense blue pen — parcel numbers, addresses, shell company names, acquisition dates. Months of work. The strap closure visible at the edge. His hand holding it out toward Axel.
- **Panel 06:** (Close-up/Phone) The wall phone by the parts cabinet. Axel holding the receiver. The coiled cord. His expression: listening. Carmen's voice on the other end — not visible, present.

**Page 4 (The Garage Settling)**
- **Panel 07:** (Wide/Workbench) Jean-Pierre moving the reel-to-reel off the workbench. Sol closing the notebook. Elio emerging from under the Cutlass — shop rag, one finger at a time, the familiar ritual. The four of them in the garage: the moment before Elio speaks.
- **Panel 08:** (Close-up/Pocket) Axel's left jacket pocket receiving Sol's notebook. The pocket already holding: the recipe card, the address list. Now the notebook. The pocket full — everything accumulated since March in one place.
