# EPISODE 32: THE AUDIT (MAY 19, 1980)

## SCENE 01: THE LEGER AND THE OIL
**[PANEL 1 - WIDE SHOT]**
**Visual:** The interior of Elio's garage, morning. Heavy, dusty shafts of sunlight slice through the rusted vents of the corrugated metal roll-up door. The air is thick with the scent of ozone, exhaust, and stale Cuban coffee. Elio, Axel, Jean-Pierre, and Sol look hollowed-out and exhausted, clustered around a grease-stained workbench under a single caged work-light.
**AXEL (CAPTION):** May 19th. 9:00 AM. The fires in Liberty City have burned themselves down to a heavy grid of ash. We stopped running at dawn.

**[PANEL 2 - CLOSE UP]**
**Visual:** Tight focus on the workbench texture. Sol's pristine, leather-bound notebook lies open flat, its meticulously hand-written blue ink numbers stark against a backdrop of greasy wrenches, brass fittings, and a discarded oil filter.
**SOL (DIALOGUE):** "Look at the time codes. They didn't buy these lots after the fires started. The shell companies were incorporated three weeks ago."
**AXEL (CAPTION):** The adrenaline is gone. Now there is only the math.

**[PANEL 3 - MID SHOT]**
**Visual:** Jean-Pierre expertly threading the massive, heavy magnetic tape of the Mesh Archive into a customized, scavenged reel-to-reel deck hooked up to a cathode-ray oscilloscope. The screen glows with a faint green sine wave.
**JEAN-PIERRE (DIALOGUE):** "The physical deeds are gone. Vanguard municipal sweepers formatted the county mainframe during the blackout. But we have the analog echoes. The Mesh caught every data transmission."
**AXEL (CAPTION):** Eighteen months of Vanguard's breathing, caught on magnetic rust.

**[PANEL 4 - DRIVER'S PERSPECTIVE / WIDE]**
**Visual:** Axel leaning against the reinforced front grille of the '78 LeMans, looking at the exhausted crew. The deep shadows of the garage make the chrome trim of the car look dull.
**AXEL (CAPTION):** The LeMans smells like smoke and survival. Elio's transmission held the line. But surviving the night isn't the same as knowing what to do with the morning.
**AXEL (DIALOGUE):** "So we have a map of buildings that Vanguard technically doesn't own yet. What do we do with it?"

**[PANEL 5 - LOW ANGLE]**
**Visual:** Sol adjusting his glasses, looking up from the notebook. He is out of his element in a chop-shop, but his intellect is sharp, cold, and assessing.
**SOL (DIALOGUE):** "They need these physical locations to anchor their Grid-Spikes perfectly across the county. If we disrupt the anchor points before they finalize the hardware installation..."
**ELIO (DIALOGUE):** "...The network collapses underneath them."

## SCENE 02: THE SILENT COUNTERMEASURE
**[PANEL 6 - CLOSE UP]**
**Visual:** Sol's hands turning a page in his notebook. He taps a thick, gnarled finger on a specific address in Overtown.
**SOL (DIALOGUE):** "It starts here. Parcel 402 on NW 3rd Avenue. An old textile warehouse. If they anchor there, they control the mid-city routing."

**[PANEL 7 - MID SHOT]**
**Visual:** Elio standing near the garage's wall-mounted rotary phone. He has the receiver pressed to his ear, listening. Through the half-open garage door behind him, the distant, rhythmic thud of a demolition wrecking ball can be heard echoing across the city blocks.
**ELIO (DIALOGUE):** "That was Ricky. He's at the salvage yard. Says city crews just pulled up to a textile warehouse on NW 3rd Avenue with bulldozers and emergency demolition permits."

**[PANEL 8 - TIGHT SHOT]**
**Visual:** Axel's face, partially obscured by the heavy shadows of the garage. He looks down at the greasy floor. The weight of the situation settling over him heavily.
**AXEL (CAPTION):** Vance isn't looking for the notebook anymore. He isn't sending heavily armed squads to kick down doors.
**AXEL (CAPTION):** He's just erasing the things the notebook describes. We sit in a hot garage holding a list of places that are ceasing to exist.
