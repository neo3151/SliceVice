# EPISODE 37: THE SCATTERED DECK (MAY 22, 1980)

## SCENE 01: ELIO'S GARAGE / 8:15 AM

**[PANEL 1 - WIDE / GARAGE, MORNING]**
**Visual:** Elio's garage, early. The tape reels from the Mutiny are in a cardboard box on the workbench beside the routing cards. JP is running a wire from his mesh transmitter to a secondary unit — a frequency monitor, analog dial face. Sol has two documents spread on the workbench: the routing card and what appears to be a city electrical inspection form, blank, with a Dade County seal. Axel leaning against the car.
**AXEL (CAPTION):** Vanguard knows about the Mutiny. They pulled the service contract the same afternoon we cleared the utility room. Two locations left before the summer window closes: the Fontainebleau and The Forge.
**AXEL (CAPTION):** We can't go in the same way twice.

**[PANEL 2 - TWO-SHOT / JP AND ELIO]**
**Visual:** JP pointing at the frequency monitor, explaining something. Elio listening with his arms crossed, expression neutral — he's not technical but he's following.
**JEAN-PIERRE (DIALOGUE):** "If I park the van on Collins Avenue and run the transmitter at full output, I can flood the Fontainebleau's local band. The extractor starts fighting interference. It won't trip an alarm — it'll just look like a bad signal day."
**ELIO (DIALOGUE):** "How long does that buy me?"
**JEAN-PIERRE (DIALOGUE):** "Twenty minutes before someone starts making phone calls."

---

## SCENE 02: THE FONTAINEBLEAU, MIAMI BEACH / 11:30 AM

**[PANEL 3 - SPLIT PANEL / JP'S VAN AND THE FONTAINEBLEAU EXTERIOR]**
**Visual:** Left half: JP's van parked on Collins Avenue, the transmitter running — a needle on the frequency monitor swinging into the red. Right half: the Fontainebleau's service entrance, Elio in a maintenance uniform walking through with a toolbox. A bellman holds the door without looking at him.
**AXEL (CAPTION):** JP holds the frequency. Elio holds the toolbox. The Fontainebleau runs on the assumption that anyone in a uniform is supposed to be there.

**[PANEL 4 - INTERIOR / FONTAINEBLEAU UTILITY CORRIDOR]**
**Visual:** Elio in a narrow corridor, utility lighting, the olive-green hardware box on the wall ahead of him. He works the padlock with a pick — not glamorous, just methodical. The toolbox open at his feet.
**AXEL (CAPTION):** Elio has been opening things that don't belong to him since before I learned to drive. He doesn't rush.

---

## SCENE 03: THE FORGE, MIAMI / 11:30 AM

**[PANEL 5 - EXTERIOR / THE FORGE RESTAURANT]**
**Visual:** The Forge on Arthur Godfrey Road, mid-morning. A service entrance on the side street. Two men in business suits are at the utility box mounted to the exterior wall — one holds a clipboard, the other is working the padlock. They have the look of people who are supposed to be there: unhurried, institutional. Axel and Reya approaching from down the block, both carrying document folders.
**AXEL (CAPTION):** Vanguard moved fast. Two contractors at the box, legitimate service paperwork, and nobody inside the restaurant knows who they work for.

**[PANEL 6 - MID / AXEL AND REYA AT THE BOX]**
**Visual:** Axel and Reya arriving at the utility box. Axel holding out a document toward the nearest Vanguard contractor — the Dade County inspection form, filled out, sealed. The contractor taking it, reading it. His expression is careful, not hostile.
**REYA (DIALOGUE):** "County electrical coordination. We have a standing audit flag on this tap. Routine."
**VANGUARD CONTRACTOR (DIALOGUE):** "We have a service contract on this unit."
**AXEL (DIALOGUE):** "Then you can stand here while we complete the inspection. That's how this works."

**[PANEL 7 - CLOSE / THE CONTRACTOR ON HIS PHONE]**
**Visual:** The contractor a few steps away, phone to his ear, speaking quietly. His back to Axel. Axel at the utility box, working quickly. Reya watching the contractor, her folder open in her hands — performing the audit.
**AXEL (CAPTION):** He's calling it in. We have the time it takes Vanguard's office to call back and tell him to hold his ground. Maybe four minutes.

**[PANEL 8 - MID / REYA'S EXIT]**
**Visual:** Axel and Reya walking away from The Forge, the hardware in the document folder under Reya's arm. Half a block away, a second Vanguard operative — a woman in a gray blazer, on foot — stops on the sidewalk and looks directly at Reya's face for two full seconds before Reya turns away. The operative is already reaching for something in her jacket pocket.
**AXEL (CAPTION):** Three seconds. Long enough.

---

## SCENE 04: THE LEMONS, MOVING / 12:10 PM

**[PANEL 9 - INTERIOR / LEMONS]**
**Visual:** Axel driving. Reya in the passenger seat, the hardware on her lap, not looking at it. The city moving past. Axel's eyes on the mirror.
**AXEL (CAPTION):** Two for two. The Fontainebleau dark, The Forge dark. One venue left on the card. One venue nobody's been able to identify.
**REYA (DIALOGUE):** "She got a look at me."
**AXEL (DIALOGUE):** "I know."
**REYA (DIALOGUE):** "Then you know what that means for the Cinema Rouge."

**[END OF EPISODE]**
