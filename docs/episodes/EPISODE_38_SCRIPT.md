# EPISODE 38: THE GHOST CLUB (MAY 23, 1980)

## SCENE 01: ELIO'S GARAGE / 9:00 AM

**[PANEL 1 - CLOSE / THE FINAL ROUTING CARD]**
**Visual:** The Biscayne Entertainment routing card on the workbench under a work lamp. JP's finger tracing the last entry: a sector code without a street address — just a set of coordinates in the margin, handwritten smaller than the rest. Beside the coordinates, a single word in the same bureaucratic black marker: STILTS.
**JEAN-PIERRE (DIALOGUE):** "It's not a street address. It's a water coordinate. Biscayne Bay — five miles offshore."
**SOL (DIALOGUE):** "Stiltsville."
**AXEL (CAPTION):** Stiltsville. A dozen houses on stilts built on sandbars in the middle of the bay. Most of them abandoned or close to it. Whatever's out there, it's not on any permit Vanguard filed with the city.

---

## SCENE 02: BISCAYNE BAY / 2:14 PM

**[PANEL 2 - WIDE / THE BAY, OPEN WATER]**
**Visual:** A center-console boat running south across flat, bright water. Axel at the wheel, one hand on the throttle. The Miami skyline receding behind them, low and hazy. No other vessels nearby. The water color shifting from green to blue-gray as depth increases.
**AXEL (CAPTION):** Manny's boat. Borrowed. He didn't ask why and I didn't explain. He handed me the keys and said bring it back with fuel.

**[PANEL 3 - WIDE / STILTSVILLE APPROACH]**
**Visual:** A cluster of structures on the horizon — the Stiltsville houses on their pilings, scattered across the sandbar flats. Most look weathered and dark. One structure ahead has what might be a light on inside — difficult to tell in the afternoon glare. Axel slowing the boat.
**AXEL (CAPTION):** Seven structures still standing. Most of them empty for years. The coordinates put us at the southernmost one.

**[PANEL 4 - MID / DOCKING AT THE STRUCTURE]**
**Visual:** Axel tying the boat to a weathered piling. The structure above: a low wooden building on stilts, wide deck, once a gathering place of some kind — a bar rail still bolted to the deck railing, rusted. A door standing half-open. No light from inside.
**AXEL (CAPTION):** The lights were off before we got close enough to see them go off. Or they were already off. Either way.

---

## SCENE 03: INTERIOR / THE GHOST CLUB

**[PANEL 5 - INTERIOR WIDE / THE EMPTY ROOM]**
**Visual:** Inside. A single room, low ceiling, salt-warped wood. Recently occupied: folding tables still set up, an overturned glass, a deck of cards on the floor. On the far wall, an olive-green utility box — identical to the others, door already open, the routing card slot empty. They packed the hardware. Left everything else.
**AXEL (CAPTION):** They knew we were coming. They had time to pull the hardware and leave the furniture.

**[PANEL 6 - CLOSE / THE WALL-MOUNTED PHONE]**
**Visual:** A rotary telephone mounted to the wall beside the empty utility box. Black Bakelite, institutional, the kind that's been on that wall for fifteen years. It begins to ring. The sound fills the empty room.

**[PANEL 7 - MID / AXEL PICKING UP THE PHONE]**
**Visual:** Axel lifting the handset. His expression is flat. Outside the window behind him, the open bay, the boat, nothing else.

**[PANEL 8 - CLOSE / AXEL'S FACE, LISTENING]**
**Visual:** Axel holding the receiver. His eyes still. Whatever he's hearing, he's processing it the way he processes everything — as information, not as threat.
**VANCE (PHONE):** "The Fontainebleau. The Forge. Now this. You've been thorough, Mr. Díaz. I want you to know that I consider it a professional courtesy that you've identified our anchor points so efficiently. It saved us the trouble of a full grid audit."
**VANCE (PHONE):** "We know where the garage is. We've known for three days."

**[PANEL 9 - WIDE / THE EMPTY ROOM, AXEL AT THE PHONE]**
**Visual:** Wide shot. Axel alone in the empty stilt house, the phone to his ear, the open utility box on the wall, the cards on the floor. Through the window, the flat water of the bay. He's already thinking about the boat, the motor, the time it takes to cross five miles of open water.
**AXEL (CAPTION):** He hangs up first. I'm already moving.

**[END OF EPISODE]**
