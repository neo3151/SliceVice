# EPISODE 39: THE FARADAY CAGE (MAY 23, 1980)

## SCENE 01: BISCAYNE BAY / 2:31 PM

**[PANEL 1 - WIDE / THE BOAT, OPEN THROTTLE]**
**Visual:** The center-console running hard back toward Miami, bow up, engine noise implied in the spray. The skyline growing ahead. Axel at the wheel, leaning into it. The motor running at a pitch it wasn't built to sustain.
**AXEL (CAPTION):** Five miles. The motor burns at full throttle in about forty minutes. I need to make it in twenty.

---

## SCENE 02: ELIO'S GARAGE / 2:19 PM (CONCURRENT)

**[PANEL 2 - INTERIOR / GARAGE — JP AT THE WORKBENCH]**
**Visual:** JP at the workbench, frequency monitor in front of him, needle jumping. His mesh transmitter is live, receiving signals it shouldn't be receiving. Sol beside him, watching the needle. The Mutiny tape reels and routing hardware are on the shelf behind them.
**JEAN-PIERRE (DIALOGUE):** "Someone's pinging our transmitter frequencies. Systematic sweep — NW corridor, sector by sector."
**SOL (DIALOGUE):** "Vanguard."
**JEAN-PIERRE (DIALOGUE):** "They have the routing card data from the Stiltsville unit. They're using it to triangulate us."

**[PANEL 3 - MID / ELIO FROM THE BACK OF THE GARAGE]**
**Visual:** Elio emerging from the rear of the garage bay, already carrying a roll of copper mesh — the kind used for automotive shielding projects, heavy gauge, on a wooden spool. He sets it on the workbench without being asked.
**ELIO (DIALOGUE):** "I have three rolls. Was for the Cutlass."
**JEAN-PIERRE (DIALOGUE):** "That'll do it."

**[PANEL 4 - CLOSE / HANDS WRAPPING TAPE REELS IN COPPER MESH]**
**Visual:** JP's hands and Sol's hands working together — unrolling copper mesh, cutting it with tin snips, wrapping the Mutiny tape reels and the stack of routing cards. The mesh is crude and physical. The wrapped reels look like something salvaged, not something protected. That's correct.
**AXEL (CAPTION):** JP wraps the archive. Sol cuts the mesh. The frequency monitor needle drops back toward center.

---

## SCENE 03: OVERTOWN, ONE BLOCK FROM THE GARAGE / 3:08 PM

**[PANEL 5 - EXTERIOR / THE BLOCK]**
**Visual:** Axel on foot, having left the boat at the marina and come in on the street. He stops half a block from the garage. The block is wrong: a city truck — Dade County lettering on the door — parked facing the wrong direction. A man with a clipboard on the corner, not moving, watching the garage building. Across the street, a second truck. The demolition permit on the windshield is visible from here.
**AXEL (CAPTION):** Not armed men. Clipboard men. Which is how Vanguard works — they send paper before they send crews, and the paper is the threat.

---

## SCENE 04: INSIDE THE GARAGE / 3:11 PM

**[PANEL 6 - INTERIOR / AXEL ENTERS]**
**Visual:** Axel coming in through the side door. The garage is dim, the roll door down. JP at the frequency monitor — the needle is calm now, the shielding holding. Sol is at the workbench phone, speaking quietly, her address book open beside it.
**AXEL (DIALOGUE):** "How long before they serve the permit?"
**ELIO (DIALOGUE):** "Clipboard man's been there forty minutes. They're waiting for a second signature."
**SOL (DIALOGUE):** "Give me ten minutes."

**[PANEL 7 - CLOSE / SOL ON THE PHONE]**
**Visual:** Sol on the phone, her finger running down a column of handwritten entries in her address book. She's not rushing. She's working her way through a chain of people she knows, each one a step closer to someone specific.
**AXEL (CAPTION):** Sol has been building her network since before Vanguard existed as a company. She knows who runs what in this city. She knows who at Radio Maria has been waiting for exactly this kind of material.

**[PANEL 8 - WIDE / OUTSIDE THE GARAGE — THE CREWS PULLING BACK]**
**Visual:** Through the narrow gap at the bottom of the roll door — seen from inside the garage, ground level — the feet of the clipboard man on the sidewalk. Then a sound from his direction: a portable radio crackling. Then his feet turn and walk away. The city truck's engine starts. Through the gap, the truck pulling away from the curb.
**AXEL (CAPTION):** Radio Maria broadcasts at 1550 AM. The Mutiny tape reels have forty-three hours of recorded calls. Sol's contact runs the first four minutes — two commissioners and a federal contract number — live on the air at 3:19 PM. Vanguard's crews are off the block by 3:24.

---

## SCENE 05: THE GARAGE, AFTER / 3:30 PM

**[PANEL 9 - WIDE / ALL FOUR, THE GARAGE]**
**Visual:** The four of them. The roll door open now, late afternoon light. The wrapped archive on the shelf. The frequency monitor dark, powered down. Nobody celebrating. Elio with the copper mesh spool in his hands, already thinking about putting it back. Sol closing her address book.
**AXEL (CAPTION):** The Biscayne Entertainment Band is dark. Four sectors. Four hardware units. Forty-three hours of tape that Vanguard will spend the next month trying to contain.
**AXEL (CAPTION):** And Sol's diagram from EP34 still on the workbench. The circles and lines. Every underground parcel, every informal economy, every off-book ledger this city runs on. Vanguard wasn't just anchoring a grid. They were mapping the architecture of everything that exists outside their system, so they could own that too.

**[END OF EPISODE — END OF VOLUME 05]**
