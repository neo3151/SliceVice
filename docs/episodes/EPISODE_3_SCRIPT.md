# EPISODE 3 SCRIPT: "THE AUDIT"

**Panel 01**
AXEL: (Caption) I stood on the edge of the Sunken Causeway, dripping wet. Below, the smuggler yacht was a fading shadow in the dark. The job was done.

**Panel 02**
AXEL: (Caption) The LeMans was half-submerged in the tidal mud below the bridge piling. I climbed down, hauled her out with the winch. The engine coughed, spitting out salt water and grit.

**Panel 03**
AXEL: (Caption) The radio crackled. No dispatch. Just a cold, recorded voice. "Cutter. Your previous slip has been flagged for a manual audit. Remain at your current coordinates for retrieval."

**Panel 04**
AXEL: (Caption) Corporate doesn't send accountants for an audit. They send the 'Vanguard Recovery'—ex-special forces with a license to liquidate.

**Panel 05**
AXEL: (Caption) Two black Vanguard SUVs tore up the embankment, blocking the only road out of the Glades. Pure white searchlights pinned me against the mangroves.

**Panel 06**
AXEL: (Caption) I slammed the LeMans into gear. The V8 was rough, misfiring, but she still had enough heart for one more sprint.

**Panel 07**
AXEL: (Caption) They fired a tear-gas canister. It shattered against the LeMans' rear window, filling the cabin with a white, choking haze.

**Panel 08**
AXEL: (Caption) I hit the emergency nitro-toggle. The tank was nearly empty, just enough pressurized gas for a five-second burst.

**Panel 09**
AXEL: (Caption) I didn't run. I turned the LeMans around and aimed the high-beams directly at the lead SUV. If they wanted the audit, they were going to have to sign for it.

**Panel 10**
AXEL: (Caption) We charged. A two-ton game of chicken on a narrow gravel road. 

**Panel 11**
AXEL: (Caption) At the last second, I yanked the E-brake. The LeMans performed a violent, mud-slinging pivot, slamming her reinforced rear bumper into the lead SUV's front-wheel well.

**Panel 12**
AXEL: (Caption) The SUV’s axle snapped like a dry twig. It rolled, treading mud and flipping into the swamp-drainage ditch.

**Panel 13**
AXEL: (Caption) The second SUV braked hard to avoid the wreck. That was my window. I hit the back-roads, the smell of burning rubber and swamp rot following me.

**Panel 14**
AXEL: (Caption) The radio finally cleared. Riley's voice, sounding rattled. "Cutter? You still on the grid? The Board is pissed. Get to the safehouse. Now."
