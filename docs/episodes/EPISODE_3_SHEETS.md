# EPISODE 3 PANEL BREAKDOWN (14 PANELS)

**Page 1 (The Glitch)**
- **Panel 01:** (Establishing) Axel stands at the edge of the brutalist concrete dead-end bridge, looking down into the pouring rain. Below, the massive Cartel yacht speeds away into the dark.
- **Panel 02:** (Close-up) Inside the LeMans, a tight shot of the central console Nixie timer. It is completely frozen on [00:00]. It is not flashing. It's radiating a sickly, solid green.
- **Panel 03:** (Insert) The massive, blocky dispatch radio on the dash. It's smoking slightly, the green LEDs flashing uncontrollably with raw static.

**Page 2 (The Auditor)**
- **Panel 04:** (Wide) The bridge in the storm. Suddenly, four blindingly bright, pure white searchlights pierce through the fog, aimed directly at the LeMans from further down the bridge.
- **Panel 05:** (Reveal) A massive, terrifying "Audit Drone." An armored 1980s street-sweeper, heavily modified with reinforced steel plates and glowing green "INTERNAL AUDIT" decals.
- **Panel 06:** (Action) Axel slamming the gear shifter into reverse. His face is illuminated only by the frantic green light of the broken timer.
- **Panel 07:** (Action) The drone fires a massive pressurized cannon. Thick, glowing pink sludge splatters violently against the driver's side window of the LeMans.

**Page 3 (Desperation)**
- **Panel 08:** (Interior) Axel looks over at the passenger seat. The massive Pizza Safe is empty and still glowing violently with residual 'Neon Spice' magic.
- **Panel 09:** (Action) Axel violently rips the glowing heating elements directly out of the Pizza Safe. Sparks fly everywhere. He holds them like glowing brass knuckles.
- **Panel 10:** (Wide) The LeMans reverses at breakneck speed right toward the edge of the broken bridge, the Auditor drone advancing relentlessly.
- **Panel 11:** (Climax setup) The LeMans' rear wheels hang perfectly over the 70-foot drop. The car tilts dangerously upward. Axel violently kicks the driver's side door open.

**Page 4 (Resolution)**
- **Panel 12:** (Action) Axel leaps from the tilted car directly onto the advancing Drone. He slams the glowing, sparking Pizza Safe coils directly into its sensor array.
- **Panel 13:** (Explosion) A spectacular, blinding explosion. The Drone violently detonates in a massive burst of green sparks and pink foam.
- **Panel 14:** (Resolution) Axel walks back to the precarious LeMans, smoking heavily, brushing pink foam off his jacket. The radio finally crackles back to life. End of Episode 3.
