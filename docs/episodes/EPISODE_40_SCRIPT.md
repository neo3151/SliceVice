# EPISODE 40: DEAD AIR (MAY 25, 1980)

## SCENE 01: ELIO'S GARAGE / 6:14 PM

**[PANEL 1 - WIDE / GARAGE, LATE AFTERNOON]**
**Visual:** Elio's garage, the roll door half-open. Late afternoon light cutting low across the floor in amber slabs. JP at the workbench with salvaged components spread out — a disassembled frequency relay, wire leads, a soldering iron cooling in its stand. No urgency in his posture. Sol at the far end of the bench, her ledger open, a colada in a plastic cup going warm beside her, a folded newspaper open to the real estate listings. Elio's legs visible under the Cutlass — finally working on it for himself. Nobody talking.
**AXEL (CAPTION):** May 25th. Five days since Radio Maria. Vanguard pulled their crews off the block and nobody has sent anything since — not a letter, not a contractor, not a clipboard man on a corner.
**AXEL (CAPTION):** The quiet feels like a held breath.

**[PANEL 2 - CLOSE / JP'S HANDS ON THE RELAY COMPONENTS]**
**Visual:** JP's hands, careful and unhurried, separating copper leads with a small pick. Not fixing anything that needs fixing. Just understanding how something was built.
**JEAN-PIERRE (DIALOGUE):** "This relay is 1971. Whoever built it knew what they were doing."
**AXEL (DIALOGUE):** "What does it do?"
**JEAN-PIERRE (DIALOGUE):** "Nothing anymore. That's not the point."

**[PANEL 3 - CLOSE / SOL AND HER LEDGER]**
**Visual:** Sol's finger running down a column in the newspaper — real estate transfer listings, small print. Her pen making a mark beside one entry. The colada untouched.
**SOL (DIALOGUE):** "Vanguard filed three new parcel transfers in Liberty City this week. Quiet ones. No demolition permits attached."
**AXEL (DIALOGUE):** "That's not nothing."
**SOL (DIALOGUE):** "No. But it's slow. They're regrouping."

## SCENE 02: THE DOCK BEHIND THE GARAGE / 7:40 PM

**[PANEL 4 - WIDE / EXTERIOR DOCK]**
**Visual:** A narrow concrete dock at the back of the garage property, overlooking a drainage canal. The city skyline visible in the distance, going orange and pink. Axel sitting on the edge of the dock, feet hanging, a joint burning between two fingers. The smoke rising straight up — no wind tonight. His jacket off, shirt untucked, the first time in weeks he looks like someone who isn't about to leave.
**AXEL (CAPTION):** The Grid hums in the back of my skull even when nothing's running. JP says that's the analog frequency bleed from the mesh transmitter. I think it's just what happens when you spend five months listening for things that are trying to stay quiet.
**AXEL (CAPTION):** The weed slows it down. Not off. Just slower.

**[PANEL 5 - MID / THE DOCK, REYA ARRIVES]**
**Visual:** Reya coming through the side gate, no knock, no announcement. She's not dressed for a mission — she's just here. She sits down on the dock beside Axel without asking. He doesn't look at her. She doesn't explain herself. The joint passes.
**REYA (DIALOGUE):** "The Cinema Rouge is going dark for two weeks. Marisol is re-threading the projectors."
**AXEL (DIALOGUE):** "You came to tell me that?"
**REYA (DIALOGUE):** "I came because I didn't want to be in my apartment."
**AXEL (CAPTION):** That's a reason I understand.

---

## SCENE 03: THE LEMONS, PARKED / 11:30 PM

**[PANEL 6 - INTERIOR / LEMONS AT NIGHT]**
**Visual:** Axel in the driver's seat, parked on a quiet block in Overtown. Engine off. The radio on low — something slow, a late-night set. The city outside the windshield doing its own thing without him in it for once. The joint is done. He's just sitting.
**AXEL (CAPTION):** JP mentioned a salvage yard in Hialeah — swamp-edge, been there since the fifties. Has a 1974 alternator housing that matches the LeMans's secondary mount. I said I'd run out there this week.

**[PANEL 7 - CLOSE / AXEL'S HAND ON THE STEERING WHEEL, NOT DRIVING]**
**Visual:** His hand resting loose on the wheel, not gripping it. The radio dial glowing amber. Outside the window, a dog barks somewhere three blocks over and goes quiet.
**AXEL (CAPTION):** No particular reason to go tonight. No particular reason to wait.

**[END OF EPISODE]**
