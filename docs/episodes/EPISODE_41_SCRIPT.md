# EPISODE 41: THE HIALEAH YARD (JUNE 2, 1980)

## SCENE 01: SWAMP-EDGE MECHANIC YARD, HIALEAH / 10:15 AM

**[PANEL 1 - WIDE / THE YARD ESTABLISHING]**
**Visual:** A salvage yard at the edge of Hialeah, where the city gives up and the sawgrass starts. Rusted chassis stacked in rows, a canal running along the back fence, cattails at the water's edge. A tin-roofed office shack. The air visibly thick — humidity and motor oil and the particular green smell of standing water nearby. The LeMans parked at the gate. Axel walking in.
**AXEL (CAPTION):** June 2nd. The Hialeah yard has been here since before the Korean War. The owner, a man named Gustavo, knows where every part is by memory and doesn't write anything down.

**[PANEL 2 - MID / AXEL AND GUSTAVO AT THE PARTS SHELF]**
**Visual:** Gustavo — sixties, stained undershirt, methodical — pulling a 1974 alternator housing off a shelf in the back of the shack and handing it over. Axel checking the mount alignment. Routine transaction.
**GUSTAVO (DIALOGUE):** "Your friend called ahead. I had it pulled already."
**AXEL (DIALOGUE):** "JP. Yeah."
**GUSTAVO (DIALOGUE):** "You want it wrapped or you'll carry it loose?"
**AXEL (DIALOGUE):** "Loose is fine."

---

## SCENE 02: THE YARD, NEAR THE CANAL FENCE / 10:28 AM

**[PANEL 3 - WIDE / AXEL CROSSING THE YARD TO THE CAR]**
**Visual:** Axel walking back through the yard, alternator housing under his arm. He slows. Near the canal fence, at the far edge of the property in a strip of shade between two stacked chassis — a husky. Female, about three years old, full coat despite the heat. She's not cowering, not approaching. Just standing in the shade watching him cross the yard. Blue eyes. Her coat is intact, well-kept enough that someone was brushing it not long ago.
**AXEL (CAPTION):** She's just standing there. Not hungry-looking. Not afraid. She's watching me the way you watch traffic — present, patient, not asking anything.

**[PANEL 4 - MID / AXEL CROUCHING DOWN]**
**Visual:** Axel crouched in front of her, the alternator housing set on the ground beside him. He's checking for a collar — running a hand gently around her neck. Nothing. No tag, no mark, no worn fur where a collar used to sit. But her coat is clean along the back where someone's hands have been, repeatedly, over time.
**AXEL (CAPTION):** No collar. But she's been brushed. Recently enough. Somebody loved this dog and she got lost, and lost is different from abandoned.

**[PANEL 5 - CLOSE / HER FACE]**
**Visual:** Her face, close. Blue eyes, calm, returning Axel's look without urgency. Not performing. Not asking. Just present.

**[PANEL 6 - WIDE / GUSTAVO IN THE BACKGROUND]**
**Visual:** Gustavo watching from the shack doorway. Axel still crouched with the dog.
**GUSTAVO (DIALOGUE):** "She showed up maybe three weeks ago. Just walked in off the canal path. I put water out."
**AXEL (DIALOGUE):** "Nobody came for her."
**GUSTAVO (DIALOGUE):** "Nobody came."

---

## SCENE 03: THE LEMONS / 10:41 AM

**[PANEL 7 - MID / THE CAR DOOR OPEN]**
**Visual:** Axel at the LeMans, driver's door open, loading the alternator housing into the back seat. He leaves the passenger door open — not as an invitation, just because he hasn't closed it yet. She's there in his peripheral vision, at the edge of the yard. He doesn't look at her directly.

**[PANEL 8 - INTERIOR / BOTH IN THE CAR]**
**Visual:** Axel in the driver's seat. She's in the passenger seat. Nobody made a decision. She's sitting upright, looking through the windshield at the road ahead. Her posture is calm, settled, as if she's done this before.
**AXEL (CAPTION):** I don't have a plan for this. I don't have food at the garage, I don't have a leash, I don't know what she eats. I know she's been in a car before because she isn't nervous.
**AXEL (CAPTION):** I close the door and start the engine.

**[PANEL 9 - CLOSE / HER PROFILE AT THE PASSENGER WINDOW]**
**Visual:** Her face turned toward the passenger window, watching the Hialeah blocks go past as the LeMans pulls onto the road. Ears up. Eyes moving. She's reading the city.
**AXEL (CAPTION):** Carrier.
**AXEL (CAPTION):** She doesn't look at me. That seems right.

**[END OF EPISODE]**
