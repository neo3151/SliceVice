# EPISODE 42: CALLE OCHO, LATE (JUNE 5, 1980)

## SCENE 01: ELIO'S GARAGE / 4:00 PM

**[PANEL 1 - WIDE / GARAGE INTERIOR — CARRIER'S INTRODUCTION TO THE CREW]**
**Visual:** The garage, afternoon. Carrier is asleep in a patch of sunlight near the roll door, on her side, completely at ease. Sol is at the workbench and has very clearly arranged her ledger books so they don't get near the dog's space. JP is watching Carrier from a respectful distance, mildly fascinated. Elio has already put a bowl of water near the side wall without saying anything about it.
**AXEL (CAPTION):** Three days. She found the sun patch on the first morning and returns to it with the precision of someone who has done a survey.
**JEAN-PIERRE (DIALOGUE):** "Does she have a name?"
**AXEL (DIALOGUE):** "Carrier."
**JEAN-PIERRE (DIALOGUE):** "Like the frequency."
**AXEL (DIALOGUE):** "Sure."

**[PANEL 2 - CLOSE / SOL WATCHING CARRIER SLEEP]**
**Visual:** Sol looking at the dog over her reading glasses. Her expression is unreadable — not warm exactly, but not indifferent either. The expression of someone recalibrating.
**SOL (DIALOGUE):** "She's going to shed on everything."
**AXEL (DIALOGUE):** "Probably."
**SOL (DIALOGUE):** "She can stay."

---

## SCENE 02: THE LEMONS, MOVING / 10:45 PM

**[PANEL 3 - INTERIOR / LEMONS AT NIGHT — AXEL AND CARRIER]**
**Visual:** The LeMans moving through Little Havana. Carrier in the passenger seat, window cracked, nose working the night air without leaning out — contained, composed, just receiving information. Axel driving, a delivery address on a slip of paper on the dash. Late courier run, low stakes.
**AXEL (CAPTION):** A parts manifest for a contact on SW 8th. Elio's paperwork, nothing Vanguard has touched. The kind of run I used to do before any of this started.

**[PANEL 4 - WIDE / CALLE OCHO NIGHT MARKET EXTERIOR]**
**Visual:** SW 8th Street, Calle Ocho, 11 PM. The night market in full motion — folding tables under string lights, a domino game running loud outside a ventanita, cumbia from somewhere, a man selling mangoes off a truck bed. The LeMans parked at the curb, Carrier visible in the passenger window watching the market with her ears forward, reading everything.
**AXEL (CAPTION):** The market runs until two in the morning six nights a week. The city government has tried to shut it down four times. It's still here.

---

## SCENE 03: THE MARKET / 11:10 PM

**[PANEL 5 - MID / AXEL MOVING THROUGH THE MARKET]**
**Visual:** Axel on foot through the market crowd, the manifest delivered, no hurry now. The Art Deco pastel ruins of a South Beach hotel are just visible across the water to the east — pink stucco going gray, a neon sign missing half its letters, a building that was someone's dream before the economy shifted under it. Axel not looking at it particularly, just moving through.
**AXEL (CAPTION):** Before the season, before the riots, before Vanguard, this block ran on the same logic it runs on tonight. Credit and community and people staying up too late because the heat doesn't break until three AM.

**[PANEL 6 - MID / DOÑA CARMEN AT HER TABLE]**
**Visual:** Doña Carmen at a folding table near the ventanita, a small hand fan going, coffee in front of her. She spots Axel. He stops.
**DOÑA CARMEN (DIALOGUE):** "You look like you slept."
**AXEL (DIALOGUE):** "A little."
**DOÑA CARMEN (DIALOGUE):** "Who's in the car?"
**AXEL (DIALOGUE):** "Carrier."
**DOÑA CARMEN (DIALOGUE):** "Bring her next time. This market has too many men and not enough dogs."

---

## SCENE 04: THE LEMONS, PARKED / 12:20 AM

**[PANEL 7 - INTERIOR / LEMONS — CARRIER ASLEEP ACROSS THE SEAT]**
**Visual:** Carrier stretched across the passenger seat, head resting near the center console, asleep. Axel in the driver's seat, window down, a joint burning low between his fingers, watching the market wind down through the windshield. The amber glow of the radio dial. The smoke drifting out the window into the humid air.
**AXEL (CAPTION):** The Grid is quiet tonight. Not gone. Just at a distance.

**[PANEL 8 - CLOSE / HER PAW NEAR HIS KNEE]**
**Visual:** Carrier's paw resting loose near Axel's knee on the seat — not a gesture, just where it landed when she shifted. He hasn't moved away from it.
**AXEL (CAPTION):** She doesn't ask for anything. She just occupies the same space and that turns out to be enough.

**[PANEL 9 - WIDE / EXTERIOR — THE LEMONS ON CALLE OCHO AT NIGHT]**
**Visual:** Wide exterior shot. The LeMans at the curb, the market behind it still going, the string lights above. Smoke curling from the driver's window. The city alive and indifferent and continuous, the way it has been and will be. Carrier's silhouette visible in the passenger window, ears up, watching something down the block that Axel can't see from where he's sitting.
**AXEL (CAPTION):** Vanguard will move again. They always do. But right now it's June in Miami and the market is still running and she has her ears up like something good might be coming.

**[END OF EPISODE]**
