# EPISODE 4 SCRIPT: "THE SILENT DISTRICT"

**Panel 01**
AXEL: (Caption) Pelican Bay. The wealthiest, most manicured fortress in Sector 2. Reinforced steel gates and a noise curfew enforced by Vanguard snipers.

**Panel 02**
AXEL: (Caption) 60 decibels. That’s the limit after 9:00 PM. The LeMans idles at 110. One turn of the ignition inside those gates is a death sentence.

**Panel 03**
AXEL: (Caption) I have to coast. Two miles of winding, identical streets entirely on momentum. No lights. No engine. Just the slow crush of gravel under dead tires.

**Panel 04**
AXEL: (Caption) The mansions here are like brutalist mausoleums. Zero shadows. Blinding white floodlights hitting the perfectly trimmed hedges.

**Panel 05**
AXEL: (Caption) Ahead, the road is narrowed by high-tech motion sensors disguised as copper lawn ornaments. If I touch the curb, the alarms trigger the roof-mounted security teams.

**Panel 06**
AXEL: (Caption) Without the engine running, I have no power steering. The wheel feels like it's bolted to a cement block. I have to fight it for every inch of the turn.

**Panel 07**
AXEL: (Caption) A white spotlight snaps on from a second-story balcony. I see the silhouette of a Vanguard guard—a retiree with a suppressed Remington. He’s looking for the source of the tire-hum.

**Panel 08**
AXEL: (Caption) I hold my breath. My heartbeat feels louder than the car. We glide past the guard’s line of sight by a fraction of an inch.

**Panel 09**
AXEL: (Caption) The LeMans finally loses its momentum. It rolls to a dead stop right in front of the gates of House 404.

**Panel 10**
AXEL: (Caption) I slip out of the car, staying low. The driveway is covered in silent laser-tripwires. One wrong step and the audit is final.

**Panel 11**
AXEL: (Caption) The front door opens. A woman stands there, silhouetted by the dim hallway light. REYA. She doesn't look like a resident. She looks like she's sitting on a bomb.

**Panel 12**
REYA: (Dialogue - whispered) "You're early, Cutter. I didn't think anyone could ghost through the Bay without triggering a sweep."

**Panel 13**
AXEL: (Caption) I hand her the metallic courier-case. Her fingers brush mine—they’re steady, unlike everyone else I deliver to. 
REYA: (Dialogue - whispered) "Tell Riley the V-Sentry is secure. Now get out before the shift change."

**Panel 14**
AXEL: (Caption) I get back in the LeMans. I don't care about the curfew anymore. I twist the key and the V8 roars to life like a gunshot. I'm out of the Bay before they even rack their bolts.
