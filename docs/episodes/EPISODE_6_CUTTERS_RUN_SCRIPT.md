# SLICE VICE: Episode 6 - "CUTTER'S RUN"

**SETTING:** Stiltsville / Rickenbacker Causeway, Miami, FL.  
**TIME:** 03:22 AM.  
**TONE:** High-octane, neon-drenched, practical stunt action.

### THE STORY PIVOT
Axel and Reya are cornered in a Stiltsville shack. The "Master-Tape" isn't reality-warping software—it's the **V-Sentry Encryption Prototype**, a physical hardware module containing the direct geolocation of every Vanguard black site in the state. Director Vance has deployed a heavy practical blockade to retrieve it.

### SCENE BREAKDOWN
1. **The Blockade**: Vance's pearl-white F-350s have Stiltsville surrounded. The searchlights are blinding.
2. **The Hardware**: Reya slams the chunky prototype module into the LeMans' dash. No "Vector lines"—just high-speed signal noise and industrial sparks.
3. **The Breakout**: Axel keys the nitro. The '78 LeMans bursts through the wooden walls of the shack, smashing onto the pier.
4. **The Rickenbacker Jump**: The bridge is broken. No rendering—just raw velocity. Axel hits the NOS, the car screams, and they leap the massive gap through the sea mist.
5. **The Escape**: The pursuing trucks can't make the jump. They crash into the water. Axel hits the mainland, the neon skyline of Miami looming ahead.

### THE FINAL BEAT
"We aren't couriers anymore, Reya. We're the leverage."
