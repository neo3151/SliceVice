# EPISODE 6 PANEL BREAKDOWN (14 PANELS) - "CUTTER'S RUN"

**Page 1 (The Blockade)**
- **Panel 01:** (Wide) Stiltsville at night. Blinding white floodlights from a ring of Vance's pearl-white F-350s surround the dark, barnacle-covered shacks. The water is black and choppy.
- **Panel 02:** (Interior) Axel's hands white-knuckling the steering wheel of the LeMans. Reya is jamming the chunky V-Sentry Hardware Module into the dash. The nixie tubes are glowing orange.
- **Panel 03:** (Detail) Close-up on the dash. Industrial sparks shower from the module's contact points as it syncs with the car's alternator. No digital lines—just raw electricity.

**Page 2 (The Breakout)**
- **Panel 04:** (Action) The LeMans bursts through the side of a Stiltsville shack, wood splintering, crashing onto the pier. Dust and salt spray in the air.
- **Panel 05:** (Reveal) Director Vance inside his Ford F-350, face illuminated by high-tech dash screens. He's calm, cold, adjusting a headset.
- **Panel 06:** (Wide) The LeMans hurtling down the abandoned, broken Bay Bridge. It’s heading straight for a massive highway gap where the bridge has collapsed into the sea. Behind it, three massive F-350s are in pursuit.

**Page 3 (The Jump)**
- **Panel 07:** (POV) Through the windshield of the LeMans at 80mph. The gap looms. Axel kicks the Nitro. High-speed motion blur streaks the neon skyline.
- **Panel 08:** (Dynamic) High-angle shot of the LeMans flying across the gap. Blue and magenta nitro flames spit from the exhaust. Intense motion blur, sparks, and sea mist.
- **Panel 09:** (Reaction) From Vance's perspective. He is stunned as he sees the LeMans actually make the leap. The truck behind him is swerving, tires screaming on the asphalt.

**Page 4 (The Mainland)**
- **Panel 10:** (Action) Vance's F-350 follows onto the bridge edge but stops too late. The truck skids and plunges into the dark abyss. Headlights cutting through the dark toward the water below.
- **Panel 11:** (Interior) Reya laughing, lit by the clinical glow of the dashboard. The V-Sentry module is humming. The car's alternator is sparking.
- **Panel 12:** (Impact) The LeMans slams back onto solid asphalt on the mainland. A trail of black smoke and shredded rubber follows the car.
- **Panel 13:** (Detail) Axel holding the physical hardware module in his hand, looking at it with grim determination. "We're the leverage now."
- **Panel 14:** (Splash) The battered 1978 LeMans driving towards the massive neon skyline of Miami under a purple dawn. Title: "CUTTER’S RUN."
