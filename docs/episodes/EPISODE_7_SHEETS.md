# EPISODE 7 PANEL BREAKDOWN (14 PANELS) - "THE UNINSTALLER"

- **Panel 09:** (Wide) The drawbridge over the Miami River. The massive steel leaves are creaking upwards just as the lead Bronco reaches the summit. Toll arms are snapping like bone.

- **Panel 14:** (Splash) Axel and Reya hauling a heavy metallic crate towards the LeMans, the bridge towers of Miami glowing in the background. Title: "POST-COMPLIANCE."
