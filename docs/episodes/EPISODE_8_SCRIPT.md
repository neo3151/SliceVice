# EPISODE 8 SCRIPT: "THE SIEGE OF THE MARINE STADIUM"

**Panel 01**
AXEL: (Caption) Virginia Key. The Marine Stadium. A concrete ribcage rotting in the salt spray. It’s the perfect place to die, and a hell of a place to park.

**Panel 02**
AXEL: (Caption) The transmission had been screaming for three blocks. A high-pitched, metallic wail that sounded like a dying ghost. 
REYA: (Dialogue) "Axel, the RPMs are redlining! We’re not shifting!"

**Panel 03**
AXEL: (Caption) I slam the shifter. Nothing but the sound of grinding gears and shattered synchronizers. The LeMans rolls to a halt right under the 300-foot concrete canopy.
AXEL: (Dialogue) "Car's dead. Out. Now!"

**Panel 04**
AXEL: (Caption) We scramble into the shadows of the grandstands. Behind us, three sets of blinding amber headlights crest the causeway. Vanguard.

**Panel 05**
AXEL: (Caption) They don't have to guess where we are. The V-Sentry module in the passenger seat is basically a homing beacon. 
REYA: (Dialogue) "I can't kill the signal, Axel. But I might be able to mask it if I can tap into the stadium's old PA system."

**Panel 06**
AXEL: (Caption) VANCE. (Voice over Comm) "Cutter. You're out of road. Surrender the Module and maybe I'll let the sweepers leave your organs in the chassis."

**Panel 07**
AXEL: (Caption) I pull the Mossberg from the trunk. The steel is cold and honest. I take a position behind a massive concrete pillar. 
AXEL: (Dialogue) "Vance! You want the module? Come and audit it yourself!"

**Panel 08**
AXEL: (Caption) The first Vanguard Bronco charges. It ignores the stairs, bouncing its heavy shocks over the concrete ledges. High-intensity amber floods the stands.

**Panel 09**
AXEL: (Caption) I wait for the glare to hit the pillar. Then I pivot. Three rounds of buckshot into the Bronco’s radiator. Steam and sparks erupt like a geyser.

**Panel 10**
AXEL: (Caption) A grazing shot from a Vanguard Interceptor clips my shoulder. A hot, searing streak of pain. The 'Uninstaller' arc isn't a game anymore.

**Panel 11**
REYA: (Dialogue) "Frequency synced! Axel, cover your ears! I'm feeding the V-Sentry pulse directly into the stadium's horn-speakers!"

**Panel 12**
AXEL: (Caption) A deafening, 140-decibel industrial screech mirrors the sound of a failing transmission—but amplified a thousand times. The concrete canopy vibrates.

**Panel 13**
AXEL: (Caption) The Vanguard drivers are blinded by the sonic feedback. Their Broncos swerve, tires screaming on the slick concrete. They’re effectively deaf and dumb.

**Panel 14**
AXEL: (Caption) I grab Reya. The LeMans is a corpse, but we’re still moving. We vanish into the mangrove dark, leaves rustling like a closing warranty.
