# EPISODE 8 VISUAL SHEETS: "THE SIEGE OF THE MARINE STADIUM"

**Style Anchors (Volume 2):**
> "...heavy industrial ink style, deep amber lighting, rain-drenched rusted metal, brutalist 1980s miami architecture, cinematic noir shadows, thick mechanical detail, analog grit, 4k high fidelity graphic novel art."

| Panel | Scene Description | Lighting/Palette |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **01** | Wide shot of the Miami Marine Stadium. A massive, jagged cantilevered concrete roof hanging over the water. Torrential rain. | Blue-grey atmosphere, distant amber lights. |
| **02** | Close up of Axel’s hand on the shifter of the 1988 LeMans. Sparks and smoke are rising from the center console. | Harsh amber dash glow, industrial grit. |
| **03** | The LeMans comes to a stop under the massive concrete canopy of the stadium. Axel and Reya are exiting the car, silhouettes against the rain. | High contrast noir, amber highlights on the car. |
| **04** | Three Vanguard Broncos (1986, matte black) crest the stadium causeway, their roof-mounted amber light bars blindingly bright. | Blinding amber glare, deep black shadows. |
| **05** | Reya ducked behind a concrete pillar, holding the V-Sentry module. The module is pulsing a radioactive orange. | Industrial amber, cold concrete textures. |
| **06** | A low-angle shot of Director Vance (silhouetted) standing in front of a white Ford F-350. He’s holding a tactical radio. | High-key amber backlight, cinematic noir. |
| **07** | Axel taking a tactical position behind a pillar, racking a Mossberg shotgun. His face is illuminated by a nearby amber floodlight. | Dramatic shadows, warm amber skin tones. |
| **08** | A Vanguard Bronco jumping over a concrete seating tier inside the stadium, suspension fully compressed, headlights beaming. | Dynamic motion, heavy ink splashes (rain). |
| **09** | Axel firing the shotgun. The muzzle flash is a bright orange burst illuminating the concrete dust and rain. | Explosive orange/amber, deep black shadows. |
| **10** | Close up of Axel’s shoulder as a bullet graze tears through his leather jacket. Blood is a dark, heavy ink. | Intense amber lighting, grit and pain. |
| **11** | Reya connecting wires to a rusted stadium PA speaker hanging from the concrete ceiling. Sparks are flying. | Electrical blue sparks against amber background. |
| **12** | Extreme wide shot of the stadium. The massive concrete roof seems to vibrate. Small birds (pelicans) scattering in terror. | Moody grey and amber, cinematic scale. |
| **13** | Vanguard Broncos swerving uncontrollably, the drivers clutching their ears. Heavy rain masking the chaos. | Blurry motion, high-intensity amber flares. |
| **14** | Axel and Reya vanishing into the mangrove swamp at the edge of the stadium. The LeMans is left behind, a dark silhouette. | Deep blacks, mangrove greens, fading amber. |
