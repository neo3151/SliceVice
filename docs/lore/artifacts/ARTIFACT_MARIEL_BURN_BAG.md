# MARIEL SURGE "BURN-BAG" IDENTIFIER (COUNTERFEIT)
**Historical Anchor:** The Mariel Surge / The Data Flood (April 1980)
**Item Status / Condition:** Water-damaged, heavily creased, smells of stale seawater and Cubita espresso.

## 🔍 PHYSICAL INSPECTION
A jagged, rectangular strip of heavy-stock industrial paper. Originally pristine white with the stark, blocky Vanguard logo printed in corporate gray, it is now stained a sickly, sun-bleached yellow. The edges are crusted with dried sea-salt. A crude, magnetic strip—scavenged from an old cassette tape—has been glued to the bottom rim using a brown resin that reeks of cheap cigar smoke. The ink on the central identification numbers is heavily smudged, deliberately warped by exposing the thermal-paper to the steam of a high-pressure espresso machine. 

## 📖 THE EVENT MEMORY
When the Mariel flotilla hit the Florida coast, the Vanguard's servers overheated trying to assign permanent "Existence IDs" to 125,000 undocumented refugees. To stave off a total mainframe meltdown, Vanguard resorted to temporary, analog tracking: "Burn-Bags." These were physical paper tags meant to serve as a low-level identifier until the grid cooled down. 

Almost immediately, the Little Havana locals realized the paper logic was flawed. Elio Valdés and his network figured out that the temporary tags lacked the biometric encryption of a true Existence ID. By steaming the thermal paper over Doña Carmen’s *ventanita* espresso machine and gluing magnetic tape over the barcode, they created "Ghost Identifiers." Anyone holding a counterfeited Burn-Bag was technically logged into the system as an unassigned, low-priority refugee, effectively rendering them invisible to high-level corporate audits.

## 🔺 FACTION TIES
- **The Vanguard:** They violently despise these. Possession of a manipulated Burn-Bag is a Level-4 Compliance Violation, carrying immediate repo-orders. Director Vance has deployed thermal-drones specifically tuned to detect the specific resin glue used on the counterfeits.
- **The Cartels:** The Miami Cartels initially bought these in bulk to move high-priority assets past port blockades under the guise of refugee transports, creating a lucrative black market.
- **The Mesh (Little Havana):** To the exile community and the underground network, producing and distributing these false tags is an act of humanitarian rebellion against the corporate state.

## ⚙️ CUTTER LOGIC / STREET UTILITY
*Hold onto it when the heat drops.*
If Axel's '78 LeMans is carrying heat and about to hit a Vanguard Toll blockade, a Cutter can feed a manipulated Burn-Bag into the toll's physical receiver. The forged magnetic strip causes a localized "Buffer Overrun" in that specific toll booth, forcing the scanner to default to a 60-second "System Reboot." It creates a one-minute analog window to slip through a blockade without pinging the central grid—provided you don't stall the engine.
