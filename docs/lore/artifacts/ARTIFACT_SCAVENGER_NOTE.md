# Scavenger's Warning Note

> *[Found crumpled under the passenger seat of a wrecked '77 Malibu. Written in charcoal on the back of a fuel-rationing coupon.]*
> 
> To whoever pulls this iron out of the mangroves:
> 
> Don't cross the Intercoastal bridge after the 10 PM Compliance Curfew. The Billboard-Kings are running dark loads of white gold tonight. They’ll shoot anything with a headlight just to keep the Vanguard searchlights off their wake.
> 
> I saw what they did to Frankie. He stopped near 82nd to fix a frozen fuel line. He didn't even see the blacked-out cigarette boat coming. They hit him with a burst of MAC-10 fire before he could reach for his lug wrench. I only found his driver-side mirror. 
> 
> It's not just the Cartel. The Great Frost is turning the asphalt into a skating rink. If your engine cuts out—if you lose your idle for even ten seconds—the moisture in the lines will freeze solid. 
> 
> Keep the revs up. Do not downshift. If the dashboard clock hits zero, just ditch the car and run for Stiltsville. The Corporation can repossess a rusted chassis, but they can't foreclose on a man hiding in the salt-fog.
> 
> -- Jax
