# THE SECTOR L "MCDUFFIE" THERMAL BATON
**Historical Anchor:** The McDuffie Riots & The Cordon (May 1980)
**Item Status / Condition:** Melted synthetic casing, fractured power-cell, permanently smells of burning rubber and ozone.

## 🔍 PHYSICAL INSPECTION
A standard-issue Vanguard Compliance Baton, heavily warped. The sleek, matte-black carbon-fiber shaft is blistered and bubbled, having been subjected to an intense, sustained heat flash. The striking head—originally designed to deliver a non-lethal, paralyzing electromagnetic pulse—is shattered. Inside the cracked polycarbonate head, the exposed copper coils arc sporadically, emitting a faint, high-pitched whine and smelling sharply of ionized air. Etched crudely into the scorched grip with a hunting knife are the words: "GRID DEAD - 1980."

## 📖 THE EVENT MEMORY
During the devastating McDuffie Riots of May 1980, the Vanguard attempted to suppress the unrest in Sector L (Liberty City/Overtown) by deploying heavy riot-suppression units and installing rapid "Compliance Plinths" at major intersections. The community responded with systematic, tactical destruction of corporate infrastructure. 

This specific baton belonged to a Vanguard Enforcer whose unit was ambushed near a power substation. The local resistance didn't just beat back the patrol; they used scavenged thermite to permanently melt the substation's relay towers, creating the "Scar"—a permanent dead-zone with Zero-Compliance where Vanguard signals physically cannot penetrate. The heat from the thermite blast flash-melted the baton. It was recovered from the ash by the resistance and kept as a grim trophy of the day the Grid was broken.

## 🔺 FACTION TIES
- **Sector L Residents:** The baton is a symbol of defiance. It proves that despite Vanguard's claims of omnipotence, their armor melts and their tech breaks.
- **The Vanguard:** A deeply embarrassing operational failure. Director Vance has purged the official logs of the Sector L retreat, but recovering these melted tools is a high priority to scrub the historical record clean.
- **The Cutters:** Analog smugglers treat these melted batons with borderline superstition. They are traded in back-alleys as charms of good luck.

## ⚙️ CUTTER LOGIC / STREET UTILITY
*A dead weapon still has teeth.*
Though useless as a club, the fractured electromagnetic coils inside the baton still contain a latent, unstable charge. A skilled mechanic (like Elio) can wire a melted Thermal Baton into a vehicle’s alternator as a rigged "EMP Spike." If an Interceptor drone attaches a tracking limpet to the chassis, discharging the baton through the frame will selectively short out external electronics without killing the LeMans' analog engine. It's a one-time-use get-out-of-jail-free card.
