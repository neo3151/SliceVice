# MAINLAND TACTICAL LEDGER: "POST-RICKENBACKER" STATUS

With the Rickenbacker Causeway cleared and the Rickenbacker bridge jump neutralized, the "Cutter's Run" mission has transitioned to the mainland. This ledger outlines the grounded mechanical and tactical status for the final delivery phase.

## 1. VEHICLE STATUS: THE "CUTTER" (LEMANS)
- **Engine Temp:** High. Nitro-burn from the bridge jump has stressed the 400 CID block. Axel’s professional cooling mods (dual-core industrial radiator) held the gaskets together, but the car is idling rough and the cabin smells of ozone.
- **Handling Anomaly:** Severe weight shift. The **V-Sentry Vault** (50lbs of solid corporate hardware) is currently bolted into the under-seat tactical safe. This has thrown off the G-Body center of gravity, making high-speed cornering at 90+ MPH highly unpredictable.
- **Tactical Protocol:** **"GHOST DRIVE" (Cold-Start Toggle)**. Axel has activated the electronic bypass, killing all external lights and electrical signatures while maintaining engine pressure—effective for high-speed stealth through the Port of Miami industrial zones.

## 2. THE TARGET: V-SENTRY ENCRYPTION PROTOTYPE
- **Value:** The module contains the **Personal "Life-Contract" Signatures** of Axel’s mother. It serves as the only physical "Release Key" in existence.
- **Leverage:** The hardware also holds the **Corporate Audit Route-Keys** for all Vanguard Interceptor patrols. To save the asset, Axel has to shut down the very infrastructure Vance uses to hunt them.

## 3. STAGING GROUND: BISCAYNE SAFE-SITE
- **Location:** Abandoned **Miami Subs Grill** on Biscayne Blvd.
- **Defensive Setup:** **"Drive-Thru Perimeter"**. The LeMans is backed into the kitchen area through a smashed service door, keeping the engine warm and the wheels aimed at the exit.
- **Intelligence:** Reya has repurposed the old 1980s drive-thru intercom system as a makeshift tactical scanner, listening to un-encrypted Vanguard radio chatter between commercial signal breaks.

## 4. THE THREAT: PRIVATE LIQUIDATION
Director Vance has entered a state of **"Private War."** By avoiding official law enforcement, he has dispatched unofficial **Recovery-Kill Teams**. If the ledger isn't balanced at the drop-off, the order is **Total Liquidation**— scrapping both the driver and the car.
