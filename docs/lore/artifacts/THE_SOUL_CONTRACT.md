# THE 30-MINUTE GUARANTEE (INTERNAL POLICY)

**CONFIDENTIAL: HUMAN RESOURCES DIVISION ONLY**

## 1. Primary Operating Directive
The public-facing guarantee ("30 Minutes or it's Free") is entirely a legal front used to establish the baseline parameters of the blood-pact contract signed by all incoming Drivers. 

**Neon Slice Inc. does not care if the pizza is free.** The pizza is a delivery vehicle for the underlying magical tether.

## 2. The Nixie Timer Tether
Upon assigning an order, a Driver is cryptographically tethered to a proprietary Nixie-tube countdown timer welded into the vehicle's dashboard. 
- At `30:00`, the timer initializes the pact.
- The countdown establishes a localized pocket of high-stress reality around the driver. This stress generates extreme kinetic momentum ("Slipstream Mana").
- If the pizza touches the delivery drop-zone before `00:00`, the pact is resolved. The kinetic energy generated during the run is logged, and the driver retains their soul.

## 3. Failure Event (`00:00` Resolution)
If the timer hits `00:00` prior to drop-off, a complete contractual default occurs.
- The Dashboard Timer does not simply "time-out." It triggers a localized vacuum, instantly stripping the remaining biometric "Life Force" from the driver to pay for the cost of the delayed pizza.
- This Life Force is compressed within the radio receiver, routed back to Corporate Data Centers via the FM frequency, and ultimately fed directly into the subterranean megastructures beneath Miami to power the Board's immortality engines.

## 4. Anomalous Resolutions
In the event that the delivery is completed during the exact, localized millisecond of `00:00` (an event mathematically impossible by Corporate actuarial tables), the pact glitches. The soul cannot be harvested, but the contract cannot be resolved. 

In this event, Internal Audit Drones are dispatched to physically execute the driver and balance the mathematical error manually using chemical correction.
