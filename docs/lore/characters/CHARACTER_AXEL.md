# PROTAGONIST: AXEL "OVERDRIVE"
**DATE OF ORIGIN:** JANUARY 1, 1980

- **The Liability:** In late '79, Axel's mother collapsed under the weight of accumulated "Grievance Points"—corporate default metrics that increase the physical gravity weighing down a person. Her physical body failed, and her entire consciousness archive was repossessed and stripped onto an air-gapped Vanguard Trust server.
- **The Debt:** Axel inherited a localized "Blood Debt." He drives his un-tracked 1978 LeMans through the off-grid routes, completing lethally dangerous deliveries to earn "Re-Enfranchisement Credits" to buy back his mother's archive before Vanguard permanently formats her drive at the Freedom Tower.
- **The Supernatural Mechanics:** The "Nixie Timer" hardwired into his dashboard doesn't just count minutes; it ticks down Time Credits. If it hits 00:00, it triggers a localized vacuum that extracts the physical oxygen from his cabin. Mixtapes serve as spatial spell catalysts to bend the geometry of the road.
- **The Vehicle:** A heavily modified 1978 Pontiac LeMans (G-Body). It’s a rusted, matte-black land yacht functioning outside the DMV grid. The left headlight is held in by silver duct tape, and the V8 idles with a rhythmic mechanical knock. It isn't just a car; it's a mobile Faraday cage constantly shuffling parts.
- **The Flaw:** "Analog Pragmatism." Axel has no patience for corporate municipal structure. He treats the entire banking and political system of Miami with absolute, mechanical contempt.
- **The Moral Compass:** He refuses to transport lethal ordinance or target civilians, though he'll charge double if the drop involves bleeding Vanguard resources.
