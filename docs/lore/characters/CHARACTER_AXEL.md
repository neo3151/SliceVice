# PROTAGONIST: AXEL "OVERDRIVE"
**DATE OF ORIGIN:** JANUARY 1, 1980

- **The Ghost:** Axel’s mother, Maria, was "Foreclosed" by the HOA Vanguard on Christmas Eve, 1979. A $15.00 lawn-trimming violation during the first frost was enough to trigger a total asset seizure. Her consciousness was backed up to a Vanguard storage drive—now he has to pay the monthly "maintenance fee" just to keep her from being deleted.
- **The Debt:** Axel drives for Neon Slice strictly to service the 15% interest rate on his mother's legacy. He's not a courier; he's a biological interest payment.
- **The Vehicle:** A 1978 Pontiac LeMans (G-Body). It’s a rusted, matte-black land yacht. The left headlight is held in by silver duct tape, and the V8 idles with a rhythmic mechanical knock that sounds like a countdown. It’s not a car; it’s a temporary reprieve from the scrap heap.
- **The Flaw:** "Functional Vulgarity." Axel has no patience for the Grid's "Neo-Neon" PR. He knows the system is built on low-grade steel and high-grade debt, and he treats it with the appropriate amount of mechanical contempt.
- **The Moral Compass:** He refuses to deliver to "Active Sanitization zones" if it means running over civilians, though he'll charge double if the customer is a Cartel boss.
