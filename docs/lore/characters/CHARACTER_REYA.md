# ALLY: REYA (The Drowned District Scavenger)
**ROLE:** ANA-LOGIC MEDIUM

- **The Skill:** The only person in Stiltsville who understands the magnetic-logic of the "Grid-Tape." She is a "Mechanical Medium"—she doesn't read code; she hears the physical friction of the tape across the heads. In 1980, she's the only one who can decrypt the Vanguard's "Great Frost" sweep patterns.
- **The Vehicle:** She doesn't have a car; she has a **1979 Boston Whaler** skiff with a supercharged outboard motor that coughs blue smoke and sounds like a machine gun. It's painted a dull, non-reflective grey to disappear into the fog of the Everglades.
- **The Motivation:** She wants to use the Tape to hard-code a "Safe Zone" for the scavengers—a piece of the Grid that Director Vance can't legally or physically repossess.
- **The Vibe:** Smells like saltwater and WD-40. Her hands are permanently stained with magnetic oxide and motor oil.
- **Relationship to Axel:** She treats Axel like a useful but dangerous tool. He’s the muscle and the wheels, but she knows that if he ever manages to pay his debt, he'll become part of the system she’s fighting.
