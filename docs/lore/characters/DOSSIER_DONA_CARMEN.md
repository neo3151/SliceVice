# CIVILIAN ANCHOR DOSSIER: DOÑA CARMEN (The Ventanita)
**ROLE:** MANAGER OF INTELLIGENCE & ANALOG CONTRABAND
**LOCATION:** Versailles Restaurant (3555 SW 8th Street, Little Havana)

## The Character Profile
Doña Carmen is approximately eighty years old, standing barely five feet tall, typically dressed in floral prints with a tortoiseshell clip holding back white hair. She emigrated from Havana in 1961, bringing with her a lethal pragmatic intelligence. She operates the exterior walk-up coffee window (*la ventanita*) at the Versailles Restaurant on Calle Ocho. Her physical demeanor is that of a warm, overbearing grandmother demanding people eat her pastry; her actual function is that of a spymaster.

## The Narrative Function
In a city where electronic communication is heavily audited by the Vanguard Trust, Doña Carmen manages the "Ventanita Defense"—the completely analog, physical distribution of intelligence for the off-grid resistance. 
- **The Pastry Drop:** She conceals handwritten shipping manifests, analog bypass-transmitters, and physical cash inside wax-paper pastelito boxes, distributing them to couriers (like Axel) under the guise of free food.
- **The Information Hub:** Because the ventanita is neutral territory for all of Little Havana's political and social factions, she hears every rumor, tracks the deployment of every Repo-Truck on Calle Ocho, and monitors the subtle shifts in Grievance Point gravity before anyone else in the city.

## Relationship to Axel
She instantly clocks Axel not as an Anglo outsider, but as an undernourished, dying combatant. She actively feeds him (literally and metaphorically), leveraging his speed to run analog interference for the refugee surge. She calls him a "ghost," knowing his lack of digital footprint makes him the perfect asset for her contraband.

## Supernatural Proximity
Carmen is completely immune to the physical manifestation of Vanguard's Grievance Points. The corporate gravity wells pass over her without effect because her psychological debt is nonexistent; she paid her soul's toll when she crossed the Florida Straits in '61. She views Vance not as a supernatural terror, but just another corrupt bureaucrat who thinks paperwork can stop a hurricane.
