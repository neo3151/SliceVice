# CIVILIAN ANCHOR DOSSIER: JEAN-PIERRE (The Mesh-Engineer)
**ROLE:** ARCHITECT OF THE INVISIBLE GRID
**LOCATION:** Sector H / Little Haiti (NE 2nd Avenue)

## The Character Profile
Jean-Pierre is in his mid-forties, lean, severe, and physically anchored by the tension in his shoulders. An immigrant fleeing the Duvalier regime, he arrived in Miami without refugee status and was immediately targeted for "foreclosure" by the Vanguard Trust. Instead of submitting to corporate extraction, Jean-Pierre utilized his background in high-frequency engineering to construct a parallel society. He is calm, intensely patient, and views the technological suppression of his people with cold, mathematical precision.

## The Narrative Function
Jean-Pierre is the mechanical heart of the resistance against Director Vance. While Axel fights the Vanguard on the asphalt, Jean-Pierre fights them in the broadcast frequencies.
- **The Analog Mesh:** Using scavenged copper wire, shortwave radio transceivers, and car batteries, he built the "Mesh-Network"—an untraceable, decentralized communication grid spanning Little Haiti.
- **The Faraday Sanctuaries:** More than just radios, Jean-Pierre's copper fortifications physical insulate city blocks. The heavy wiring acts as a localized Faraday cage against Vanguard's "Soul Extractors," ensuring that the undocumented residents of Sector H cannot be targeted by digital repossessions or Grievance Gravity.

## Relationship to Axel
Jean-Pierre is Axel's intellectual mirror. Both men are deeply technical and obsessed with remaining off-grid, but where Axel operates in isolated, suicidal motion, Jean-Pierre operates in decentralized, collective defense. Jean-Pierre respects Axel's hardware (the heavily modified LeMans) and trusts Reya's endorsement, viewing Axel as a blunt but necessary instrument to protect the Mesh.

## Supernatural Proximity
Jean-Pierre monitors the decrypted Coast Guard channels, but he isn't listening for boats—he is listening for the specific thermal ping of Vanguard's extraction hardware. He understands the mechanics of the Soul Debt better than Axel. Jean-Pierre is the architect of the "Analog Jammer," the heavy radio devices Axel must throw onto incoming Mariel refugee boats to scrub their souls from Vance's incoming nets.
