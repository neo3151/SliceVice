# CIVILIAN ANCHOR DOSSIER: SOL ABRAMOWITZ (The Oblivious Canary)
**ROLE:** A WINDOW INTO THE MUNDANE
**LOCATION:** Condo Canyon (Hollywood Beach, FL)

## The Character Profile
Sol Abramowitz is seventy-four years old, retired from the garment-industry in the Bronx, and living the promised Florida dream on the 18th floor of the Imperial Towers condominium. He is defined by his sharp New York wit, his insistence on precise deli standards (only sour rye), and his profound, echoing grief over the loss of his wife, Miriam. He is completely disconnected from the brutal, off-grid street wars of southern Miami.

## The Narrative Function
Sol serves as the ideological and emotional counter-weight to the extreme tension of the Vanguard conflict. He is the oblivious canary in the coal mine.
- **The Innocence of the Ignorant:** Sol is entirely unaware of Nixie Timers, Soul Extractors, or the Blood Debt. He believes the city is just changing, as cities do. 
- **The Accidental Informant:** Because he interacts with the legitimate, surface-level economy, he catches the downstream physical symptoms of Vance's supernatural aggression. When Vanguard initiates massive land-grabs or hoards steel to build Repo-Trucks, Sol complains about it over corned beef, attributing it to "shady county commissioners building the Metrorail."

## Relationship to Axel
To Sol, Axel is just a quiet young man doing "deliveries," reminding him of his nephew Lenny. To Axel, Sol represents the reality he is fighting to preserve: a mundane, boring, safe existence where the only real tragedy is natural grief, not corporate foreclosure. Sitting in the diner booth with Sol is the only time Axel's internal RPMs drop to idle. As Axel notes: both men carry ghosts in their pockets (Sol's photograph; Axel's cassette tape).

## Supernatural Proximity
Sol resides in "Condo Canyon," the very epicenter of Vanguard's legitimate banking architecture. He lives directly above the beast. The supernatural elements—like Grievance Points—do not touch him because he represents a demographic Vanguard has already secured: the heavily pensioned, legally compliant retiree class. He is shielded by his legitimate paperwork, completely oblivious that the young man across the table is suffocating from the weight of the air.
