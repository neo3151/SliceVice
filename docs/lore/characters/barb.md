# Barb

**Class/Craft:** Union Steward (Paladin Elder)
**Table Status:** Table 1 Ancient (Pre-2013, effectively immortal)
**Alignment:** Lawful Good (Driven entirely by the CBA)

## DBZ Power Scaling (Bureau-Kinesis)
*In the Mailstorm universe, power isn't measured in "Ki"—it's measured in PPH (Pieces Per Hour) output and Grievance Mana.*

* **Aura Signature:** A brilliant, blinding white armor of pure Contractual Logic. When Management attempts to violate the National Agreement, Barb's aura expands into a towering shield that smells faintly of old coffee and mimeographed paper, physically pushing supervisors backward.
* **Base Power Level:** 100% Contract Enforcement. Nothing slips past her.
* **Max Output Level:** "Step B Ascension" (Only invoked when management refuses a cease and desist at the local level).

## Passive Abilities
* **[Immortal Anchor]:** Barb cannot be fired, reassigned, or broken. She has survived multiple facility consolidations, hazardous material spills, and five different Postmaster Generals. She is a foundational pillar; if she retires, the station's physical architecture will likely collapse.
* **[The All-Seeing Ear]:** If a supervisor pulls a carrier into a private office without union representation, Barb can hear the violation from three zip codes away and instantly teleport to the doorway to stop it.

## Signature Attacks (Arts & Wards)
* **[The Weingarten Smite]:** The ultimate counter-attack. When a carrier is interrogated, Barb steps between them and Management, raising her hand. She unleashes a shockwave of raw "Grievance Mana" that completely stops the interview, rendering all of Management's questions legally void.
* **[The 14-Day Paper Cut]:** A devastating damage-over-time attack. Barb files a grievance so meticulously massive that the supervisor is forced to spend their entire week organizing paperwork instead of bothering the floor, effectively silencing them for the duration of the cooldown.

## Inventory Loadout
* **[The Master Contract]:** (Blessed) A literal tome of holy, protective text. The CBA (Collective Bargaining Agreement). Simply holding it causes Management's DOIS numbers to visually blur.
* **[The Union Pen]:** (Blessed) A promotional plastic click-pen that she uses to sign Grievance Forms. The ink is forged from pure spite and undeniable contract facts; looking directly at a signed form burns the ocular nerves of any EAS employee.

## Deep Lore / Secrets
Barb has the actual power to single-handedly bind "The Audit Demon" to a 90s fax machine. She understands that the Algorithm is severely weak to analog interference. She refuses to train a successor because she firmly believes the current generation lacks the required "Spite Mana" to endure the true horrors of Step-B grievance meetings.
