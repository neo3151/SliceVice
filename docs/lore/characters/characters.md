# PROTAGONIST: AXEL "OVERDRIVE"
- **The Ghost:** Axel’s mother, Maria, was "Foreclosed" by the HOA Vanguard after a $15.00 lawn-trimming violation escalated into a seizure of the family estate. Her consciousness was backed up to a Vanguard storage drive.
- **The Debt:** Axel drives for Neon Slice strictly to earn the "Re-Enfranchisement Credits" needed to buy back his mother's legacy.
- **The Vehicle:** A grimy 1988 Pontiac LeMans hatchback. It’s a boxy, matte-black coffin on wheels with a silver duct-taped headlight.
- **The Flaw:** "Mechanical Hubris." Axel believes anything can be fixed with a 10mm socket and enough torque—including the broken system he lives in.

# SUPPORT: "DISPATCH" (Riley)
- **Role:** The cynical, chain-smoking operator for Neon Slice. 
- **The Secret:** Riley was a top-tier Vanguard data-thief before she got caught and forced into corporate service. She keeps Axel alive because his reckless success is the only thing skewing the metrics enough to hide her own digital embezzlement. 
- **The Personality:** Exhausted, sarcastic, but possesses a deep-seated hatred for the HOA Vanguard that matches Axel's.

# ANTAGONIST: DON FLAMINGO
- **Role:** The cartel boss / HOA President of the most exclusive gated community, "The Palms".
- **Motivation:** Wants to buy out Neon Slice Pizza to launder his dark-magic operations.
- **Personality:** Wears immaculate pastel suits. Speaks softly. Will brutally murder people for not cutting their lawn to the required 2.5 inches.
