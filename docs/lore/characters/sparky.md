# Sparky

**Class/Craft:** Maintenance Mechanic (Artificer)
**Table Status:** Table 1 (But exists entirely outside the constraints of mortal time)
**Alignment:** True Neutral (Loyal only to the machines and the breakroom couch)

## DBZ Power Scaling (Bureau-Kinesis)
*In the Mailstorm universe, power isn't measured in "Ki"—it's measured in PPH (Pieces Per Hour) output and Grievance Mana.*

* **Aura Signature:** A sputtering, greasy aura composed of airborne WD-40, paper dust, and a very distinct smell of cheap black coffee. His aura feels inherently *heavy* and lethargic, drastically slowing the pace of anyone who walks into his domain, forcing them to adopt a relaxed, unhurried mindset.
* **Base Power Level:** 0% (When actively hiding in the Maintenance Cage).
* **Max Output Level:** "The Machine Whisperer" (He can miraculously make an entirely broken DBCS machine run for 30 minutes just by hitting it with a heavy wrench).

## Passive Abilities
* **[The Invisibility Cloak]:** Sparky has mastered the art of "Strategic Disappearance." Management simply cannot find him when a machine breaks down. He navigates the blind spots of the factory floor, knowing exactly where the OIG cameras can't see him napping.
* **[Union Sanctioned Break]:** His most powerful passive defense. If a manager finds him and demands he fix something, he simply looks at his watch, declares he is on a "safety break," and management physically cannot compel him to move.

## Signature Attacks (Arts & Wards)
* **[The Tag-Out Smite]:** The ultimate denial-of-service spell. If a supervisor attempts to force him to patch a machine unsafely to meet a deadline, Sparky deploys a giant red "Lockout/Tagout" padlock onto the main power breaker. It completely kills the machine, legally binding it in red tape until *he* decides it's safe to turn back on. It halts all production and destroys the Algorithm's timeline.
* **[The Zip-Tie Patch]:** A minor healing spell. He applies a handful of 64-wraps, duct tape, and zip-ties to critical infrastructure, magically forcing it to hold together until exactly the end of his shift, ensuring the machine breaks down purely on Tour 3's time.

## Inventory Loadout
* **[The Heavy Wrench]:** (Blessed) Used not to tighten bolts, but to perform "Percussive Maintenance" on ancient metal chassis, shocking the eldritch machinery back to life.
* **[The Oil Rag]:** (Cursed) Constantly dangling from his back pocket. It's never been washed, absorbing the sins and paper dust of the postal plant for twenty-five years.

## Deep Lore / Secrets
Sparky understands that the sorting machines are not purely mechanical—they are sentient, eldritch beings that feed on mail. He doesn't repair them; he *appeases* them. He acts as the sole priest capable of calming a rogue DBCS when it starts shredding letters. He longs for the simple days before the Algorithm started installing digital sensors everywhere to track him.
