# Stan

**Class/Craft:** City Carrier (Tank)
**Table Status:** Table 1 (Elder / Heavy Aura)
**Alignment:** Lawful Apathetic (Only cares about the CBA, his route, and going home in 8 hours)

## DBZ Power Scaling (Bureau-Kinesis)
*In the Mailstorm universe, power isn't measured in "Ki"—it's measured in PPH (Pieces Per Hour) output and Grievance Mana.*

* **Aura Signature:** A heavy, suffocating blue haze of pure apathetic cigarette smoke and LLV exhaust. When Management inevitably pisses him off and he is forced into **V-Time Overdrive** (Penalty Overtime mode), the aura solidifies. The surrounding air drops by twenty degrees, instantly freezing out "The Algorithm's" influence and making Supervisors physically unable to approach him without shivering.
* **Base Power Level:** 100% Street Efficiency. He never hurries, yet never falls behind.
* **Max Output Level:** Table 1 Ascendant (He only unlocks this when protecting a CCA from unjust discipline).

## Passive Abilities
* **[The 8-Hour Glint]:** Management cannot alter his punch-out time. If a supervisor attempts to hand him a pivot (extra work) after 3:30 PM, the physical paper simply burns up in his hand.
* **[Unbreakable Stride]:** Stan is immune to all weather debuffs. Rain, sleet, or "All-Call Blizzards" do not reduce his movement speed. He simply pulls his worn-out Postal Beanie down further over his eyes and keeps walking. 

## Signature Attacks (Arts & Wards)
* **[The 64-Strike: Infinite Rebound]:** Using his legendary mastery of the 64-wrap rubber bands, Stan snaps a single band with enough kinetic force to break the sound barrier. It ricochets violently between mailboxes, sorting cases, and scanners, creating a localized EMP of sheer physical force that shatters the Algorithm's tracking signals in a 50-foot radius.
* **[The Weingarten Ward]:** A massive, impenetrable defensive shield. If a supervisor tries to pull him into the office for discipline, Stan loudly invokes his union rights, physically summoning Barb (if she isn't already there) and reducing the supervisor's attack power to zero.

## Inventory Loadout
* **[The Stained Mug]:** (Blessed) His coffee mug acts as his primary conduit. As long as it holds liquid (even toxic breakroom sludge), his "Pain Numb" buff remains active.
* **[The Heavy Satchel]:** (Cursed but Mastered) A piece of armor that has weighed on his spine for thirty years. It grounds him to reality, allowing him to carry impossible loads without his knees tearing.

## Deep Lore / Secrets
Deep down, Stan doesn't want Kip to quit. He acts like the rookie is annoying, but Stan remains haunted by the disappearance of his former partner, "Eight-Hour Eddie." Stan trains Kip ruthlessly not out of malice, but because he refuses to watch another soul get eaten by the Table 2 Curse. He wants Kip to survive the 90-day probation and undergo "The Hardening," secretly hoping Kip will be the one to break the Algorithm.
