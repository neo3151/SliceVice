# DELIVERY CONTRACT: The Española Tripwire Trace
**Destination:** Sector A, Española Way (Third-floor Mediterranean walk-up, Pink Flamingo balcony)
**The Client:** "Numbers" Ricky, a highly paranoid, over-caffeinated Cartel accountant trying to liquidate his standing before the Vanguard audits his existence.
**The Payload:** The "Vice City Meat-Lovers." But heavily baked into the oversized, garlic-stuffed crust are three micro-cassettes containing the offshore analog routing numbers to Ricky's Cayman accounts.
**Grid Hazard:** Sector A is a High-Compliance Archive Zone. Española Way specifically is rigged with "Compliance Tripwires"—sensors strung across the narrow alleyways that trigger localized EMP flashes and alert Vanguard Interceptors if a vehicle exceeds exactly 40 MPH.

## 🏎️ THE RUN SYNOPSIS
**Act I: The Drop-In:** 
Axel hits the MacArthur Causeway at twilight, the dense 86-degree humidity sticking his shirt to the vinyl seat of the '78 LeMans. The pizza box sits shotgun, the greasy cardboard radiating heat over the hum of the V8. As he crosses the bridge into South Beach, the neon pastel wash bleeds through his salt-stained windshield. He downshifts to slip quietly into the narrow, claustrophobic corridors of Española Way, keeping the RPMs low to avoid pinging the noise-ordinance sensors.

**Act II: The Friction:** 
Halfway down the cobblestone alley, Axel’s dashboard shortwave crackles—a Vanguard Interceptor drone has been deployed to Ricky's balcony. Axel has 90 seconds before Ricky is audited. The problem? He needs to cover four blocks, but the alley is rigged with '40 MPH Compliance Tripwires'. Axel punches the gas, the rear tires spinning out on wet cobblestone. He visually times the neon flicker of the tripwires, slamming the brakes to drop to 38 MPH just as he crosses each sensor line, then aggressively redlining back up to 60 MPH in the gaps between them. The LeMans' transmission screams in agony from the violent start-stop whiplash. 

**Act III: The Hand-Off:** 
Axel drifts the LeMans 90-degrees, slamming the heavy steel bumper into a concrete planter to force the car to a dead halt directly beneath Ricky's balcony. He grabs the pizza box, climbs out the driver-side window to stand on the roof, and hurls the box up exactly as the Vanguard drone spotlights the alley. Ricky snatches the box from the third floor, tosses a ziplock bag of Re-Enfranchisement Credits down to the roof, and vanishes inside. Axel dumps the clutch in reverse, ripping out of the alley backwards just as the drone attempts to scan his license plate.
