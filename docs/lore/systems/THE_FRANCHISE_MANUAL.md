# NEON SLICE CORPORATE: DRIVER OPERATING AGREEMENT & LEGAL BINDING

## ADDENDUM A: THE 30-MINUTE GUARANTEE (LITERAL SOUL BINDING)
**CORPORATE DICTATE 1A:** The "30-Minute Guarantee" is not a marketing tool. It is an active Blood-Pact initiated the moment the Delivery Seal is placed on a proprietary Neon Slice Pizza Box. A chronometric spell (henceforth referred to as the "Guarantee Spell") is bound to the assigned Driver's aura.
**PENALTY STRUCTURE:** Upon the timer reaching 00:00 prior to delivery transference, the Guarantee Spell will extract compensation directly from the Driver's telomeres. The Driver will experience rapid aging (approximately 72 hours of cellular decay per 1 second late).
> **[AXEL'S NOTES]:** *They don't tell you the extraction hurts. It feels like pulling a cold fishing hook out of your chest. The mirror is a bastard. I look 45. I'm 26.*

## ADDENDUM B: THE PIZZA INTEGRITY CLAUSE
**CORPORATE DICTATE 2A:** The proprietary ingredients of a Neon Slice Pizza—specifically the synthetic "Neon Spice"—are highly volatile. The product must remain perfectly horizontal and must not be subjected to G-forces exceeding 6.2 Gs.
**PENALTY STRUCTURE:** If the structural integrity of the Pizza is compromised, the Neon Spice will instantly petrify the cheese into a concrete-dense polymer to protect trade secrets. Compromised product results in automatic delivery failure and immediate penalty extraction.
> **[AXEL'S NOTES]:** *Lost three days of my life last month because I hit a pothole on I-95 and the pepperoni shifted. Drive smooth. Drive like the box is a ticking bomb. Because it is.*

## ADDENDUM C: THE BODILY INJURY DEDUCTIBLE
**CORPORATE DICTATE 3A:** Corporate does not provide medical insurance. However, Corporate does claim ownership over all mechanical and biological components within the vehicle during active deliveries. In the event of a fatal biological termination (e.g., Driver is eviscerated by Swamp Cryptid), Corporate reserves the right to harvest any undamaged organs to offset the cost of the lost Pizza.
> **[AXEL'S NOTES]:** *This is why Riley won't call an ambulance for you. If you crash, Dispatch just waits for the pulse monitor to zero out, then they send the sweepers to gut you like a fish.*

## ADDENDUM D: CARTEL & HOA PROTOCOLS
**CORPORATE DICTATE 4A:** Neutrality is mandated across all active Grid Zones.
- If pulled over by Miami Cartel Runners: Drivers are to surrender all cash tips, but defend the Pizza with lethal force if necessary.
- If engaged by HOA Vanguard: Drivers are to attempt evasion. Do not return fire on HOA properties, as property damage will be deducted directly from your soul-ledger.
> **[AXEL'S NOTES]:** *If the Vanguard catches you, they don't shoot to kill. They shoot to blow your tires so they can cite you for "Illegal Parking" before they hang you in a cul-de-sac.*
