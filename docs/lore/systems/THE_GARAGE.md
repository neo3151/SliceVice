# THE GARAGE: 1978 PONTIAC LEMANS ("THE CUTTER")

Axel’s machine isn't a museum piece. It’s a 3,800lb blunt force instrument designed to breach corporate checkpoints and deliver high-value hardware through the wet heat of the Florida swamp. It is loud, heavy, and faster than any security auditor would expect.

## 1. THE POWERPLANT: 400 CID "IRON-BLOCK" V8
The original 301 is gone, replaced by a punched-out 400 CID Pontiac block salvaged from a scrap yard in Hialeah.
- **The Mod:** 750 CFM Holley double-pumper carburetor. It drinks fuel faster than Axel drinks coffee.
- **The Nitro:** Twin 10lb "Cutter-Special" Nitro bottles are bolted into the floorboards where the passenger seat used to be. Activated by a physical toggle on the Shifter.
- **The Note:** *The nitro isn't for racing. It's for clearing the Rickenbacker Gap when the drawbridge is stuck at 30 degrees.*

## 2. THE HARDWARE SAFE (V-SENTRY COMPATIBLE)
Where most cars have a glovebox, Axel has an armored tactical vault.
- **The Safe:** A lead-lined, steel-welded container bolted directly to the transmission tunnel. It’s designed to shield sensitive hardware from the Vanguard’s electromagnetic "Compliance Scanners."
- **The Interface:** A series of five toggle switches and a physical Nixie-tube countdown timer. If the timer hits zero without a verified drop-off, the safe's incendiary charges trigger, melting everything inside into slag.
- **[AXEL'S NOTES]:** *If you hear the Nixie tubes start to hum, you have exactly six seconds to exit the vehicle. Don't worry about the car; worry about your skin.*

## 3. THE CHASSIS: G-BODY REINFORCEMENT
To survive the physical impact of Vanguard pit-maneuvers and Causeway jumps, the LeMans has been structurally "fixed."
- **The Frames:** Heavy-duty box-frame reinforcements and a 6-point roll cage. 
- **The Suspension:** KYB adjustable gas shocks and heavy-duty leaf springs. It sits low in the front and high in the rear—perfect for high-speed evasion.
- **The Appearance:** Flat black primer over industrial rust. It doesn't reflect light, making it nearly invisible under the Miami streetlights when the headlights are off.

## 4. THE COCKPIT: ANALOG SURVIVAL
No computers. No digital dashboards. If it isn't mechanical, Axel doesn't trust it.
- **The HUD:** A series of oversized Stewart-Warner gauges. Oil pressure and water temp are the only things that matter.
- **The Mirror:** Cracked, showing the blur of the I-95 behind him. 
- **The Smell:** A permanent haze of leather, unburnt gasoline, and stale coffee.
