# CORPORATE PIZZAMANCY & VEHICULAR COMBAT

The magic system in Slice Vice is tied to the gig economy, corporate policies, and the vehicles themselves.

## THE 30-MINUTE GUARANTEE (The Primary Curse)
When a customer orders from Neon Slice Pizza, a blood-pact is sealed with the Delivery Driver.
- A glowing digital timer appears on the driver's wrist.
- As the timer counts down, the driver gains **Momentum Mana**. They drive faster, hit harder, and can briefly warp physics to make jumps.
- If the timer hits 00:00, the **Guarantee Breach** occurs. The cost of the pizza is violently extracted from the driver's soul, permanently lowering their max health (unless they can defeat the customer and reclaim it).

## NEO-TIPPING
Customers don't tip in cash; they tip in **Bargains**. A good tip might be a protective ward against speed traps, or a vial of pure synthetic adrenaline for the car's gas tank. 

## VEHICLE ENCHANTMENTS (The LeMans)
Drivers bind their souls to their cars. Our protagonist, Axel, drives a rusted 1988 Pontiac LeMans hatchback. 
- **Fuel Injected Surge:** Axel can sacrifice his own blood to the engine block to temporarily boost speed past the sound barrier.
- **Cassette-Deck Wards:** Playing bootleg mixtapes through the tape deck provides a protective aura against hostile cryptid magics.
- **The Rusted Hull:** The car's rust acts as a natural ward against Swamp Magic, but makes it highly vulnerable to HOA citation magic (which demands pristine aesthetics).

## THE NO-GO ZONES
Certain zones on the map are off the grid. Delivering to these zones requires the driver to shift out of the physical world and into the "Neon Slipstream", where the sky is always grid-lined and the sun is always setting.
