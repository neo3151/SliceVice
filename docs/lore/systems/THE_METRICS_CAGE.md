# THE METRICS CAGE: THE PSYCHOLOGICAL ECONOMY

Neon Slice drivers are not paid in USD. The entire corporate structure is an algorithm designed to extract the maximum amount of human suffering via "Grid Optimization."

## 1. THE TIME CREDIT ECONOMY
When a driver signs the contract (in Axel's case, to pay off his mother's Cartel hospital debt), their lifespan is digitized.
- A successful delivery under 30 minutes earns exactly **1.5 Hours of Lifespan** added to the driver's ledger.
- An unsuccessful delivery (30:00 or more) instantly docks **72 Hours of Lifespan**.
- Axel is not driving to pay rent. He is literally driving to stay alive until tomorrow morning.

## 2. THE GRIEVANCE POINT SYSTEM
The Dispatch bunker continuously runs an algorithm analyzing the driver's performance over the radio telemetry.
- **Grievance Points:** Accrued for minor infractions (damaging the Neon Spice pizza, swearing on the radio channel, stopping at a red light instead of running it to save time). 
- Accumulated Grievance Points increase the physical weight of gravity inside the LeMans. The worse your rating, the harder it physically is to move your arms to steer the car.

## 3. THE "GOLDEN PEPPERONI" INFLATION
Occasionally, Dispatch forces "Surge Pricing" on drivers. During massive supernatural storms or Cartel Turf Wars, the corporate entity increases the requirement—a delivery under 30 minutes now only earns 0.5 hours of lifespan. The psychological torture of the Metrics Cage is built to ensure a 99% driver attrition (death) rate within 6 months.
