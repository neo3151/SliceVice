# THE MIXTAPES: SYNTHETIC AUDIO-MAGIC

In the Florida Grid, music is not entertainment. It is kinetic magic bound to magnetic tape. The Cartel uses giant digital sound-systems, but delivery drivers like Axel rely entirely on illicit, heavily-worn cassette tapes. 

## 1. THE PHYSICS OF MAGNETIC TAPE
- Cassette tapes act as spell catalysts. The magnetic strip stores pure "Vibrational Mana". 
- When a tape is inserted into the LeMans' dashboard, the alternator pulls the kinetic energy from the music and feeds it directly into the drivetrain or the protective chassis wards.
- **Degradation:** A tape can only be played so many times. Every time a spell is cast via the tape deck, the magnetic strip wears down. A "Cursed" or fully degraded tape loses its magic.
> **[AXEL'S NOTES]:** *I keep a pencil on the dash to rewind the slack. If the alternator is running hot and the tape jams, it doesn't just ruin the cassette—it fractures the steering column.*

## 2. THE BRAND WAR
The physical quality of the cassette dictates the stability of the spell.
- **Maxell UR-90s (The Standard):** Cheap, unreliable. Dispatch issues these for standard runs. They provide minor speed boosts but are prone to overheating.
- **TDK SA-90 (The Black Market Cast):** High-bias chrome tapes. These are smuggled from Japan. A TDK wrapped in foil can hold potent defensive spells, granting the LeMans temporary invulnerability against low-caliber arms fire.
> **[AXEL'S NOTES]:** *Don't ever buy Memorex from a gas station. They're cursed. Played one near the glades and my rearview mirror turned into a portal to the void.*

## 3. THE "EATEN TAPE" BACKFIRE
If the LeMans' tape deck malfunctions and "eats" the tape, the magnetic spell unspools violently inside the cabin.
- **The Backlash:** The tangled tape acts like razor wire charged with kinetic energy. It will whip around the cabin, attempting to slash the driver or entangle the Pizza Safe.
- The only way to stop a Backfire is to physically rip the tape deck out of the dashboard or cut the unspooling ribbon with a silver knife.
