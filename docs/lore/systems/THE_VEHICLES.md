# THE VEHICLES: IRON & OXIDE

In the wet heat of Miami, your car isn't an "asset." It's a survival suit made of low-grade steel and fueled by high-octane desperation. There is no magic here—only torque, traction, and the sound of a redlining V8.

## 1. THE REBELS

### THE METROPOLITAN CUTTER: 1978 PONTIAC LEMANS
- **Operator:** Axel
- **Mechanicals:** 400 CID Iron-Block V8, Twin Nitro-Bottles, Reinforced G-Body.
- **Role:** A heavy, tactical battering ram. It’s built to take a pit-maneuver from a Vanguard Interceptor and keep rolling.
- **The Look:** Flat black primer, rusted wheel wells, and a persistent scent of burnt transmission fluid.

### THE MARITIME GHOST: 1979 BOSTON WHALER (17ft)
- **Operator:** Reya
- **Mechanicals:** Original hull reinforced with anti-reflective grey coating. Twin 70hp outboards.
- **Role:** Silent littoral transport. It carries the "Cutter's" hardware through the Stiltsville shallows where the Vanguard trucks can't follow.
- **The Mod:** Lead-lined storage under the center console for physical hardware modules.

## 2. THE CORPORATE FLEET

### THE EXECUTIVE ENFORCER: 1980 FORD F-350 (4x4)
- **Operator:** Director Vance
- **Mechanicals:** 400 Modified V8, Dana 60 axles, heavy-duty push bar. 
- **Role:** Personal command vehicle. Heavier than a house and twice as stubborn. 
- **The Look:** Factory "White Pearl" paint that stays suspiciously clean, even in the mud of the Drowned Districts.

### THE VANGUARD INTERCEPTOR: 1986 FORD BRONCO (BLACK-OPS)
- **Operator:** Vanguard Security SWAT
- **Mechanicals:** High-output Windsor V8, reinforced suspension, run-flat tires.
- **Role:** High-speed pursuit and parameter enforcement. 
- **Tactics:** These vehicles operate in "Wolf Packs" of three, using tactical radio coordination to box in couriers. If they hit you, they don't stop until your chassis is scrap.

### THE HARBINGER: PETERBILT 359 RECOVERY TOW
- **Operator:** Internal Audit (The Board)
- **Mechanicals:** Industrial Diesel, 15-ton winch, reinforced hydraulic "stinger."
- **Role:** The "Final Repo." They don't chase; they recover. If the Peterbilt shows up, the delivery has already failed.
- **The Look:** Massive, rusted, and terrifying. It moves like a glacier but hits like a mountain.

## 3. THE CARTEL SPECIALS

### THE HAMMER: 1984 FERRARI 512 BBi (MODIFIED)
- **Operator:** Cartel Runners
- **Mechanicals:** Flat-12 engine tuned to the edge of explosion. 
- **Role:** Pure velocity. They carry the "Soft Assets"—cash and drugs—that don't weigh 3,800lbs.
- **Weakness:** Fiberglass doesn't hold up to Axel's steel bumper. One solid hit and the Ferrari is a cloud of red dust.
