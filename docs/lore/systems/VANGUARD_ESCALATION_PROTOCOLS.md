# VANGUARD ESCALATION PROTOCOLS: POST-MAY 1980

**Classification:** RESTRICTED - VANGUARD COMMAND ONLY
**Context:** The systemic collapse resulting from the Mariel Surge and Liberty City Riots forced Director Vance to rewrite the Vanguard's "Total Compliance" rules of engagement.

Prior to Spring 1980, Vanguard security operated like hyper-aggressive bounty hunters focused on debt-reclamation. After the riots, they retooled into an urban occupation force.

## 1. THE "BLIND FIRE" DIRECTIVE
Under original Vanguard protocols (The Audit Model), Interceptors were required to ping a vehicle's corporate existence ID before executing a Pit Maneuver. This ensured they didn't accidentally total a high-value corporate asset. 

- **Escalation:** In response to the massive influx of undocumented "Marielito" vehicles and the destruction of the Grid in Sector L, Director Vance initiated the "Blind Fire" Directive.
- **The Rule:** If a vehicle cannot be pinged within 3.5 seconds, or if it crosses from a Dead Zone into a Corporate Stronghold without a broadcasted Visa, it is classified as a "Lethal Anomaly." Vanguard units are authorized to permanently disable (destroy) the vehicle without running standard debt checks.
- **The Danger to Axel:** Axel used to rely on his low-profile "Audit Nullification" to sneak by. Now, being invisible to the system makes him a primary, shoot-on-sight target.

## 2. THE RIOT-PACIFICATION INTERCEPTOR (THE "IRONCLAD")
The standard Vanguard Ford Bronco Interceptor was designed for high-speed highway pursuits (The "Triangle Box" tactic). However, Broncos were getting decimated by cinderblocks and Molotovs in the ruined streets of Overtown and Liberty City.

- **The Upgrades:** In June 1980, Vance introduced the "Ironclad" variant for urban containment.
- **Bulldozer Plowing:** Massive angled steel wedges welded to the front chassis, designed specifically to ram through burning barricades or stacked Cartel cars.
- **Shatter-Cage:** Heavy rebar grating over all windows and engine intakes. 
- **Tear Gas Deployments:** Rear-mounted pods capable of firing continuous streams of chemical pacification agents into the exhaust wake, blinding anyone pursuing them or dispersing crowds surrounding them.

## 3. V-SENTRY SKY DRONES
With the fiber-optic grid shredded in the Dead Zones, the Vanguard was functionally blind on the ground. To compensate, they deployed prototypes of the V-Sentry Drone program.

- **Design:** Tethered blimps and low-level heavy rotor-drones hovering near the borders of the Dead Zones. 
- **Function:** They blast high-intensity UV floodlights into the analog neighborhoods. Below the blinding light, they scan exclusively for high-yield electromagnetic signatures—listening for unshielded engine blocks and analog radios.
- **Countermeasure:** The drones are heavily armored on the bottom, but their tethers (or charging lifelines) trace back to fortified "Battery Outposts" on the ground. Scavengers often attempt to sever these tethers to briefly silence the sky.

## 4. THE RIVER CULL
Frustrated by his losses in the urban sectors, Vance has ordered the Vanguard to be utterly merciless on the water.

- **The Strategy:** The Vanguard employs heavy gunboats mounting the Rickenbacker causeway and the entrance to the Miami River. 
- **The Toll:** Any unregistered boat spotted during curfew hours—be it a Cartel Cigarette Boat or a refugee trawler—is subjected to extreme kinetic disruption (ramming). The Vanguard claims these are "Maritime Foreclosures."
