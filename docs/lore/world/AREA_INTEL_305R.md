# SECTOR INTEL: 305-R (RICKENBACKER-STADIUM)

**CONFIDENTIAL: VANGUARD LOGISTICS DIVISION**
*Vandalized by [THE CUTTERS] - "Vance is blind in the dark."*

## 1. SECTOR OVERVIEW
Sector 305-R is the primary arterial choke point for the "Grid" in South Miami. It encompasses the Rickenbacker Causeway, the Virginia Key industrial flats, and the abandoned Marine Stadium. To the Audit teams, it is a resource-rich logistical bottleneck. To the Runners, it is high-speed execution ground.

## 2. THE RICKENBACKER BLOCKADE
The Causeway is the entry point. Vanguard maintains a permanent Level-4 Blockade at the toll-plaza.
- **Hardware:** V-Sentry 900 Signal Jammers. These create a "Radio Silent" bubble that forces drivers to rely on manual steering and visual cues.
- **The Stinger:** Pressurized spike-strips hidden under the asphalt.
- **Cutter Note:** *The jammers have a 3-second reboot cycle if you hit them with a Mag-Pulse. That's your window.*

## 3. THE MARINE STADIUM ("THE CONCRETE MAW")
Originally built for boat racing, the Stadium is now a brutalist concrete carcass sinking into the bay. 
- **The Pit:** The water level varies. At high tide, the "Grand Prix" circuit is a series of treacherous concrete platforms barely 2 inches above the blackwater.
- **Activity:** Unauthorized high-speed vehicular combat. Neon Slice delivery drivers often use the Stadium as a neutralizing zone to lose Vanguard Interceptors.
- **Cutter Note:** *Watch the barnacles. They glow purple when a Scavenger is near.*

## 4. STILTSVILLE (THE SHALLOWS)
The scattering of wooden houses on stilts in the bay.
- **Intel:** Scavenger sanctuary. Long-range optics are rendered useless by the dense swamp gas and radioactive fog (Area-Refraction).
- **Tactical:** Vanguard Interceptors cannot enter the shallows due to their weight. Hover-drones are the only corporate presence here.

## 5. TACTICAL WEAKNESSES: SECTOR 305-R
- **Visibility:** Zero natural light after 19:00. The only illumination is corporate floodlighting and the LeMans' halogens.
- **Structural Integrity:** The Causeway bridges are 60% rusted. High-impact collisions in the center span may result in bridge collapse.
- **Cutter Note:** *The third pillar on the bridge has a loose panel. Hide your stash there.*
