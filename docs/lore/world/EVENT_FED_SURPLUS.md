# HISTORICAL ANCHOR: The $1.5 Billion Federal Surplus
**Date:** February 27, 1980

## THE REAL WORLD CONTEXT
By early 1980, the drug trade was so prolific that the Federal Reserve branch in Miami reported a staggering $1.5 billion cash surplus, while the rest of the nation's banks were physically running out of currency. Unmarked duffel bags of cash were literally breaking the banking infrastructure of South Florida.

## SLICE VICE LORE INTEGRATION
The paper currency overflow wasn't just a byproduct of the Cartel; it was a physical attack on the Vanguard's digital tracking operations. As banks ran out of vault space for illegal bills, Sol Abramowitz discovered that the sheer weight of the physical currency was warping the financial Grid. 

To avoid detection, the Vanguard established the "Repossession Zones," legally seizing entire blocks of properties in Condo Canyon just to store the overflow of illicit debt. Axel received an urgent delivery request from Doña Carmen: transport a 50-pound lockbox of hyper-inflated bank notes across the Causeway. It wasn't money anymore; it was physical shielding. The radio signals couldn't penetrate the density of the cash. For two days, Axel's LeMans acted as a rolling Faraday cage, insulated by the corrupted wealth of the city.
