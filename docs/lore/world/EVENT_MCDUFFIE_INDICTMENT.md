# HISTORICAL ANCHOR: The McDuffie Indictments
**Date:** January 29, 1980

## THE REAL WORLD CONTEXT
Five Dade County police officers were formally indicted for the manslaughter of Arthur McDuffie, who had been beaten to death after a traffic stop in December 1979. The indictment sent shockwaves through the city, fostering deep mistrust of law enforcement that would ultimately detonate in the catastrophic May riots.

## SLICE VICE LORE INTEGRATION
The indictment hit the airwaves right as Axel was navigating the Sector H perimeter. With conventional law enforcement circling the wagons and bracing for civilian blowback, the Vanguard seized the blind spot. 

Director Vance ordered the "Grid-Hounds" out in force, running dark operations while local police were paralyzed by internal investigations. In Little Haiti, Jean-Pierre tapped the analog mesh to redirect Coast Guard signals, realizing that the police frequencies had gone terrifyingly silent. The streets were emptying, save for the hum of Vanguard drones and the heavy rumble of Axel's LeMans. 

The lack of conventional sirens meant only one thing: the predators were off the leash, and anyone caught on the physical road without a localized beacon would simply vanish into the bureaucracy.
