# HISTORICAL ANCHOR: The Refugee Act of 1980
**Date:** March 17, 1980

## THE REAL WORLD CONTEXT
President Jimmy Carter signed the Refugee Act, fundamentally altering U.S. immigration laws and the definition of asylum status. This legislative shift directly laid the groundwork for the structural and bureaucratic framework that would strain to handle the sudden influx of the Mariel Boatlift just one month later.
