# THE 1980 GRID ATLAS: OPERATIONAL SECTORS
**Confidential Intel for [THE CUTTERS]**
*Historical Anchor: Early 1980s Miami-Dade*

This document maps the physical "Grid" of South Florida, translating the 1980s landscape into the flooded, noir-soaked operational zone of the Slice Vice universe.

---

## 🌊 SECTOR A: THE NEON COAST
**Real-World Location:** South Beach / Ocean Drive
**Grid Status:** *High-Compliance Archive Zone*

South Beach is a battlefield of light. While the rest of the city struggles with power-grids, Sector A is permanently lit with neon—a corporate requirement for Art Deco preservation.

- **The Fontainebleau:** A massive, tiered white monument to corporate-cartel overlap. It’s where the high-interest Existence Loans are negotiated over lobster and cocaine. 
- **Española Way:** A Mediterranean-style trap. The narrow streets are rigged with "Compliance Tripwires" that trigger localized blackouts if a driver exceeds 45 MPH.
- **Cutter Logic:** *The neon flicker hides the thermal drones. If the lights go pastel, you’re in the kill-zone.*

## 🏢 SECTOR B: THE CORPORATE SPRAWL
**Real-World Location:** Brickell / Downtown Miami
**Grid Status:** *Logistical Fortress*

The skyline is dominated by the **Vanguard High-Rises**. This is where the servers live.

- **The Atlantis (Relay Tower A):** The building with the distinctive square hole and the red spiral staircase. It serves as the primary data-relay for the V-Sovereign drone network.
- **The Metrorail Construction:** The city is being carved up. Massive concrete supports and half-finished tracks create a permanent "Construction Hazard" (Level 3). 
- **Hazard Mechanic:** *Construction Zones* act as sudden bottlenecks. Axel must navigate around scaffolding and "Audit Mortar" pits that slow delivery times by 1d8 minutes.

## 🌴 SECTOR C: THE MECCAS
**Real-World Location:** Coconut Grove
**Grid Status:** *Neutral Black-Market Zone*

The Grove is where the humidity meets the high-life. It’s deeply overgrown and incredibly dangerous.

- **The Mutiny Hotel (Sector M):** The mecca for cocaine kingpins and corrupt corporate suits. It is **Level-0 Neutrality**. No repossession orders can be filed within its walls.
- **The Grand Bay (The Pyramid):** A tiered luxury hotel housing the elite of the Miami Cartel. They use the pyramid’s balconies for long-range surveillance of the I-95 Slipstream.
- **Cutter Logic:** *If you’re being chased by an Interceptor, head for the Mutiny. Even Vance won’t pull a trigger on that curb.*

## 🍅 SECTOR D: THE REDLAND SHALLOWS
**Real-World Location:** Homestead / The Redlands
**Grid Status:** *Rural Scavenger Territory*

This is where the city meets the swamp. Large swaths of farmland are three inches underwater, turning the agricultural belt into a "Shallow Grave."

- **The Sunken Rows:** Vast fields of tropical fruit (mangos, avocados) rotting in the rising blackwater. 
- **The UDB Wall:** The Urban Development Boundary. To the east, corporate sprawl. To the west, the absolute lawlessness of the Everglades.
- **Scavenger Cache:** The "Fruit & Spice" Park is used as a localized "Healing Station." A Publix Sub hidden in a rotting avocado crate can purge all timer-fatigue.

## 🔥 SECTOR L: THE SCAR
**Real-World Location:** Liberty City / Overtown
**Grid Status:** *Zero-Compliance Fracture Zone*

A sector where the Grid is physically and logically broken. Following the 1980 McDuffie Riots, the Vanguard lost 100% of its data-signal in this area.

- **The Burned-Out Neon:** Streets where corporate light is K.S. (Kill-on-Sight). Residents have systematically destroyed all Vanguard surveillance flamingos.
- **The Dead-Zone Blockade:** A permanent military-grade cordon around the sector. Entering requires a "Cutter Bypass" or a specialized "Riot Shield" modification for the LeMans.
- **Cutter Logic:** *Vance doesn't go here. In Sector L, the only law is the 1980 memory. If you’re carrying corporate payload, you better have a white flag or a fast engine.*

## 🌴 SECTOR H: THE MESH
**Real-World Location:** Little Haiti / Lemon City
**Grid Status:** *Invisible Grid / Analog Sanctuary*

While the corporate skyscrapers towers over it, Sector H exists in "Analog Silence." 

- **The Lemon City Relays:** A decentralized mesh-network of short-wave radios hidden in church steeples. It tracks Vanguard movements without using the digital Grid.
- **The Refugee Corridor:** A network of side-streets that avoid all thermal detectors via "Voodoo-Jammers"—low-frequency acoustic resonators that vibrate the LeMans' hull to mimic background noise.
- **Cutter Logic:** *If you want to vanish from the Vance Audit, Sector H is your home. Just don't expect the corporate AC to work here.*

## 📜 HISTORICAL HAZARDS (Global Rules)

### 1. THE METRORAIL BOTTLENECK
The 1980s infrastructure boom has turned the main arteries of the city into a maze of concrete barricades.
- **Impact:** Routes through Sector B have a 30% chance of encountering a "Dead End Construction Zone," forcing a high-speed manual detour.

### 2. THE 3:00 PM MONSOON (THE FLASH-FLOOD)
At exactly 15:00, the sky turns the color of a bruised lung.
- **Impact:** Visibility drops to 5%. All vehicles suffer a -50% Traction Penalty (The Hydroplaning Mechanic). Chases during this window are 400% more lethal.

### 3. LOVEBUG SEASON (MAY/SEPTEMBER)
The air becomes a thick, acidic fog of mating insects.
- **Impact:** Every 60 seconds, your windshield obscures by 20%. You must activate wash-cycles manually. If your visibility hits 100%, you are driving blind.
