# THE BESTIARY: ENFORCERS & ANOMALIES

Surviving a run through 1980s Miami isn't about avoiding "magic"—it's about avoiding high-velocity lead and desperate men. This dossier outlines the primary threats Axel encounters on the asphalt.

## 1. THE HUMAN ELEMENT

### THE STREET BERSERKER (BATH-SALT VARIANTS)
Driven by high-purity narcotics and absolute desperation, these individuals are unpredictable and often violent.
- **Behavior:** They roam the strip malls of North Miami, often oblivious to traffic until they are on your hood.
- **Tactics:** They don't use weapons; they use mass. They will attempt to cling to the LeMans' door handles or windshield at speed.
- **[AXEL'S NOTES]:** *Keep the windows rolled up and the doors locked. If one of them gets a grip on the mirror, don't stop. Physics will do the work for you.*

### THE CARTEL ENFORCER (GULF SYNDICATE)
Professional, heavily armed, and territorial. 
- **Behavior:** They control the ports and the luxury high-rises. They don't like couriers encroaching on their "distribution channels."
- **Equipment:** Uzi submachine guns, customized sportscars (The Hammer), and encrypted radio networks.
- **Threat Level:** High. They shoot first and ask about the cargo later.

## 2. THE CORPORATE HOUNDS

### 4. THE MIAMI CARTEL

The aquatic cartel crime boss. He operates out of the luxurious bay-front mansions and hyper-yacht syndicates. Employs precision logistics and heavy-duty enforcers.

### THE VANGUARD SWAT (HOA DIVISION)
Tactical, disciplined, and backed by a corporate legal team.
- **Behavior:** They enforce the parameters of the gated communities. They are the primary obstacle for any delivery involving the "Gold Coast" districts.
- **Equipment:** Remington 700 sniper rifles, night-vision optics, and silenced 9mm sidearms.
- **Tactics:** Precision ambushes from manicured lawns. They target the driver, not the car.

### THE INTERNAL AUDITORS (THE REPO CREW)
The heavy lifters of Neon Slice corporate.
- **Behavior:** They are only deployed for high-value debt recovery. 
- **Equipment:** Heavy-duty Peterbilt recovery trucks and illegal hydraulic "stinger" arms designed to flip a moving vehicle.

## 3. ENVIRONMENTAL HAZARDS

### THE SWAMP INTRUDERS (IGUANA INFESTATION)
Since the drainage of the Everglades for the new suburbs, the wildlife has been pushed onto the highways.
- **The Threat:** Thousands of large iguanas sunning themselves on the warm asphalt of the Rickenbacker. 
- **Risk:** At 90 MPH, hitting a 15lb lizard is like hitting a brick. It can shatter a radiator or puncture a tire.

### THE FLORIDA HEAT (THE SILENT KILLER)
- **The Threat:** 100% Humidity and 100-degree temperatures.
- **Risk:** Vapor-lock in the fuel lines. If the LeMans gets too hot in traffic, the V8 will stall and you'll be a sitting duck for the Repo Crew.
- **[AXEL'S NOTES]:** *Keep the heater on. I know it's 100 degrees out, but pulling that heat away from the engine is the only reason we aren't a pile of melted iron on the I-95.*
