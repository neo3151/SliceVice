# THE FACTIONS: THE GRASSLANDS & HIGHWAYS
**SETTING:** THE 1980 MIAMI-DADE GRID

The Grid is a territory split across three distinct lines of force. Axel's '78 LeMans is caught in the friction between these three powers.

## 1. NEON SLICE PIZZA (The Franchise Debt)
A corporate-paramilitary front that uses the facade of food delivery to enforce "Service Liens" on the citizenry.
- **The Protocol:** They use the "30-Minute Guarantee" to legally bind drivers into a high-interest blood-contract. Every second late isn't a discount—it's a 1% interest spike on the driver's "Existence Loan."
- **The Goal:** They want the "Grid-Tape Safe" to finalize a city-wide "Foreclosure Franchise." Their aim is to turn every mile of asphalt into a toll-road owned by the Board.

## 2. THE MIAMI CARTEL (The Cocaine Kings)
The lords of the I-95 Slipstream and the Intercoastal and the masters of the 1980 "White Gold" trade.
- **The Runners:** In 1980, they aren't using digit-synth magic; they're using **Cigarette Boats** with quad-engines and **Wedge Cars** (Countachs and 512 BBs) stripped of their mufflers.
- **The Power:** They control the "Grey-Market Grid." They want the Tape because it contains the Vanguard's acoustic-surveillance frequencies. With the Tape, they can run their payloads through the Grid without triggering a single compliance drone.

- **Goal:** To use the "Grid-Tape" to "re-level" the city. This would involve literally deleting the Drowned Districts from the municipal ledger, allowing the sea to reclaim the "encumbered" land.

### SUB-FACTIONS (THE 1980 SHIFT)
- **The River Units (Rogue Vanguard):** Corrupt Compliance Officers who prioritize "Asset Hijacking" over "Consumer Audit." They operate primarily on the Miami River, drowning evidence of their thefts. 
- **The Haitian Mesh-Network (The Invisible Grid):** A decentralized collective of Scavenger-Engineers who have built a localized analog signal-web in Little Haiti to evade corporate tracking.
