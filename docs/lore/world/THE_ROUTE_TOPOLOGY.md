# THE ROUTE TOPOLOGY: THE MIAMI-DADE GRID (1988)

The Miami Grid is not a normal highway system. Driven by massive infusions of synthetic magic, cocaine money, and unchecked swamp rot, the topography shifts. A map is only good for 48 hours.

## SECTOR 1: THE ARTERIES (I-95 & Turnpikes)
The massive stretches of concrete that act as the main conveyor belts of the city. 
- **The I-95 Slipstream:** Driving directly on the yellow line generates "Slipstream Mana". Axel can siphon this ambient energy to keep the LeMans' blood-pact stable. However, the yellow line is fiercely contested by Miami Cartel runners riding Testarossas.
- **The Palmetto Gridlock Zones:** True anomalies. Certain areas on the Palmetto Expressway are cursed by the sheer misery of the thousands of commuters trapped there daily. Time physically slows down. A two-minute traffic jam in a Gridlock Zone can drain 20 minutes off the Deliver Timer.
- **The MacArthur Causeway Chokepoint:** The only way to the islands. Heavily fortified by toll booths.
> **[AXEL'S NOTES]:** *Never take the far-left lane on I-95 after 2 AM. I saw a semi-truck vanish into thin air there last Tuesday. The concrete eats things.*

## SECTOR 2: THE LUMINOUS ZONES (Corporate Sprawl)
Districts controlled completely by corporate wealth and Home Owners Associations. 
- **The Stepford Subdivisions:** Mathematically perfect neighborhoods. The lawns are manicured down to the millimeter. Delivering here requires extreme stealth. Any noise violating the municipal codes (engines louder than 60 decibels, squealing tires) summons the HOA Security Vanguard.
- **The Strip Malls:** Glowing, neon-drenched consumer traps. Heavily populated by Bath-Salt Berserkers who congregate near the humming fluorescent lights.
> **[AXEL'S NOTES]:** *HOA turf is worse than Cartel turf. Cartels shoot you because it's business. HOA shoots you because your paint color violates the neighborhood covenant.*

## SECTOR 3: THE UNCLAIMED FRINGES (The Deep Swamp)
Where the neon lights fail, the asphalt crumbles into dirt, and the city bleeds into the Everglades. 
- **"Phantom Exits":** On humid nights, new off-ramps logically appear on the highway that don't exist during the day. Taking one traps you on a dirt road leading eternally into the black swamp.
- **The Fog:** Swamp zones naturally produce a thick, radioactive green mist. Standard headlights reflect blindly off it. The LeMans must navigate purely by the flickering yellow light of its halogen high-beams.
- **The Stilt Districts:** Communities of outcasts and swamp-witches living above the black water. The roads here are flooded constantly, meaning Axel operates the LeMans in perpetual hydroplane conditions.
> **[AXEL'S NOTES]:** *If you hear banjos mixed with synth-wave, kill the headlights and coast in neutral. It's the only way they won't see you.*

## SECTION 4: THE 1980 HISTORICAL HAZARDS (TIMECAPSULE)

The Grid is currently under intense pressure from the 1980s infrastructure boom and the native Florida ecosystem. 

- **The Metrorail Bottleneck:** Massive concrete piling projects in Sector 2 (Downtown) create high-speed chokepoints. 
    - *Mechanic:* 30% chance of a "Dead End" on primary arteries, forcing a high-stakes detour through grimy side-alleys.
- **The 3:00 PM Monsoon:** A daily climatic reset. Flash-floods the Grid with black water.
    - *Mechanic:* Visibility drops to near-zero. All pursuit vehicles and the LeMans suffer a -50% Traction Penalty. It is the best time for an ambush, and the worst time to be merging.
- **Lovebug Season (May/September):** Acidic swarms of mating insects.
    - *Mechanic:* Windshields become obscured by 20% every minute of travel. Failure to activate the industrial wash-cycle leads to total blindness at 100% buildup.

> **[AXEL'S NOTES]:** *Native Florida doesn't care about your delivery schedule. The bugs will eat your paint and the rain will eat your traction. Plan accordingly.*
