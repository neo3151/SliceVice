# COMBAT ARENAS: THE SET PIECES

The grid is massive, but the violence happens in the bottlenecks. These are hyper-specific, highly dangerous environments within the South and Central Florida landscape where the 30-Minute Guarantee constantly goes to die.

## 1. THE SUNKEN TOLLBOOTH (The Keys)
**Coordinates:** Mile Marker 73, Overseas Highway  
**Visual Definition:** A reinforced concrete toll-plaza that is 40% submerged. The ocean swells violently crash over the rusted barricades. The lighting is dominated by a failing, strobing amber caution light casting long, dizzying shadows across the wet asphalt against the deep, abyssal blue of the night ocean.  
**The Layout:** Axel must park the LeMans on the slanted, structurally compromised bridge. A narrow, rusted metal walkway leads to the toll-operator's booth. One wrong step means falling 50 feet into black water.  
**The Immediate Threat:** The Gulf Kraken. Their neon-blue tentacles frequently slither over the concrete dividers here, searching for warm engines to crush.  
**Interactive Element:** **The Coin Drop.** The mechanized toll-basket no longer accepts quarters; it requires Axel to drip blood onto the metal grate to raise the barricade arm.  

## 2. THE NEON PUBLIX RUINS (I-4 Dead Zone)
**Coordinates:** Off-Ramp 66, Central Florida  
**Visual Definition:** A massive, decaying 1980s supermarket. The exterior neon sign flickers violently, illuminating the pitch-black parking lot in bursts of radioactive green. Inside, the aisles form a claustrophobic maze under nauseating, buzzing fluorescent tubes.  
**The Layout:** Extreme tight-quarters. The aisles are littered with rusted shopping carts. Axel cannot see over the shelving units, forcing tight, blind-corner encounters.  
**The Immediate Threat:** Bath-Salt Berserkers and Citrus Husks utilize the store as a hive. They ambush from above, leaping from the tops of the frozen food sections.  
**Interactive Element:** **The Public Address Microphone.** A wired handset dangling from the meat counter. Screaming into it amplifies audio-magic through the store's broken speakers, which can disorient swarms of enemies.

## 3. THE PELICAN BAY FORTRESS (Naples)
**Coordinates:** The Platinum Coast  
**Visual Definition:** A blindingly pristine, surgical nightmare. Pure white stucco walls, perfectly angled palm trees, and manicured green lawns entirely devoid of life. The lighting consists of high-powered, military-grade white floodlights that cast zero shadows. It is too clean.  
**The Layout:** A massive, reinforced security gate with tire-spikes blocks the entrance. There is zero cover on the wide, sweeping circular driveway except for absurdly placed pink flamingo statues.  
**The Immediate Threat:** The HOA Vanguard Sniper squads. They take perfectly aimed shots from the roofs of the identical mansions to blow out Axel's tires.  
**Interactive Element:** **The Biometric Callbox.** The keypad at the front gate is rigged to spray high-pressure industrial bleach at face-level if someone inputs a non-registered delivery code.

## 4. THE OVERPASS AMBUSH (Alligator Alley)
**Coordinates:** Mile 42, I-75  
**Visual Definition:** Absolute darkness. A massive concrete highway overpass looms overhead, acting as a roof over the blackwater swamp. The air is thick with a radioactive, glowing green fog. The only illumination comes from the LeMans' cutting yellow halogen headlights reflecting off the dense mist.  
**The Layout:** The asphalt is slick and crumbling directly into the water on both sides. A massive, fallen Cypress tree entirely blocks the two lanes, forcing Axel to exit the vehicle into the knee-deep fog.  
**The Immediate Threat:** The Alpha Swamp Iguana nest. The vibrations of the car engine wake them. They drop silently from the concrete pillars overhead.  
**Interactive Element:** **The Phantom Flare.** A highway flare burning bright pink on the asphalt. It is magical cartel bait; stepping near the smoke causes severe gravity distortion.

## 5. THE CIGARETTE BOAT DOCKS (Tampa)
**Coordinates:** Port of St. Pete  
**Visual Definition:** Industrial decay mixed with extreme Cartel wealth. Rotting wooden pylons, oily black water reflecting streaks of cyan and magenta neon from the adjacent smuggler warehouses. Rain constantly slicks the wooden planks.  
**The Layout:** A wide, multi-level docking area. The Cartel's matte-black cigarette boats are suspended in the air by massive iron cranes. The footing is incredibly slippery.  
**The Immediate Threat:** Gulf Smugglers armed with Synth-Wave heavy weaponry. They utilize the suspended boats as high-ground cover to rain fire down on the parking lot.  
**Interactive Element:** **The Crane Winch Release.** A massive, rusted iron brake lever. If Axel can reach it, pulling it will instantly drop a 4-ton cigarette boat from the sky directly onto the Cartel gunmen below.

## 6. THE STILT HOUSES (Everglades Fringe)
**Coordinates:** Unmapped Sub-Sector 11C  
**Visual Definition:** Rickety, rotting wooden platforms suspended dangerously high above the deepest blackwater of the glades. Shrouded in glowing purple swamp gas. The only dry land is the narrow, rotting boardwalks.  
**The Layout:** Unstable footing over lethal depths. There are no guardrails. The LeMans must be parked on a sinking gravel turnout miles away, forcing Axel to approach on foot over groaning planks.  
**The Immediate Threat:** Swamp-Witches and Skunk Apes. The hovels emit cursed audio-frequencies that drain Axel's momentum.  
**Interactive Element:** **The Gator Trapdoor.** Rusted iron grates in the wooden floors designed to drop unlucky couriers directly into the gnashing jaws below.

## 7. THE NEON SLICE MEGAPLEX (Downtown Miami)
**Coordinates:** Sector Zero  
**Visual Definition:** The suffocating subterranean parking garage beneath Neon Slice Corporate HQ. Endless grey concrete pillars, sterile white strip lighting, and a suffocating lack of natural air. Paranoia-inducing geometry.  
**The Layout:** A multi-level concrete spiral. High-speed Cartel pursuits through here are lethal due to zero-visibility corners and polished, non-grip concrete flooring.  
**The Immediate Threat:** The "Internal Audit" Drones. Heavily armored security rovers designed to terminate drivers whose 30-Minute Timer drops into the negative.  
**Interactive Element:** **The Fire Deluge System.** The sprinklers here don't spray water; they spray corporate-grade chemical foam that instantly solidifies to lock vehicles in place.

## 8. THE SUNKEN CAUSEWAY (Sanibel Island)
**Coordinates:** The Outboard Coast  
**Visual Definition:** A multi-million dollar island approach decimated by supernatural storms. Massive 1980s luxury yachts are overturned entirely upside down on the cracked highway. Deep storm grey skies, with jagged forks of lightning freezing the scene in blinding strobes.  
**The Layout:** A pure obstacle course. The overturned hulls of the yachts act as massive barricades across the two-lane road. Axel must weave the LeMans through the wreckage while avoiding the crumbling edges of the bridge.  
**The Immediate Threat:** The Hurricane Surfers. They glide through the gale-force winds above the bridge, dropping improvised explosives (magically charged bowling balls) onto the asphalt.  
**Interactive Element:** **The Yacht Harpoons.** Ruined spearguns attached to the wrecked boats. If Axel can manually trigger them, they can shoot massive steel cables across the roadway to trip pursuing Cartel runners.

## 9. THE THEME PARK GRAVEYARD (Orlando Outskirts)
**Coordinates:** The Dead Mouse Corridor  
**Visual Definition:** An abandoned 1980s amusement park corroding in the brutal humidity. Rusted roller coasters loom like skeletal dragons. Eerie pink and teal lighting bleeds out from the broken concession stands, cutting through thick fog. Defunct, rotting animatronics.  
**The Layout:** Disorienting layout of twisted carnival paths. The overgrown tracks of a massive wooden coaster provide lethal high ground.  
**The Immediate Threat:** The Citrus Husks wander here, drawn to the memory of the tourist crowds. Also, the animatronics occasionally boot up aggressively, swinging rusted metal limbs.  
**Interactive Element:** **The Control Booth.** A glass-shattered operating room. Flipping the primary breaker will surge power to the entire park, momentarily blinding everyone with thousands of strobing carnival lights.

## 10. THE RETRO-FUTURIST STRIP MALL (Boca Raton)
**Coordinates:** The Vaporwave Ruins  
**Visual Definition:** Total 1980s commercial excess. Glass block walls, violently bright teal and pink neon signage reflecting perfectly off a rain-slicked asphalt parking lot. Synthesizer musak plays continuously from hidden, blown-out speakers.  
**The Layout:** A massive, open parking lot with zero cover, surrounded by empty storefronts. Perfect for high-speed donuts and vehicular combat.  
**The Immediate Threat:** Aggressive, roller-skating courier gangs. They move significantly faster than walking speed and carry spiked baseball bats, making them incredibly lethal in the open parking lot.  
**Interactive Element:** **The Neon Transformer box.** Shooting or colliding with this massive electrical hum-box will cause the entire mall's neon structure to violently detonate, showering the lot in electrified glass shrapnel.
