# THE SOCIOPOLITICAL GRID: THE HUMAN COST OF THE AUDIT
**Confidential Intel for [THE CUTTERS]**

The Grid isn't just made of asphalt and memory-tape; it’s made of people. The Vanguard’s "Total Compliance" model ignores the shifting tides of culture and the systemic pressure-cooker of the 1980s. This document breaks down the major social friction-points Axel must navigate.

## 🚤 1. THE MARIEL FLUX (THE 125,000)
**Historical Context:** The 1980 Mariel Boatlift.
**Grid Paradox:** The arrival of 125,000 Cubans in a single summer was treated by the Vanguard as a "Mass Buffer Overrun." 

- **The Tag:** All Marielitos were assigned a temporary "Existence ID" that allowed the Vanguard to track their every movement across the Grid. These IDs were later used to justify "Selective Sector Resets" (Foreclosures) in Little Havana.
- **The Friction:** Between the established "Old Guard" Cubans (who hold Vanguard-approved contracts) and the "Marielitos" (who live in the cracks of the system). 
- **Cutter Note:** *The Marielitos know how to bypass Vanguard signal-jammers using nothing but scavenged radio parts. They’re our best source for analog override keys.*

## 📻 2. THE INVISIBLE GRID (LITTLE HAITI)
**Historical Context:** The growth of Lemon City/Little Haiti amidst systemic marginalization.
**Grid Paradox:** While Cubans were given "Political Credit," Haitians were treated as "Unencumbered Data"—refugees with no legal standing, often hunted by Compliance Drones.

- **The Mesh-Net:** In response to the Vanguard’s digital surveillance, the Haitian community built the **"Invisible Grid"**. It’s a series of short-wave analog relays hidden in palm trees and church steeples. 
- **The Function:** It transmits "Audit Alerts" and "Scavenger Routes" without using a single Vanguard-monitored frequency.
- **Cutter Note:** *If your signal goes 100% quiet in Sector H, you’re on the Mesh. You don’t exist to Vance for as long as you stay under the palm-line.*

## ✊ 3. THE McDUFFIE SCAR (SECTOR L)
**Historical Context:** The 1980 Liberty City Riots.
**Grid Paradox:** The physical destruction of the Grid in Liberty City created a "Perm-Fault." The asphalt is shattered, the neon is dead, and the corporate servers have 100% loss-of-signal.

- **The Blockade:** The Vanguard maintains a "Level-5 Lockdown" around the perimeter of Sector L. No deliveries are officially authorized here—which makes it the heart of the "Under-the-Wire" black market.
- **The Memory:** The acquittal of the white officers (The Vanguard Audit Team) is burned into every resident's mind. Corporate uniforms are K.S. (Kill-on-Sight) in this sector.
- **Cutter Note:** *Vance is terrified of Sector L. He can’t audit a ghost, and everyone in Liberty City has been haunting him since 1980.*
