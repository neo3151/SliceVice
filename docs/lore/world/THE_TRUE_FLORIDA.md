# THE TRUE FLORIDA: DEEP LORE MECHANICS

Slice Vice is not a generic Miami Vice clone. It is a terrifying, hyper-authentic deep dive into the Native Floridian experience in the late 1980s, filtered through the lens of a neon-drenched corporate pizza delivery war. 

If you didn't grow up here, you will die here.

## 1. THE LOVEBUG WARS (May & September)
Every native knows the true horror of Lovebug Season. Twice a year, the air is choked with mating insects. For a normal person, it ruins their car's paint job. For a Delivery Driver traveling at 90mph, a Lovebug Swarm is blinding, acidic flak. 
- **The Mechanic:** During Lovebug Season, windshield wipers become the most valuable upgrade. Drivers have been known to install aftermarket industrial blades just to carve out a 6-inch viewing window through the mashed guts.
- **The Acid:** The bugs' acidity eats through the LeMans' already rusting hull if not immediately washed off, acting as a ticking debuff armor-melt.

## 2. THE 3:00 PM MONSOON
It doesn't matter what the weatherman says. During the summer, a violent thunderstorm will detonate at exactly 3:00 PM. It will flash flood the streets, obscure all vision, and turn the asphalt into a steam-cooker. It lasts exactly 15 minutes.
- **The Mechanic:** The "15-Minute Blind." Rivals use this precise window to execute ambushes on the Interstates. If you are caught on an overpass when the sky turns completely black at 2:58 PM, you brace for impact. The flash floods slow down pursuit vehicles, turning high-speed chases into hydro-planing demolition derbies.

## 3. THE PALMETTO BUG TERROR
They aren't cockroaches. They are prehistoric aerial assault vehicles.
- **The Mechanic:** Palmetto Bugs are attracted to the neon magic emitted by the pizza boxes. They will find their way into the car through the AC vents. A driver must maintain intense psychological fortitude when one lands on the steering wheel while merging onto I-4. To panic is to crash.

## 4. THE PUBLIX SUBS (The Healing Items)
The only object in the entire state of Florida capable of fully healing a Delivery Driver's soul.
- **The Mechanic:** "Chicken Tender Subs" glow with an ethereal golden light. They are incredibly rare drops (since you have to brave the chaotic parking lots of the dominant grocery monopoly to acquire one) but eating one purges all curses (including the 30-Minute Guarantee timer fatigue).

## 5. THE TOLL BOOTH TOLL
Before SunPass existed, dropping exact change in the toll baskets at 80mph was a necessary skill.
- **The Mechanic:** "The Quarter Toss." Hitting the basket without slowing down is a magical ritual. Missing the basket results in a "Toll Curse," summoning the Florida Highway Patrol out of the ether.
