# VANGUARD INTEL: CORPORATE SECURITY PROTOCOLS

Vanguard Security isn't just a mall cop unit. They are a privatized, high-budget paramilitary force hired by Neon Slice to protect its geographic assets and recover "lost" hardware. They don't use magic; they use numbers, logistics, and heavy-duty Ford engines.

## 1. THE CHECKPOINTS: RICKENBACKER CAUSEWAY
The gateway to Key Biscayne and the Stiltsville shallows. 
- **The Toll:** Vanguard operates a persistent blockade at the entrance of the Rickenbacker. 
- **The Hardware:** They use **V-Sentry Signal Jammers** to disable unauthorized long-range comms and high-intensity spotlights to blind approaching vehicles.
- **The Protocol:** If a vehicle "flags" with an expired corporate license or a high EM signature (like Axel's LeMans), the toll-arms drop and the Interceptors are scrambled.

## 2. THE INTERCEPTOR PACK
Vanguard rarely operates alone. They use a "Triangle Box" tactic.
- **Unit A (Lead):** Pushes the target to maintain high speed, preventing sharp turns.
- **Unit B & C (Flanks):** Move in for the PIT-maneuver simultaneously.
- **Target:** They aim for the rear quarter-panel. In a high-speed G-Body car like the LeMans, this almost always results in a catastrophic spin-out.

## 3. GEAR & EQUIPMENT
- **The V-Sentry Signal Jammer:** A massive, roof-mounted array on the Interceptor Broncos. It creates a localized "Dead Zone" that knocks out civilian radios and navigation.
- **Tactical Spike Strips:** Compressed air-deployed strips that can be fired from the front of an Interceptor to shred tires without the Interceptor slowing down.
- **Body Armor:** Heavy-duty Kevlar with "V-Plate" ceramic inserts. Designed to stop the high-caliber rounds typically carried by rogue runners.

## 4. DIRECTOR VANCE: THE LOGISTICIAN
Vance doesn't care about the "soul" of the grid. He cares about the **Audited Weight**. 
- **Philosophy:** "If it can't be logged, it shouldn't exist." 
- **Weakness:** He relies entirely on his monitors and field-reports. If you stay off the paved roads and maintain "Radio Silence," he's flying blind.
