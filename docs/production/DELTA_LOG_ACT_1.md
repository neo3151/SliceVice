# DELTA LOG: Act I (Jan 1980)

This log documents the environmental decay and mechanical transitions that occur between the episodic tapes.

## Jan 2 - Jan 4, 1980 (Between EP1 and EP2)
- **Mechanical:** The alternator heat during the EP1 swamp run melted the plastic housing on the battery cables. Axel bypassed it with a heavy copper bridge. The '78 LeMans is now idling at 1200 RPM just to stay alive.
- **Environmental:** Soviet tension in Afghanistan is leading to rumors of fuel rationing in the Grid. Prices at the pump just hit $1.15/gal.
- **Faction:** The HOA Vanguard has established a "Compliance Outpost" at the entrance to the Coconut Grove district.

## Jan 6 - Jan 9, 1980 (Between EP2 and EP3)
- **Mechanical:** The magnetic field from the Tape Safe has permanently magnetized the rear axle of the LeMans. It now attracts loose nails and metal debris from the road. Axel spent three hours scraping metal filings off the differential.
- **Environmental:** Humidity is peaking. The "Year of the Gun" is living up to its name—police scanners are constant static. 
- **Faction:** Director Vance has been spotted visiting the Neon Slice Megaplex. Rumors of a "Franchise Buyout" are circulating.

## Jan 11 - Jan 14, 1980 (Between EP3 and EP4)
- **Mechanical:** The LeMans cooling system exploded after the "Audit" chase. Axel replaced the radiator with a unit from a wrecked 1974 Chevy Caprice. It doesn't fit right; the hood is now held down by a 10mm steel bolt drilled directly into the frame.
- **Environmental:** **THE FROST APPROACHES.** Weather reports suggest a record-breaking freeze is coming.
- **Faction:** The Vanguard has issued a "Stay-In-Home" order for Pelican Bay. All residents are barred from using their driveways after 10 PM.
