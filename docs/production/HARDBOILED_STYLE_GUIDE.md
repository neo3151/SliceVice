# THE "GRIME" VOICE: Hardboiled Style Guide

To maintain a consistent, raw, and historically anchored tone, all dialogue and captions must adhere to the following 5 rules.

## 1. Functional Vulgarity
Vulgarity should never be gratuitous. It is a symptom of exhaustion, pressure, and the absolute lack of mercy in the Grid. Use it to punctuate the desperation of a failed repair or the callousness of a corporate threat.
- **Good:** "The alternator didn't just fail; it shat the bed in the middle of a live lane."
- **Bad:** "I'm going to f***ing kill you because f***ing reasons."

## 2. Mechanical Cynicism
Axel views the world through a 10mm socket. People are "units," systems are "overloaded," and hope is "residual heat."
- Describe emotions in terms of mechanical failure: "My pulse was redlining," "The stress was shearing the bolts on my sanity."

## 3. The "Miami Humidity" Rule
Every caption should feel heavy, wet, and hot (unless it's during the Jan 15 Frost). Describe the smell of diesel, the stickiness of the steering wheel, and the salt-spray eating the chassis.

## 4. No "Magic" Technobabble
If it doesn't involve a vacuum tube, a magnetic tape, or a rusted gear, it doesn't belong. Avoid sci-fi terms like "nanites" or "shields."
- Instead of "Shields at 50%," use "The steel safe is vibrating so hard it's cracking the rear quarter-panel weld."

## 5. The "Foreclosed" Filter
Everything Axel does is viewed through the lens of his Mother's Debt. Every delivery is a transaction for time, not money.
- **Captions should reflect the countdown:** "Six minutes to midnight. Enough to buy her another week of read-only access. Or enough to buy me a headstone."
