# NARRATIVE BIBLE: Slice Vice - Season One ("The Overdrive Engine")

## 1. THE LOGLINE
In a brutally corrupt 1980 Miami where corporate syndicates measure and extract human souls as liability, a fiercely independent off-grid courier must transport a stolen city Master-Tape to earn enough Re-Enfranchisement Credits to buy back his mother's sequestered consciousness.

## 2. THE CHASM (Theme)
**Theme:** *The Preservation of Autonomy in an Ocean of Blood Debt.*
The season explores the conflict between Foreclosure Monopolies (The Vanguard Trust) and Individual Grit (Axel). It asks: *What is a life worth when the literal weight of grief is mathematically extracted as collateral?*

## 3. THE MACGUFFIN: THE "PIZZA SAFE"
- **The Exterior:** A standard, grimy steel delivery box bolted to Axel's LeMans. 
- **The Secret:** It does not contain pizza. It contains a **"Grid Master-Tape"**—a massive, high-density magnetic spool that holds the original terrain-logic for the 1988 Miami Land Reclamation Project. 
- **The Power:** Whoever plays this tape into the central Grid Terminal in The Palms can literally "reformat" the city—raising or lowering landmasses and redirecting the rising sea.

## 4. SEASON STRUCTURE (12-Episode Roadmap)

### ACT I: THE ACCIDENT (Episodes 1-3)
- **Inciting Incident:** A high-speed pursuit leads to the death of a "Gold-Tier" courier. Axel "inherits" the payload.
- **The Realization:** Axel discovers the payload is the "Grid Master-Tape"—the foundational geometry code of Miami itself.
- **The Turn:** Director Vance (The Vanguard Trust) marks Axel for "Immediate Repossession," threatening to permanently erase Axel's mother's archived consciousness if Axel doesn't surrender the Tape.

### ACT II: THE CHASE (Episodes 4-8)
- **The Drowned District:** Axel is forced off the Grid. He hides in the rotting houses of Stiltsville.
- **The Alliance:** Axel meets Reya, a former Vanguard engineer turned scavenger, who knows how to read the Master-Tape.
- **The Heat:** The Cartel joins the hunt, wanting the Tape to create a permanent "Smuggler's Harbor" in the city center.

### ACT III: THE OVERLOAD (Episodes 9-12)
- **The Siege:** A massive three-way battle at the Concrete Amphitheater.
- **The Breaking Point:** Axel must choose between surrendering the Master-Tape to clear his personal Blood Debt and extract his mother's archive, or broadcasting the Tape to the local mesh-network to protect the communities of Sector H from being overwritten.
- **The Finale:** The Master-Tape is broadcasted. The municipal banking infrastructure fractures, but not how anyone expected. Axel performs a physical smash-and-grab on the Freedom Tower servers, successfully extracting his mother's consciousness onto a portable audio cassette.

## 5. THE TONE BIBLE
- **Visuals:** High-contrast noir, heavy 80s ink work, CRT flicker, rain-slicked asphalt.
- **Sound (Implicit):** The grinding of metal on metal, the hiss of hydraulic leaks, the silence of the black water.
