# PLOT BEATS: Act II (Episodes 6-8)

## Episode 6: "The Salt Shield"
- **Setting:** A torrential tropical storm in the Drowned District.
- **The Beat:** Reya uses a "Static Scrambler" to mask the magnetic signature of the Safe. Axel has to manually adjust the car's timing to match the frequency of the storm's bit-static.
- **The Twist:** They realize the Tape is being "Polled" by a remote signal. Vance is attempting a remote "Format" of the city.

## Episode 7: "The Harbor of Ghosts"
- **Setting:** The ruins of the Port of Miami, now a graveyard of shipping containers.
- **The Beat:** Axel and Reya encounter a Cartel "Sea-Wall" blockade. They have to jump the LeMans across a gap in the shipping lane using the Tape's residual magnetic pull to "levitate" the car for a critical two seconds.
- **The character moment:** Reya reveals why she left the Cartel—she was the one who accidentally mapped the logic for the "Grand Sanitization."

## Episode 8: "The Compliance Shock"
- **Setting:** The entrance to the Concrete Amphitheater (Marine Stadium).
- **The Beat:** Vance deploys the "Foreclosure Drones." Axel's mother's voice is broadcast over the PA system of the drones, pleading for him to "Comply."
- **The Climax:** Axel realizes it’s a synthetic recreation, but the distraction allows the Vanguard to damage the LeMans' rear axle. They are trapped at the water's edge. Act II Ends.
