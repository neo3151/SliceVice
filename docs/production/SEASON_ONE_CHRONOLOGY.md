## Universe Persistence Note:
Between every episode, Axel is spending "Re-Enfranchisement Credits" on black-market parts just to keep the '78 LeMans from seizing up. The car is becoming a "Ship of Theseus" of junkyard scrap.

## Volume 03 Persistence Note:
The LeMans received a full transmission rebuild (Grand Prix donor) and a new throttle body (Pontiac, from Dockery Salvage) during this arc. It is now the strongest it has been since Episode 1. Three new civilian characters introduced: Doña Carmen (Little Havana), Jean-Pierre (Little Haiti), Sol Abramowitz (Hollywood). Five narrative seeds planted for the Mariel Boatlift and McDuffie Riots arcs.
