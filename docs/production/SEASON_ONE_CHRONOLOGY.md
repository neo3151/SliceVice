## Universe Persistence Note:
Between every episode, Axel is spending "Re-Enfranchisement Credits" on black-market parts just to keep the '78 LeMans from seizing up. The car is becoming a "Ship of Theseus" of junkyard scrap.

## Volume 03/04 Persistence Note:
The LeMans received a full transmission rebuild (Grand Prix donor) and a new throttle body (Pontiac, from Dockery Salvage) during Vol 3. It is now the strongest it has been since Episode 1. The Mariel Surge introduces massive physical strain on the city's infrastructure and Axel's logistics network.
