# LORE BIBLE: THE MARIEL SURGE (The Ghost Month)

## Temporal Gap: March 16 – April 15, 1980
Between Axel's emotional stasis at the Cape Florida Lighthouse and the torrential arrival of The First Wave on April 15th, an exact month of "ghost time" passes in Miami. To the casual observer, the city is quiet. But underneath the municipal surface, Vanguard Trust is actively reformatting the physical and economic geography of the shoreline.

## The Vanguard Pre-Positioning
Director Vance knows the boatlift is coming before the local municipal governments do. 
- **The Drowned District Acquisitions:** Leveraging the encrypted Coast Guard traffic, Vance initiates a catastrophic wave of accelerated foreclosures. Vanguard Repo-Trucks forcibly evict hundreds of off-grid residents from the shoreline properties of Sector H.
- **The Nets:** Vance isn't acquiring this land for development. He is building physical chokepoints. By owning the literal shoreline where the refugee boats will land, Vanguard can legally claim salvage rights on the vessels and force incoming refugees to sign "Default Liability Contracts" before their feet touch public soil, instantly extracting their souls as collateral.

## The Civilian Anchors (Volume 03 Cast)
While Axel is repairing the LeMans, the civilian anchors are silently carrying the weight of the incoming crisis:
1. **Doña Carmen:** The Ventanita at Versailles becomes the primary analog intelligence hub. She hides handwritten manifests of incoming boats inside pastry boxes, utilizing Axel as the principal courier to distribute bypass-transmitters to the shore.
2. **Jean-Pierre:** Working 20-hour days in Little Haiti, he aggressively expands the copper-wire Mesh-Network. He is attempting to build analog "Faraday sanctuaries" large enough to shelter dozens of incoming refugees from Vanguard's thermal ping sweeps.
3. **Sol Abramowitz:** Sitting in Condo Canyon, Sol represents the oblivious upper crust. His complaints about the city's aggressive scraping of junkyard metal (Metrorail expansion) are the earliest signs that Vanguard is hoarding steel to build armored Repo-Trucks.

## The Supernatural Mechanics During the Surge
- **Grievance Gravity:** As Vance buys up the shoreline, the physical gravity in the Drowned District noticeably increases. Residents report feeling a constant, suffocating pressure in their chests, a literal manifestation of the debt Vanguard is laying over the soil.
- **The Nixie Timer Red-Line:** Because Vanguard is flooding the city with Repo-Trucks, the ambient "Time Credits" required to simply breathe the air within Miami city limits skyrocket, forcing off-grid couriers to drive faster to outrun the vacuum.

## The Transition to Volume 04
Episode 19 ("The First Wave") represents the final moments of Volume 03's idle anticipation and the violent ignition of Volume 04. When the first cigarette boat hits the dock, the ghost month ends, and the siege of Miami begins.
