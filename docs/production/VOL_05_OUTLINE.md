# VOLUME 05: THE PAPER TRAIL - ARC OUTLINE

**Focus:** The systematic dismantling of Vanguard's "Biscayne Entertainment Band" grid-anchor operation.
**Tone:** Heist logistics, escalating surveillance, analog tension. 

---

## EPISODE 36: THE LUNCH SHIFT DEPLOYMENT
**Focus:** The Mutiny Hotel Extraction.
**Plot Beats:**
*   Axel infiltrates the Mutiny Hotel loading dock disguised as a linen supply runner at peak lunch hour.
*   Sol calls the dock manager from a payphone, quoting the sector codes off the routing card to talk Axel's entry in as a legitimate service visit.
*   Axel breaches the olive utility box, physically yanking the routing hardware.
*   **The Hook:** Instead of just a Vanguard relay, the hardware is spliced directly into the hotel's illicit ledger network. They aren't just anchoring the Grid; they're physically archiving the hotel's analog blackmail material onto tape reels.

## EPISODE 37: THE SCATTERED DECK
**Focus:** The Fontainebleau & The Forge hits. 
**Plot Beats:**
*   With Vanguard now aware of the Mutiny breach, normal infiltration won't work. The team has to hit the next two locations simultaneously.
*   Jean-Pierre floods the Fontainebleau's frequency band from his van to degrade the extractor signal, buying Elio time to physically slip in and pull the hardware.
*   Reya and Axel hit The Forge. Vanguard has already dispatched suits with legitimate service contracts and padlocks to secure the box. Axel and Reya must use patience, forged paperwork, and the right cover to talk their way past the contractors without triggering an alarm.
*   **The Hook:** They secure the hardware, but Vanguard intercepts Reya's exit path. She escapes, but they get a partial description of her. 

## EPISODE 38: THE GHOST CLUB
**Focus:** Infiltration of the final, unknown venue.
**Plot Beats:**
*   The group decodes the final destination on the routing card: an illegal, unlisted gambling club operating out of a derelict Stiltsville property on the water.
*   Axel uses his old network to secure a fast boat out to the bay. 
*   When they arrive, the club is empty. Vanguard has already packed up. 
*   **The Hook:** A single analog phone rings in the empty club. It's Director Vance. He tracked their interference at The Forge and intentionally left the final club empty as a trap, revealing he has a lock on Sol's location at Elio's garage.

## EPISODE 39: THE FARADAY CAGE (VOL 5 FINALE)
**Focus:** Vanguard's counterstrike and the defense of Elio's Garage.
**Plot Beats:**
*   Axel races back to the mainland in the boat, burning the motor to beat Vance's demolition crews to the garage. 
*   At the garage, JP and Sol realize Vanguard is using the routing card data to triangulate JP's transmitter frequencies. They have to desperately wrap the critical magnetic tape reels and frequency cards in copper mesh from Elio's stock to hide from the signal.
*   Axel arrives just as Vanguard's institutional presence surrounds the block—not armed guards, but city inspectors and demolition crews with signed permits. A bureaucratic standoff occurs. Sol uses her real estate community contacts to route Vanguard's Mutiny Hotel wiretaps directly to Radio Maria, who broadcasts it on the analog airwaves, forcing the crews to pull back for corporate damage control.
*   **Resolution:** The Biscayne Entertainment Band is secure. Escaping the garage, the team realizes Vanguard isn't just anchoring the Grid—they are mapping the physical geography of the underground physical economy for a total hostile takeover.
