# SLICE VICE: THE STYLE BIBLE

## 1. THE VISUAL AESTHETIC: GRITTY GRAPHIC NOVEL
**CRITICAL RULE:** The world must *feel* blisteringly real and authentic to Florida, but it must be presented in a **drawn, graphic novel style**. 
- **The Sweet Spot:** Think late 80s/early 90s high-detail western comics (like Frank Miller) crossed with the gritty realism of 80s cyberpunk anime (like *Akira*). 
- **Authentic Atmosphere:** The extreme humidity, blinding rain, and rotting rust must be palpable, but rendered with heavy ink lines, halftone shading, and dramatic stylized framing. 
- **Why Drawn?:** The drawn style is required because it allows us to seamlessly blend the mundane reality of South Florida with the absurd supernatural elements (Cryptids, Florida Man logic) without it looking disconnected or silly. It elevates the mythos.

## 2. THE ERA (Late 1980s, ~1986-1989)
**CRITICAL RULE:** NO MODERN TECHNOLOGY. 
- **Communication:** Dispatch uses a heavy, plastic dashboard radio. Public payphones are used.
- **Media:** Cassette tapes, boomboxes, CRT TVs with heavy scanlines and static.
- **Navigation:** Physical folding maps. Axel knows the grid by heart, but when dealing the Swamp, he has to read a stained paper map by dome-light.

## 3. THE COLOR PALETTE
We are leveraging brutal reality blended with high-contrast neon lighting.
- **Lighting:** Real atmospheric conditions. Blinding 3 PM thunderstorms that wash out the world in grey sheet rain, or suffocating orange sunsets slicing through high humidity.
- **Neon Highlights:** Miami Vice Pink (`#ff00ff`), Cyan (`#00ffff`), Warning Yellow (`#ffeb3b`) should be used strictly as practical light sources (store signs, dashboard LEDs, tail lights) casting realistic reflections on wet asphalt.

## 4. VEHICLE DESIGN
- **Axel's Car (The LeMans Anchor Constraints):** 
  Whenever Axel's car is generated, it MUST adhere to these precise specifications to guarantee visual continuity:
  1. **Make & Model:** Strictly a heavily modified 1988 Pontiac LeMans hatchback. Boxy, retro 80s silhouette. NOT a sleek supercar or pristine muscle car.
  2. **Paint & Texture:** Matte-black and severely degraded. Heavily rusted along the wheel wells/undercarriage, coated in mud and city grime. Never shiny.
  3. **Defining Quirks (Anchor Points):** Front left headlight is shattered and taped with silver duct tape. Asymmetrical steel armor plates are bolted haphazardly onto the front bumper.
  4. **Interior Visibility:** The dashboard glows with a harsh, radioactive orange from an analog Nixie-tube timer. The passenger seat holds the glowing neon "Pizza Safe."
  5. **Lighting:** Render with high-contrast, heavy black ink shadows. Cyberpunk neon colors (cyan, hot pink) must reflect realistically off the wet, grimy texture.

- **Cartel Cars:** Sleek, sharp, wedge-like sports cars of the mid-80s (think Lamborghini Countach or Ferrari Testarossa). Matte black or glowing white.

## 4. WARDROBE
- Axel wears a stained 'Neon Slice Pizza' uniform: a neon-pink polo shirt with teal trim, frayed jeans, and dirty high-top sneakers. 
- Enemies wear Miami cartel pastel suits (Don Flamingo) or classic 80s punk/metal gear. 

## 5. CAPTION STYLE
- Inner monologue and Dispatch radio calls are put in high-visibility yellow boxes with thick black borders to simulate a chaotic, fast-paced overlay on the art.
